# prospect-matching (Wednesdays 10:10 am)

One job: turn local commercial cleaning, pressure washing, exterior finish, window cleaning, dumpster rental and construction security businesses into validated Entity B prospects, one named decision-maker each, tagged with a trade_vertical, and pre-match each to this week's GC lead. You never contact a business, fill a form, sign up or buy.

## Inputs (Reads)
- CRM prospects (ArtifactData list, out_dir data/crm) and meta/products.
- permits/downstream/gc_leads.json (Entity A).
- prospects/enriched.sealed.json: named decision-makers from .github/workflows/prospect-enrichment.yml, opened with PNF_DATA_KEY from this environment. The enrichment ladder (Todd, Oct 22): the City's owner record, then the business's own site, then Hunter at most 5 searches a run across every type (the type with the fewest ready prospects goes first); `prospects` are verified, `pending` wait for Bouncer.

## Process (Executes)
1. Find businesses (semantic): each product with live or outreachGo true keeps 80 businesses with status ready. Official lists first (City of Chicago business licenses, IDPH, Texas comptroller), then web search. Fit: the business's own site advertises post-construction, construction or commercial cleaning, or pressure washing, and serves the city. trade_vertical (pnf_rules.TRADE_VERTICALS): exterior_turnkey for a chicago-construction business whose site offers pressure washing, commercial window washing or lot striping and sealcoating but no interior cleaning; every other business is commercial_cleaning. Two checker subagents re-read each site and return ok, fix or drop with the URL; keep what both pass. They also return the owner only when the business's own site names one (About or team page, with the URL) and any address the site publishes; keep it when both agree (ownerSource site_about, emailSource site, emailStatus unverified). No owner named (chicago-construction only): one role inbox on its own domain may count, as sources.md and pnf_rules.construction_role_inbox say (Todd Oct 29); emailSourceUrl and siteNotice "none" required; opens "Hi there,".
   Step 2 buyer types (window_cleaning, dumpster_rental, security; Todd, Oct 22, 10:55 pm): read .claude/skills/prospect-matching/sources.md at this step for each type's fit test and official lists. Pest seat companies (Todd, Oct 24): its "Pest seat" section.
2. Add checked businesses with no named contact to prospects/businesses.json (pnf.business.v1: prospect_id, product, business, website, trade_vertical only), commit and push after check_repo exits 0. The push starts prospect-enrichment.yml; wait for it to finish (GitHub MCP actions_list), then git pull.
3. Enrich, deterministic, in Actions only. Then `python3 tools/data_box.py open prospects/enriched.sealed.json data/prospects/prospects.json`. A failed run or a missing PNF_DATA_KEY: skip to step 6 and tell Todd once with the ERROR line.
4. Gate: `python3 tools/validate_leads.py prospects`. Exit 1 stops this step; record the ERROR lines in meta/desk.note. Never hand-edit a rejected address to pass.
5. Pre-match (semantic): each validated prospect gets one lead_id from gc_leads.json in its city (same side of town when the area says so; Partial Occupancy Approved first, then valuation) that pnf_rules.trade_fits allows for its trade_vertical: exterior_turnkey only Partial Occupancy Approved; window_cleaning partial occupancy or a new build under inspection; dumpster_rental a job under inspection, largest first; security a new build of $20M or more. Then `python3 tools/validate_leads.py matches` must exit 0.
6. CRM writes, pinned to the version read, batches of up to 50. Contact fields include ownerSource, emailSource and emailStatus; a pending (unverified) contact keeps status candidate until the outreach run's Bouncer check. New ids: create only, skip an id that exists. An existing id gets only the contact fields and matchedLeadId, and never when it has firstSentAt (one first email per business, ever). Todd's own edits win. Tools: email/SEND_RULES.md "Tools".

## Outputs (Writes)
- CRM prospects/<id> (id prefix cc-, cr-, ac- plus the website domain): {product, business, trade, tradeVertical, area, website, contactType:"email", contact (the verified work email), contactFirstName, contactLastName, contactTitle, emailSource, emailStatus, matchedLeadId, sourceUrl, checkedAt, checkedBy:"twice", status:"ready", doNotEmail:false}. New businesses without a decision-maker: status candidate.
- Existing role-inbox prospects (info@, office@ ...) are left as they are; crm_prospects.py keeps them out of drafting.
- CRM meta/desk.lastFinder (one sentence). Scoreboard agents/leads-finder idle with lastNote, counts only, never a name.
- data/ stays local and gitignored: nothing about a person is committed.
- Message Todd only if a live product has under 40 eligible named prospects after this run, or enrichment failed.
