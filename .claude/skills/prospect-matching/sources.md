## Commission sellers for the licenses list (Todd, Oct 23, decision 4; Oct 24: POS first, then insurance)
Scope: chicago-licenses only, Chicago and its near suburbs serving Chicago businesses. Count emails only (email/L2-COMMISSION.md), sales@ only.
- pos_merchant: an independent reseller, agent or ISO whose own site sells and sets up POS systems or merchant (card) processing to Chicago restaurants, shops or salons. Skip the processors and POS makers themselves (Fiserv, Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed corporate), banks, and national call-center sales offices with no Chicago staff. Source: web search, then the site.
- commercial_insurance: an independent agency (writes for several carriers) whose own site sells business insurance (general liability, BOP, property, workers' comp, restaurant or contractor coverage) in Chicago. Skip captive agents (State Farm, Allstate, Farmers, American Family, Country Financial, Nationwide exclusive), personal-lines-only shops and the national brokers. Confirm the agency license one business at a time on the Illinois Department of Insurance producer lookup when it loads; store nothing from it.
- Contact: the owner, principal, president or partner named on the agency's own site, with the work address the site publishes on its own domain (ownerSource site_about, emailSource site, emailStatus unverified until Bouncer). A producer or account manager is not a decision-maker. Skip any site that says its email addresses may not be collected, sold or used for unsolicited email (Legal desk Oct 24, fix 11).
