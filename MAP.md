## 1. The business in one paragraph

A faceless brand selling money tools to small operators. Three shelves: $2.99 Etsy spreadsheets (proving ground, on autopilot), premium kits on Gumroad ($79 13-Week E-commerce Cash Flow), and data products sold by subscription ($29 a month Chicago Construction Cleaning Leads, live Oct 17). The free calculator site proofnotfluff.com is the traffic engine and carries the checkouts. Todd holds every account, key and payout; agents build, check and publish inside the constitution. The first goal: one $1,000 month by Jan 31, 2027 (coo/VENTURE.md).

## 5. Gates, in order, for anything a buyer sees

1. Build to design/WORKBOOK_STANDARD.md (spreadsheets) or the page templates in tools/make_site.py.
2. Self-checks: tools/check_repo.py (26+ planted failures), the product's own --selftest.
3. Holdout check: an independent reviewer that never reads the build code checks a random sample against the primary source (City records, IRS, the vendor's own page) and every written promise (Kem's holdout method, Oct 17; tools/holdout_check.py for calculator pages).
4. Design director: an independent reviewer judges the look against the best sellers in the category (design/DESIGN_DIRECTOR.md). Below the bar goes back.
5. Legal desk: legal/README.md.
6. Todd's click for anything that publishes money or changes an account (Gumroad publish, checkout links on the site, GitHub secrets).
7. Launch checklist (design/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md; Todd, Oct 17: every product 100% complete before launch): nothing is published, linked, emailed or promoted until every box is ticked with proof.

## 6. Why it is shaped this way

- Faceless brand, Todd's accounts: the business must survive any one platform or agent failing, and nothing can spend or sign for him.
- Public repo: GitHub Pages hosts the site for free. Anything paid is committed only encrypted; anything secret never is.
- The cloud cannot reach most outside sites, so public-data pulls run in GitHub Actions and the raw text comes back as an encrypted review set the crew can test against offline.
- Independent checkers beat self-checks: the builder's own tests share the builder's misreadings. Every product gets a reviewer that has not seen how it was made.
- The ledger is capped at 48 KB, so rules stay there and history moves to the archives and the log.
