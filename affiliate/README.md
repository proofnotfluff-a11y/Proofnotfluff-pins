## Gates, in order

1. **Approved.** A program's registry status becomes "approved" only after Todd says so in chat.
2. **Traffic.** A link goes on a page only when the site analytics shows that page getting visits. The COO sets the bar; it starts at 30 visits in 30 days. Until the site has analytics (quest adopt-cloudflare-web-analytics), no page qualifies.
3. **Cleared.** The Legal desk clears the page and its link block against legal/README.md section 7 before the page leaves draft.

## Who does what

- **Capability scout:** each run, re-reads registry rows whose read date is older than 30 days at the program's official page, records any change with the date, and adds programs that fit our pages (status "candidate").
- **COO:** keeps the recovery plan current (adds an account the day it exists); sets the traffic bar; once the site has its domain and analytics and eRank is approved, raises the next applications as Todd quests (Gusto, FreshBooks, Shopify), one at a time.
- **Cloud builder:** builds the disclosed "next step" block on a page that passed gate 2, from approved registry rows only.
- **Legal desk:** checks every block, description and post against section 7.
- **Never, by anyone:** an affiliate link in a product file, an Etsy listing, an Etsy message, a pin, a Short, a Reel or a TikTok caption.
