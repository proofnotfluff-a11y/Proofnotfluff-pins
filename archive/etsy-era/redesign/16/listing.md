## Description
A debt payoff tracker spreadsheet built around the payment you actually made, for anyone paying off credit cards and loans who wants to see their debt-free month. Log each payment, and every balance, payoff date and interest figure updates from that log. Then see your debt-free month with Snowball and with Avalanche side by side, and exactly what to pay each debt next month.

WHAT IT DOES
- Debts tab: type each debt once from its latest statement (balance, statement date, APR, minimum). Up to 25 debts.
- Payment Log: one row per payment, date, debt, amount. Today's balance for every debt updates from it.
- Dashboard: owed today, paid so far, interest so far, debt-free date and interest still to pay for Snowball, Avalanche and minimums only, plus a "what to pay next month" table for the plan you pick.
- Snowball and Avalanche plans run up to 240 months, roll each paid-off debt's money into the next one, and warn you if your monthly amount is below your minimums.

BUILT AROUND WHAT BUYERS FLAG ON OTHER TRACKERS
- Formulas that keep working: every tab is protected without a password, inputs are yellow, and the file recalculates with zero error cells on the example, on a blank copy and on stress copies.
- A setup you can follow without a video: one tab for your debts, one log for payments, three steps on a Start Here tab.
- A place for the payment you actually made, not just a plan. A "Counted?" column shows which rows count and flags missing fields.
- New statement? Type the new balance and date and the tracker resets to it, so nothing is counted twice.

Worked example included: five made-up debts with $831 of minimums and $1,100 a month. Opened on October 7, 2026 it shows $38,154.87 owed and debt-free in February 2030 with either plan, with $4,965.45 of interest still to pay on Avalanche and $5,233.73 on Snowball. The plans count from the month you open the file.

WHAT'S INSIDE (one zip)
- Debt-Payoff-Tracker.xlsx (8 tabs, including Terms) for Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and Google Sheets
- Debt-Payoff-Printables-Letter.pdf and Debt-Payoff-Printables-A4.pdf: My Debts list, Payment Log, color-in Progress Chart
- Start Here guide, PDF in US Letter and A4

Tested compatibility: an Excel workbook (.xlsx) that also opens in Google Sheets (upload, then open). No macros, no add-ons. Interest is estimated as APR / 12 at each month end; your lender's statement is the official number. Sources: CFPB "How to reduce your debt" and CFPB "What is a credit card interest rate? What does APR mean?", checked October 7, 2026.

FAQ
- Do I need Excel? No. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
- Does it connect to my bank? No. You type your statement balances and log your payments, so nothing is shared.
- Snowball or Avalanche? Both are shown side by side; pick the one you will follow and the next-month table follows it.
