# End the fake sale, then prove the product

ProofNotFluff's biggest problem is a policy risk, not a growth problem. The 30% shop-wide sale breaks Etsy's January 2026 truthful-pricing rules on three counts. Every listing shows a struck-through $6.99 that no buyer has ever paid. The sale never ends, so no item ever sells at its regular price. And the description names a third "real" price that has also never been charged. End it now and replace it with honest regular prices that rise later. After that, the evidence says growth comes from the levers Etsy names in its own ranking disclosures. Those are relevance (title, 13 tags, attributes, category, description), conversion (clicks, favorites and purchases), complete listings and shop, and customer service (review rating, message response rate, case rate over three months). For a shop of working spreadsheet calculators, the conversion edge is showing the file works: real screenshots, a short screen recording, exact specs, and a "start here" guide that gets buyers through the download. That guide also drives the first legitimate reviews, and those first 5 to 10 reviews carry most of the lift reviews give. Outside traffic helps ranking only indirectly, through sales. Pinterest is the best-evidenced free channel but takes about 90 days. A lot of popular advice is unsupported: daily renewing, listing every day, "Etsy throttles new shops," video as a ranking factor, the "first 160 characters" rule and $1 loss leaders. Grades used throughout: **Strong** means an Etsy policy or help page, or measured data with a stated method. **Moderate** means several credible practitioners agree, or one real seller dataset. **Weak** means advice repeated without data. **Folklore** means unsupported or contradicted by Etsy. **Forbidden** means against Etsy's or a platform's rules.

## The 30% founding sale fails Etsy's January 2026 pricing test

Etsy's Seller Handbook article on truthful advertising (January 12, 2026) is direct: "You must actually sell the item at its regular price for a reasonable period and in reasonable quantities." For a strikethrough price, "the higher price must be the genuine, usual price," "the item must have been offered at that price for a substantial period," and "the item must have sold in reasonable quantities at that price." It adds that "any discount that refers to a previous price must represent real savings." Etsy sets no minimum number of days and refers sellers to local law ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1450964704111)). Grade: **Strong** (rule).

Here is how the current setup looks to a buyer.

| What the buyer sees | Where it comes from | Has any buyer ever paid it? |
|---|---|---|
| $6.99, struck through | The listing's "regular" price | No. New listings join the sale on day one |
| $4.89 | 30% shop-wide sale | Yes. It is the only price ever charged |
| "then $[real price]" in the description | The price the shop calls real | No |

That fails on three separate counts. First, **the $6.99 reference price has never been the "genuine, usual price."** No listing has ever been offered or sold at it, so the 30% "saving" is not "real savings." Second, **a sale that every new listing joins automatically never ends.** No item ever sells at its regular price "for a reasonable period and in reasonable quantities," which is the first rule on Etsy's page. Third, **the description contradicts the strikethrough.** If the real price is the "then $X" figure, the shop's own text says $6.99 is not the usual price. So the struck-through number misleads twice over. If any listing's real price is below $6.99 (the catalog runs from $3), the strikethrough overstates even the shop's own idea of value. The "then $X" line is a future-price promise. Etsy's page, as read, addresses previous prices, not future ones. But the line implies savings against a price never charged, and it turns false if the price rise doesn't happen. UK regulator research found that shoppers take reference prices at face value and assume the "was" price was recently offered, for a meaningful time, with real sales ([UK ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/asa-research-into-consumer-understanding-of-reference-prices-in-ads.html)). That is the expectation this setup breaks.

The sale also buys nothing in search. Etsy's ranking disclosure names price only as an input to *recommendations*, not to organic search order. No Etsy page says a sale badge raises rank ([Etsy Ranking Disclosures](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/search-advertisement-recommendation/899478564529)). The notes found no record of how often Etsy enforces this rule, so the size of the risk is unknown. But the downside lands on the whole shop and the upside is zero.

**The compliant replacement is simple.** End the shop-wide sale. Set each listing's regular price to the price you actually want to charge now, and show no reference price anywhere. Drop the "then $X" sentence. Raise prices later as products and reviews improve. That is the pattern in the one real seller dataset with numbers. The shop held average prices at EUR 2.60 and EUR 1.81 in its first two years to build reviews, then raised them to EUR 3.94 and EUR 6.92, while revenue went from EUR 817 to EUR 7,717. The price moves were not isolated tests ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/3-years-of-etsy-shop-data-2022-2025)). A strikethrough sale becomes legitimate only after a listing has actually sold at its regular price for a substantial period. Etsy's built-in automated offers are compliant tools once the regular price is genuine. The Thank-you offer (expires a year after sending) reaches past buyers. The favorited-item and abandoned-cart offers reach warm shoppers, at most once every 2 days per buyer, and expire after 60 days ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108)).

**On price level, the evidence leans away from $4.89 for the core products.** A scrape of bestselling listings puts spreadsheet templates at about $9 to $10 on average (method undescribed; **Moderate**) ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy)). A July 2026 study of 91 spreadsheet listings found that templates with several linked sheets and formulas sell for 4 to 8 times as much as single-sheet files (**Moderate**) ([TableTemplates](https://tabletemplates.com/research/)). Modeled Gumroad data shows products under $10 at 35% of listings but 0.8% of revenue. Treat that as directional, since it is modeled, not taken from dashboards ([InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/state-of-gumroad-2026)). Fixed fees bite harder at low prices: the $0.20 listing fee alone is 4% of a $4.89 sale, before the 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing ([Etsy Fees](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/)). A sensible split is honest low prices for small single-purpose calculators while reviews build, and $9 to $15 for multi-tab calculators and bundles. That is a judgment call drawn from Moderate evidence, not a tested optimum.

## Etsy ranks on relevance, conversion and completeness, and says so in writing

Etsy's legal ranking disclosure (updated October 16, 2025) lists eight organic factors. They are relevancy, listing engagement rate, recency, listing and shop quality, customer service quality, shipping, translations and language, and context-specific (personalized) ranking. It also states that Etsy "does not offer sellers a higher ranking placement in organic search results in exchange for any compensation or fee" ([Etsy Ranking Disclosures](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/search-advertisement-recommendation/899478564529)). For an instant-download shop, shipping is neutral, which leaves five levers the shop controls.

**Relevance now reads the whole listing.** Etsy's April 27, 2026 title guidance says search takes "a more holistic view of your listing," including "title, tags, attributes, descriptions, first photo, and even reviews." It tells sellers to "consider using less than 15 words," "clearly state the item for sale," put the most important traits up front, avoid repeated words, and move subjective and gifting words out of the title ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)). Etsy also says exact matches between listing keywords and the shopper's query "may be ranked higher." It adds that "using all 13 tags, adding variety to your tags, adding all the relevant attribute options, and categorizing your items as specifically as you can" all raise the odds of matching ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). Tags should be "multi-word phrases" in natural language, attributes "act like tags," and keywords belong "in the first few sentences" of the description ([Anatomy of a Well-Crafted Etsy Listing](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014)). Because Etsy pools all of these fields, eRank calls repeating title words in tags unnecessary ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-seo-basics/)). That makes the best use of tag slots buyer phrases the title doesn't already cover. Two parts of the shop's internal title rule are house style, not Etsy policy: "top 3 descriptors" and "no sale or price words." Etsy's actual text is softer, and occasion words are allowed when they are essential to the item.

**Conversion is a stated ranking input.** Etsy computes engagement from "clicks, views, favorites, and purchases" to judge "how well the listing will convert" ([Etsy Ranking Disclosures](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/search-advertisement-recommendation/899478564529)). A click, favorite or purchase after a search view "contributes to the listing's quality score" ([How to Get Found in Search](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-to-get-found-in-search/22717244953)). Everything in the next section feeds this factor.

**Completeness has explicit, free checks.** The disclosure counts "completeness of the shop (including whether they have a shop photo)" and whether a listing has "sufficient high quality images." The Search Visibility page's priority checklist goes further. It wants a primary photo that is high resolution, "a singular image of a finished product," and "clear and well-lit." It wants the category "as specific as possible" and a return policy "even if it's 'No returns'." Flagged listings appear under the "Search factors" filter, and insights "typically disappear within 24 hours" once fixed ([Etsy Help: Search Visibility](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/25869947521175-How-to-Use-the-Etsy-Search-Visibility-Page)). The first photo must be at least 635 pixels on each side "to avoid showing up lower in searches." Etsy recommends 2,000 pixels or more and allows up to 20 photos ([Anatomy of a Well-Crafted Etsy Listing](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1347574487014)). An older Etsy statement, quoted by eRank, also credits complete shop policies and an About section ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-seo-these-factors-help-your-etsy-search-ranking/)). The current disclosure page doesn't restate that, so the About-section link is **Moderate**.

**Customer service counts over a rolling three months.** "We include factors like a shop's average review rating, message response rate, and case rate from the past three months in our search ranking" ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). A new shop has few reviews, so response rate and case rate are the parts it can control today.

**Recency is real but brief.** New listings get "a small, temporary boost" lasting "a few hours to a few days," and renewed listings get "a similar, though smaller, boost" ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). Any listing older than a few days has already spent its boost. The only way to use it is to launch each listing fully finished, so the test window measures the real listing.

| Ranking lever | Grade | What it means for ProofNotFluff |
|---|---|---|
| Title under 15 words, item first, no repeats or subjective words | Strong (guidance); effect size unmeasured | Rewrite all 20 titles; no published test of short vs long titles exists |
| All 13 tags as multi-word buyer phrases not already in the title | Strong (13 tags, phrases); Moderate (avoid repeats) | Tags are 20 characters each, per ListingView ([ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-s-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026)) |
| Every relevant attribute; most specific category | Strong | Unknown whether "Google Sheets" or "Excel" attributes match as hidden keywords or only filter |
| Keywords in the first sentences of the description | Strong | Description is read by search; not ignored |
| Clear every Search Visibility flag | Strong | Return policy set, single clean primary photo, specific category |
| Shop photo, policies, About section | Strong (shop photo); Moderate (About) | Free, one-time |
| Fast message replies, low case rate | Strong | Reply within 24 hours; prevent "not as described" cases |
| Etsy Ads, Etsy Plus, Star Seller for organic rank | Strong that they do **not** help | Etsy says none of them move organic placement |

## Conversion and first reviews hinge on proving the file works

No public A/B test shows what thumbnail style wins for digital templates. The only firm photo rules are format specs. Use a 4:3 landscape first photo, keep every photo the same shape, and center the product with even margins so the square crop trims background, not product. Etsy began testing vertical photos in summer 2025 but doesn't yet recommend them ([eRank photo guidelines](https://help.erank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/photo-guidelines-for-etsy-listings.pdf)). An Etsy and Drexel study of 50,000 listing images found that image-quality features improved popularity prediction beyond text. That supports "images matter," but it is about a decade old and isn't a click test ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.03663)). Vendor figures such as "70% of click decisions" or "2 to 3x conversion with 5+ images" have no source ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-listing-photos-digital-downloads)). The defensible first photo for a spreadsheet is a real screenshot of the working calculator inside a device frame, readable at phone-grid size. It should be one clean image, not a collage, to satisfy the "singular image of a finished product" check. Real screenshots also serve as proof of accuracy if a "not as described" dispute comes up. This is inference (**Weak** on style, **Strong** on format). The only way to get real evidence for this niche is to swap photo 1 on a few listings and compare visits with similar unchanged listings over 21 days.

**Video likely helps conversion modestly, but there's no evidence it helps ranking.** In 2020 an Etsy product manager said buyers are "more likely to purchase an item with a listing that has video," based on more than 5 million buyers ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-video-tips-166074/)). In January 2023, Etsy reported "an increase in conversion rate" after launching hover-to-autoplay ([Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Announcements/Updates-to-Etsy-Search-What-s-New/td-p/140979826)). Neither gave a size of lift, and no 2025 to 2026 Etsy page names video as a ranking input. A 10 to 15 second silent screen recording of inputs changing and results updating is the cheapest proof that a calculator works. Etsy's own old advice was to add videos to high-traffic listings first. Grade: **Moderate** for conversion, **Folklore** for ranking.

**The description's job is accuracy, because accuracy prevents cases.** No data links description structure to conversion. What is documented is that the main causes of refunds and cases on digital items are spec mismatches and download confusion ([ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-digital-not-as-described-refunds)). Case rate is an Etsy ranking input. Buyers can't download digital purchases inside the Etsy app and must use a browser. That should be stated in the listing and in the note to buyers ([ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/why-etsy-digital-downloads-not-selling)). Etsy allows five digital files of up to 20MB each and **no variations on digital listings** ([Etsy Help: Digital Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings); [Etsy Help: Variations](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015664047)). So Excel versus Google Sheets versions must be bundled into one file set or split into separate listings. The structure most consistent with this evidence has five parts: a one-line "what it does and who it is for," a plain list of what's included, tested compatibility, a "how you get it" section, and a short FAQ (**Moderate**). Etsy's Creativity Standards require any AI use to be disclosed "within their listing description" and ban "AI prompt bundles" outright ([Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)). Any catalog item that could read as a prompt pack should be checked against that ban.

**Reviews matter early and fast, and the rules on asking are strict.** Measured data from Northwestern's Spiegel Research Center found purchase likelihood at 5 reviews was 270% higher than at zero, with nearly all the gain inside the first 10. Purchase likelihood peaked at 4.0 to 4.7 stars, not a perfect 5.0. The data is not from Etsy (**Strong**, non-Etsy) ([Spiegel Research Center](https://www.xait.com/hubfs/Spiegel_Online-Review_eBook_Jun2017_FINAL.pdf)). For digital items, the 100-day review window "begins when the buyer downloads the file(s)" ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000572708-How-the-Review-System-Works-for-Sellers)). A buyer who never downloads is never in the review pool, so the download step is also a review step. Etsy's House Rules (dated October 5, 2026) forbid reviews that "include shilling or otherwise falsely inflate a shop's review score." They also forbid using Messages for "unsolicited advertising or promotions" ([Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/legal/buyers/)). eRank, citing Etsy's policy, says offering "discounts, coupons, refunds, gifts, or extras in exchange for a positive review" is shilling ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/how-to-get-more-reviews-on-etsy/)). The allowed playbook has two parts. Put a one-page "Start here" PDF in one of the five file slots, ending with a line like "an honest review helps a small shop." Then send one polite Etsy message a few days after download, offering help and inviting honest feedback, with no incentive and no request for 5 stars ([ListingView](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-to-get-more-reviews-on-etsy-without-breaking-the-rules)). Grade: **Moderate**. Fake or paid reviews, review swaps, friends-and-family purchases, any discount or freebie tied to a review, and refunds conditional on removing a review are all **Forbidden**.

**Functional depth is the moat against AI filler.** Among 264 negative US Etsy app reviews from June to August 2026, the share complaining about AI-generated listings rose from 19.3% to 27.9% to 32.0% (**Moderate**, method stated) ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-etsy-still-handmade-ai-listings-dropshipping-reviews-2026)). A shop that shows a real, tested calculator working is answering the exact complaint buyers are making. Multi-sheet linked templates also command the higher price band ([TableTemplates](https://tabletemplates.com/research/)). Bundles are compatible with the no-variations rule, and the one 2025 digital-shop case found grew by adding bundles. That case is self-reported, and its numbers don't add up internally (**Weak**) ([Lilys.ai summary](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/sell-digital-products-20251203/scaling-etsy-digital-downloads)).

## Outside traffic helps search only when it buys

Etsy hasn't said that outside traffic or Offsite Ads sales raise organic rank. Etsy does say conversion drives ranking ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). So outside traffic helps search only indirectly, by producing sales. Low-intent visitors who don't buy lower the conversion rate Etsy reads. Every outside post should therefore link to the listing that matches its promise, not the shop home page. A 2026 vendor claim that Etsy "heavily weighs off-site traffic" cites nothing ([GetBetterListing](https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/etsy-external-traffic-seo-guide)).

**Pinterest is the best-evidenced free channel, and it is slow.** Tailwind's study of more than 1 million pins found that a new image pointing to an existing URL keeps 64% of a first pin's distribution. Repeating the same image and URL drops that to 11%, and distribution builds over about 90 days (**Strong** on distribution) ([Tailwind](https://tailwindapp.com/pinterest-marketing/research/2025-benchmark-study-part-three)). One vendor dataset of 148,000 pins puts outbound clicks per 1,000 impressions at 4.8 for static pins, 4.1 for carousels and 1.6 for video (**Moderate**, vendor) ([Gensumo](https://gensumo.com/research/pinterest-marketing-benchmarks-report/)). Pinterest merged Idea Pins into standard Pins in 2023, so the "Idea pin vs standard pin" debate is obsolete ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/11/pinterest-is-combining-pins-and-idea-pins-into-a-single-format/)). One rule matters for an automated operation. Pinterest's developer guidelines say "the end user must choose each Pin to be published" ([Pinterest developer guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/developer-guidelines)). Fully unattended API pinning is **Forbidden** as written. A batch where a person approves each pin is the compliant model. Since Pinterest added Gen AI labels and a setting to see fewer of them, real screenshots are also the lower-risk image choice ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404034/pinterest-combats-ai-slop-with-new-content-label.html)).

**YouTube needs long-form to carry a link.** Links in Shorts descriptions and comments haven't been clickable since August 2023. The working path is a Short, then its related-video link, then a long-form walkthrough with the Etsy link in its description ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157)). Search-intent walkthroughs ("how to track X in Google Sheets") are the one YouTube format with a direct clickable path (**Moderate**, mechanism sound, outcome unmeasured).

**Share & Save is the cheapest win, with one interaction to watch.** Etsy credits 4% of qualifying sales from the seller's own shared links, including links in emails, websites and blogs, within a 30-day window. If the buyer later clicks an Offsite Ad within that window, the sale becomes an Offsite Ads sale. The terms were last updated February 20, 2024, and the notes found no 2026 change. Etsy can end the program at any time ([Etsy Share & Save Terms](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/etsys-share-save-terms-program-terms/1162874007996)). Offsite Ads charge 15% of an attributed order for shops under $10,000 a year, capped at $100. Opting out is allowed at that size, and sellers can't choose which listings run ([Etsy Help: Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000338367)). Whether to stay opted in is a separate decision from "no paid Etsy Ads," because the fee applies only to attributed sales. Google Merchant Center generally rejects digital products, so Offsite Ads probably reach downloads mainly through display, Pinterest and Meta placements. That is an expert forum answer, not Google policy ([Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/digital-products-on-google-shopping/415596)).

| Channel | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pinterest static pins, fresh image each time, person approves each pin | Strong (distribution); Moderate (clicks) | Judge at 90 days, not weekly |
| YouTube long-form walkthroughs, Shorts feeding them | Moderate | Link in description line one |
| Share & Save links on every owned post and email | Moderate | 4% fee credit; 2026 status unconfirmed |
| Email list and free lead magnet | Moderate mechanism, Weak evidence | No 2025 to 2026 conversion data for low-priced downloads |
| TikTok with a Business account bio link | Weak | Bio link available with no follower minimum ([link.boo](https://link.boo/guides/tiktok-website-field-requirements)) |
| TikTok or Instagram with no link | Weak/Folklore for sales | No 2025 to 2026 case found |
| Reddit, Facebook groups | Weak; Forbidden if automated or cross-posted | Manual, rule-bound, low scale |

## Twelve popular beliefs that fail the evidence test

The most common advice in seller forums and vendor blogs is either unsupported or already contradicted by Etsy's own pages. These are the ones that would waste the shop's time or put it at risk.

| Belief | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Renew listings daily to stay fresh | Folklore | The boost is real but "smaller" than for a new listing and lasts hours to days; $0.20 per renewal ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/gyc-etsy-academy/deep-dive/how-etsy-search-really-works)) |
| List something every day; more listings rank the shop higher | Folklore | Listing count is not on Etsy's factor list; more listings only add more doors into search ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/how-many-etsy-listings-do-you-actually-need/)) |
| Etsy throttles new shops | Folklore | No source supports it; Etsy describes only a listing-level boost |
| Outside traffic gets you throttled, or heavily boosts rank | Folklore both ways | Etsy pays sellers 4% back for it through Share & Save and says nothing on rank |
| Listing video is a ranking factor | Folklore | No 2025 to 2026 Etsy statement; video's case rests on conversion |
| Keyword-stuffed titles rank better | Against Etsy guidance | April 2026 guidance: short, no repeated words |
| Etsy ignores descriptions | False | Etsy says search reads the description ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)) |
| Keywords must sit in the "first 160 characters" | Folklore | No Etsy page says it; comes from Google snippet advice |
| Tag order matters | Folklore | Old Marmalead analysis, not contradicted since ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/busting-popular-etsy-seo-myths/)) |
| $1 loss leaders harvest reviews | Weak | No 2025 to 2026 data |
| Star Seller lifts ranking | False | Etsy: it "does not directly impact the ordering of search results" |
| Deactivate and relist to regain the new-listing boost | Untested, not advised | A recreated listing loses its favorites, sales and reviews, the signals Etsy ranks on |

Two contested points deserve honesty. Whether the first words of a title carry extra ranking weight is disputed. Etsy's January 2025 advice to keep important terms at the beginning "to maximize their importance in search" ([How to Get Found in Search](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-to-get-found-in-search/22717244953)) sits against an older analysis saying position no longer matters. Front-loading costs nothing and helps phone shoppers either way. Whether editing a listing triggers a fresh evaluation is also split between eRank and Marmalead, and neither cites Etsy.

## Ranked action list: no Etsy Ads, nothing against the rules

The order weighs evidence strength, risk removed, and cost in time. Items 1 to 6 are free and use Etsy-stated levers. Judge listing changes at 21 and 42 days, not daily. Practitioners put ranking settle time at 2 to 4 weeks, though that range is unsourced.

## Conclusion

Strip out the folklore and the honest picture is narrower than most guides suggest. Etsy has told sellers almost exactly what it ranks on, and none of it can be bought, sped up or gamed. That puts a new shop's growth almost entirely inside the listing: does a buyer who lands there see a working tool, trust the specs, get the file open, and come away satisfied enough to review? For ProofNotFluff that is good news. "Proof, not fluff" matches the conversion and review signals Etsy rewards, and it answers the AI-listing complaints buyers are now posting in measurable numbers. The founding sale is the one place the shop is doing the opposite of its own name.

The evidence also sets expectations. Every numeric success story found ran on months-to-years timelines and grew to roughly 30 to 130 listings, and the one with a price history raised prices only after its products improved. Nothing published measures a new spreadsheet shop's path in 2025 to 2026. No data ties any outside channel to sales of $3 to $15 downloads. And there's no current figure for how much a listing video or a first-photo style changes clicks. Treat the shop's own 21- and 42-day before-and-after comparisons as the real evidence base from here, and judge Pinterest no earlier than about 90 days after consistent pinning.
