# Win small phrases, prove the file, climb

The Etsy spreadsheet shops that won did four things together. They put each listing on a search phrase buyers already type. They showed the file working with a demo video, dashboard screenshots and an instructions tab. They charged an effective $1 to $7 for single-purpose tools. And they let reviews compound for two to three years. The verified leaders in ProofNotFluff's own niches (JrenDigital with 82,355 sales, TheProductivePlans with 56.1k, TheSheetStudio with 7.2k) all opened around 2023. Most verified 2024 to 2026 openers in the same niches sit at 120 to 550 lifetime sales, or 7 to 45 a month. The one young breakout found, SheetDesignLab (3,962 sales since 2025), sells $2 budget sheets behind a permanent 70% "sale". That pattern runs on nearly every top listing, and it conflicts with Etsy's January 12, 2026 rule that a struck-through price must be one the item really sold at. The compliant version is simpler: charge the price buyers actually pay at the winners, honestly, with no reference price. For a new shop with no reviews and no Etsy Ads, the evidence points to one strategy. Become the page-one answer for narrow money-decision phrases where page 1 is still held by shops with fewer than about 50 reviews, starting with service-business and maker pricing tools, plus a seasonal January money slot. Build every listing as visible proof. Turn the first 50 to 75 buyers into the first 5 reviews through the download path. Add one owned outside channel with real evidence behind it: free, Google-indexed calculators that demo each paid file. The data also says to judge this at 6 and 12 months, not weekly. A good first year looks like hundreds of sales, not thousands. The current traffic targets of 10 to 50 visits a day by November have no precedent among comparable shops. Etsy Ads are the one lever in the data that gets zero-review listings onto page 1. That is a real trade-off, stated plainly below, but the plan does not depend on ads.

## The secret sauce is demand-first listings shown working at a low price

The winners and the stalled shops in these niches differ on five things. Production quality or social audience is not one of them. Reviews arrive at a steady **4% to 14% of sales** across every shop where both counters are visible, with most mature shops at 5% to 9%. That gives a planning rule of **about 1 review per 10 to 15 sales**, so a new shop needs roughly 50 to 75 sales for its first 5 reviews (calculated from the Etsy counters in the shop links above). Those first reviews matter more than any later ones. Northwestern's Spiegel Research Center measured purchase likelihood at 5 reviews as 270% higher than at zero, with nearly all the gain inside the first 10. Likelihood peaked at 4.0 to 4.7 stars, not 5.0 ([Spiegel Research Center](https://www.xait.com/hubfs/Spiegel_Online-Review_eBook_Jun2017_FINAL.pdf)).

| Factor | Winners | Stalled shops |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Each listing on a proven, high-volume phrase; McDermott ran several listings per strong keyword ([Medium](https://emily-mcdermott.medium.com/how-ive-made-280k-selling-spreadsheets-on-etsy-51b0759a9465)) | Clone of a crowded phrase with no angle (CosmicTouchBlog), or a scattered catalog (SheetworksStudio, Parsons' first 9 months) |
| Proof | Demo video, dashboard images, instructions tab and sample data on every listing | 3 of 4 stalled listings found had no video ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1530957080/product-pricing-calculator-small)) |
| Effective price | $1 to $7 for single-purpose tools | $10+ with no reviews (RoseBudCoShop), or a same-price clone |
| Catalog | 24 to 70 focused listings | 1 listing, or 455 scattered ones |
| Time | 2 to 4+ years of compounding reviews | No 2025 to 2026 opener in a business niche has broken out yet |

Two things the winners did **not** depend on matter as much as what they did. The first is social reach. Excel Dictionary has about 4.3M Instagram and 2.6M TikTok followers ([Favikon](https://favikon.com/blog/who-is-emma-chieppor-aka-excel-dictionary)), yet its Etsy shop shows 43 sales since 2024 from one $12.99 listing ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/exceldictionary)). The audience-first template brands that did grow on social (Easlo on X, Miss Excel on TikTok) were named people posting several times a day and replying by hand. They sold through their own checkouts ([Yahoo Money](https://money.yahoo.com/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html)). The second is listing volume. Etsy does not list shop listing count as a ranking factor, and "listing count should go up with any good strategy, but it's not good strategy on its own" ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/how-many-etsy-listings-do-you-actually-need/)). SheetworksStudio's 455 listings make that concrete.

Page 1 shows where a new shop can actually compete. On broad phrases, page 1 belongs to big shops. The "airbnb spreadsheet" page showed shops with 384 to 12.7k reviews, and the smallest organic card was TheSheetStudio at 457 ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/airbnb_spreadsheet)). "Etsy profit calculator spreadsheet" is held by shops with 33 to 12.7k reviews ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/etsy_profit_calculator_spreadsheet)), and Craftybase gives away a free Etsy pricing calculator that caps paid demand ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/free-craft-seller-tools)). Narrow trade phrases are different. On "cleaning price calculator," shop review counts on page 1 ran 1, 9, 1.1k, 7, 7, 1, 1 and 33. Four organic cards came from shops with 1 to 33 reviews ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/cleaning_price_calculator)). The only Bestseller-badged cleaning listing came from a shop with about 33 reviews ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/cleaning_business_pricing_calculator_spreadsheet)). "Hourly rate calculator" has just 274 relevant listings, rated low competition, and most are workbooks and printables rather than working calculators ([Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/niche/freelance-hourly-rate-calculator)). **Phrases like these are the only places in the data where a near-zero-review listing reaches page 1 without paying for it.**

Format is a second gap. HayeCreativesCo (96k sales), SheetDesignLab, SimplyOrganizedPro's Bestseller and ManjaSheets all say their files are Google Sheets only and won't work properly in Excel ([HayeCreativesCo](https://www.etsy.com/shop/hayeameri); [ManjaSheets](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1263520001)). Several small shops are Excel only. A shop that tests and ships both, and says so on photo 1, turns the giants' limitation into its reason to be chosen.

Etsy's 2026 direction rewards the same proof-first build. The Q1 2026 shareholder letter describes search moving to "a more personalized, relevance-driven approach" ([Etsy IR](https://investors.etsy.com/_assets/_1a39ccc8616e735402e1359c7fbbd55c/etsy/db/938/10070/shareholder_letter/Q126+Shareholder+Letter_Final.pdf)). On the Q2 call, the CFO described surfacing "items that are higher quality with a little bit higher listings price that are better matches" ([transcript](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/transcripts/ETSY-2Q26-transcript)). The app feed now shows "more new and diverse listings" ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/etsy-q2-earnings-call-highlights)), a mild help for new shops. Buyer pressure runs the same way. Among negative US Etsy app reviews, the share complaining about AI-generated listings rose from 19.3% in June to 32.0% in August 2026 ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-etsy-still-handmade-ai-listings-dropshipping-reviews-2026)). A real, tested, multi-tab calculator shown working answers that complaint directly. A July 2026 study of 91 spreadsheet listings found linked multi-sheet templates sell for 4 to 8 times the price of single-sheet files ([TableTemplates](https://tabletemplates.com/research/)).

## Nearly every top listing runs a sale that Etsy's 2026 rule forbids

The most visible thing the winners share is a permanent strikethrough. SoraCollective and SheetDesignLab show 70% off. TheSheetStudio, KeepItSimpleTemplate, SavvyandThriving and PlannerSheetsCo show 50%. SimplyOrganizedPro shows 60%. CreativeJamCo, ManjaSheets and HayeCreativesCo show 40%, often with a countdown timer ([KeepItSimpleTemplate](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1376080306); [SoraCollective](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1453313943/airbnb-welcome-book-template-house-host); [TheSheetStudio](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1827564178/airbnb-income-expenses-tracker)). This is the "secret sauce" most copied by new sellers. **Under Etsy's January 12, 2026 rule it is not compliant unless the higher price is real.** Etsy's Seller Handbook says: "You must actually sell the item at its regular price for a reasonable period and in reasonable quantities." For a strikethrough, "the higher price must be the genuine, usual price," and "any discount that refers to a previous price must represent real savings" ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1450964704111)). A listing that has shown "50% off" since the day it went live has never sold at its list price. UK regulator research found shoppers assume a "was" price was recently charged, for a meaningful time, with real sales ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/asa-research-into-consumer-understanding-of-reference-prices-in-ads.html)).

ProofNotFluff's own setup has the same flaw. A 30% shop-wide sale (September 28 to October 28) shows new listings at $4.89 against a never-charged $6.99, and the descriptions add a third "real" price that has also never been charged. Incumbents carrying this risk is not a reason to copy it. The pattern has no search upside either: Etsy's disclosure names price only as a recommendations input, not an organic search factor ([Etsy Ranking Disclosures](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/search-advertisement-recommendation/899478564529)). The strongest counter-argument is that a visible discount may lift conversion through anchoring. The notes found no data measuring that on Etsy, and the downside (a policy action against the whole shop) is open-ended. Etsy publishes no enforcement statistics, so the risk can't be sized. That uncertainty is not a reason to take it.

**The compliant alternative is to charge, honestly, what buyers actually pay at the winners.** The effective prices that sell are visible. Single-purpose pricing and budget tools sit at $3.25 to $6.38 (PlannerSheetsCo, MahoganySheets, KeepItSimpleTemplate, ManjaSheets, SimplyOnBudget) ([PlannerSheetsCo](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1813532949)). Bigger multi-tab "systems" sit at $8.92 to $9.75. ReadyFormsStudio's 15-tab cleaning business sheet is $8.92 ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4533006158/house-cleaning-business-spreadsheet)), and khdigitaldesignsco's 302-page emergency binder is $9.75 ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1872152316)). Two- and three-tool bundles sit at $12 to $16: TheSheetStudio's A$16 debt bundle ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/debt-pay-off)) and an 11.2k-review shop's $14.67 small-business bundle ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/cleaning_business_pricing_calculator_spreadsheet)). Fixed fees argue against going much below that band. The $0.20 listing fee plus the 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing take a much larger share of a $2 sale than of a $7 one ([Etsy Fees](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/)). The one seller dataset with a price history held average prices at EUR 2.60 and EUR 1.81 in its first two years, then raised them to EUR 3.94 and EUR 6.92 as reviews and products improved. Revenue grew from EUR 817 to EUR 7,717 over that time, though the price moves were not isolated tests ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/3-years-of-etsy-shop-data-2022-2025)). ProofNotFluff's single-purpose tools at $12.99 to $14.99 cost two to four times the winners' effective price for a comparable file, while the shop has no reviews. That is the price problem to fix, more than the sale badge.

Etsy's own offer tools remain compliant once the regular price is genuine. Favorited-item and abandoned-cart offers go to warm shoppers at most once every 2 days per buyer and expire after 60 days, and Thank-you offers reach past buyers ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108)). A real time-limited sale becomes legitimate after a listing has sold at its regular price for a substantial period. Etsy sets no minimum and defers to local law, so the plan below uses a conservative house rule.

**On paid Etsy Ads, the evidence leans toward them for one specific job, and that should be said plainly.** In the October 2026 market-page snapshots, the low-review listings on page 1 were mostly ads. The top four results for "airbnb spreadsheet" were all ads ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/airbnb_spreadsheet)). A shop with 2 reviews appeared on page 1 of "etsy profit calculator spreadsheet" only as an ad ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/etsy_profit_calculator_spreadsheet)). A first-month digital seller who reached 10 orders on 265 visits was spending $2 a day on ads ([VideoHighlight](https://videohighlight.com/v/6Z3GKZAtN4c)). Ads do not raise organic rank. Etsy says "Running an Etsy Ads campaign does not influence where your listings appear in Etsy search outside of the designated advertising spaces" ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). But the sales they bring add to conversion history and produce reviews, and those do feed organic rank. So the trade-off is concrete. Without ads, a zero-review listing reaches page 1 only on narrow phrases where small shops already sit there. With a few dollars a day on those same narrow phrases, it would likely collect its first sales and reviews faster. The owner has ruled ads out, so the plan below is built entirely on organic levers and accepts the slower start.

## Google-indexed tools, not social posts, are the outside channel with evidence

For an Etsy-first template shop, outside traffic is a small share at best. CraftedCharts, the best-documented template seller with dated 2025 to 2026 numbers, got about 80% of its Etsy visits from Etsy's own search and browse every year. Social media sent 383 Etsy visits in all of 2025, about one a day ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/etsy-vs-shopify-for-digital-products)). Its fastest growth came from somewhere else: Google organic search to an owned store launched in July 2025. Google took about two months to index and test the site. Clicks grew from 800 a month in September 2025 to 10,000 a month by July 2026, impressions from 187,000 to 400,000, and "after about four months of consistent content publishing and updates, backlinks began flowing in organically" with no link building ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/one-year-on-shopify-case-study)). The owned store out-earned the Etsy shop in 2025 (EUR 9,166 vs 7,717), with almost 90% of its traffic from organic search. It also earned more per visit: EUR 0.31 against EUR 0.25 on Etsy ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/etsy-vs-shopify-for-digital-products)). These figures are self-reported, and the two posts' conversion numbers don't fully match, but the direction is clear.

AI Overviews cut clicks on informational pages. Pew data showed users clicked a traditional result on 8% of visits with an AI summary present vs 15% without ([PPC Land summary of Pew](https://ppc.land/ai-search-summaries-reduce-link-clicks-for-google-users/)). Working tools resist that loss. Ahrefs (July 2026) puts it this way: an AI summary "can describe how to... calculate a margin, but it can't hand the user the working converter or the interactive calculator." Its examples include Omni Calculator at about 2.3M US visits a month and Gusto's paycheck calculator feeding payroll sign-ups ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)). A small calculator site sold on Flippa got about 90% of its traffic from a non-Google engine "and its affiliates, including traffic from artificial intelligence sources," which is a reason not to block Bing or AI crawlers ([Flippa](https://flippa.com/12032211-728-peak-monthly-profit-25k-monthly-pageviews-100-margins-passive-calculator-site-earning-with-mediavine-primed-for-seo-growth)). Generic calculator queries are owned by Omni and large SaaS brands. The realistic wins for a small shop are narrow tools tied to its own paid files, like a house-cleaning price calculator or a craft fair booth break-even, where the free page gives the one-off answer and the paid file does the ongoing job. One leg is unproven: **no case in the notes shows Google traffic sent on to Etsy converting.** CraftedCharts sold on its own checkout. ProofNotFluff should measure that leg rather than assume it.

Among the other channels, Pinterest has the best evidence and it is slow. Tailwind's study of more than a million pins found a new image pointing at an existing URL keeps 64% of a first pin's distribution, against 11% for a repeated image, with distribution building over about 90 days ([Tailwind](https://tailwindapp.com/pinterest-marketing/research/2025-benchmark-study-part-three)). Static pins out-click video pins 4.8 to 1.6 per 1,000 impressions in one vendor dataset ([Gensumo](https://gensumo.com/research/pinterest-marketing-benchmarks-report/)). Pinterest's developer guidelines require that "the end user must choose each Pin to be published," so unattended API pinning is off-limits ([Pinterest](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/developer-guidelines)). The last strong spreadsheet case built on Pinterest, ChartSheets at about $40K a month, sold on its own site and predates 2025 ([Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25460887-40-000-per-month-selling-spreadsheet-templates-via-pinterest)). YouTube Shorts can't carry a clickable link in descriptions or comments, so the only clickable YouTube path is a long-form walkthrough with the listing link in its description, with Shorts pointing to it through the related-video link ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157)). The shop's own ledger confirms the mechanism: thousands of Short views have produced no measurable Etsy visits. Etsy's Share & Save program credits 4% of sales that come through the seller's own links in emails, sites and posts. If the buyer later clicks an Offsite Ad within 30 days, that sale becomes an Offsite Ads sale, which carries a 15% fee for shops under $10,000 a year ([Etsy Terms](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/etsys-share-save-terms-program-terms/1162874007996); [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000338367)). Faceless LinkedIn and X posting has no supporting case for template sales. The template brands that grew on X did it through a named person posting 2 to 3 times and replying 10+ times a day ([Yahoo Money](https://money.yahoo.com/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html)).

## Realistic timelines run in months and years, not weeks

No source publishes a credible median time to first sales for a no-ads digital shop. The verified comparables give the honest range. MahoganySheets, the closest match to ProofNotFluff's catalog, reached 121 sales in about 8 months, so about 100 sales took 6 to 8 months on a well-built, video-demoed $3.95 pricing calculator ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4422131039/product-pricing-calculator-for-small)). SimplyOnBudget and RoseBudCoShop ran at 7 to 9 sales a month. At $4 to $10 a sale, the 2024 to 2026 openers imply roughly **$30 to $450 a month in year one**. That is well below the "$500 to $2,000 a month" first-year figure that generic guides repeat without shop data ([SellerToolsHQ](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/how-to-sell-spreadsheets-on-etsy/)).

ProofNotFluff's own traffic sets the floor. At about 2 visits a day and the 3% to 4.4% conversion range in the one multi-year digital-shop dataset ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/3-years-of-etsy-shop-data-2022-2025)), the shop earns about 2 sales a month. That puts 10 sales 4 to 6 months away and 50 sales two years away, and a zero-review shop may convert below mature-shop rates. Reaching 10 sales by the end of January 2027 needs about 3 visits a day at 3.5%. Reaching 50 by mid-2027 needs an average of 5 to 6 a day. Reaching 50 by January would need 11 to 13 a day. The current internal targets (10 visits a day by October 20, 25 by November 3, 50 by November 17) are far above anything a comparable shop shows. They will read as failure even when the shop is on a normal path, so the plan below replaces them with milestones drawn from the comparables. Seasonal demand gives one real lift. "Annual budget" searches run about 1,900 a month most of the year and 2,900 in January and February, against 9,450 listings ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/annual-budget)), and "excel budget" peaks at 3,600 in January ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/excel-budget)). eRank warns that holiday listings need time "to gain traction, collect clicks, and start performing in search" before peak ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/holiday-trend-reports-etsy-sellers/)). That means January money tools should be live in October, not December. "2027 planner" demand already peaked in August 2026 at 22,200 against 70,241 listings ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/2027-planner)), so calendar planners are a late, crowded bet. For an owned Google channel, the one dated case implies about 2 months to index, about 4 before backlinks arrive, and meaningful traffic at 6 to 10 months ([CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/one-year-on-shopify-case-study)). Pinterest is judged at 90 days.

## Operating plan for the automated crew

**The strategy in one sentence:** list only where a new shop can reach page 1, make every listing the obviously proven choice at an honest market price, turn buyers into reviews through the download, and feed it all with free Google-indexed calculators. Everything below serves that sentence. Anything that doesn't serve it gets cut. The threshold numbers marked "house rule" are judgment calls drawn from the evidence above, not Etsy requirements.

### What to build: the demand gate decides, not a listing quota

A new listing gets built only if its primary phrase passes two tests. First, demand: the phrase shows meaningful volume in a third-party tool (keep the existing floor of 100+ eRank searches a month, treated as a relative signal because no tool discloses its method). Second, reachability: a signed-out Etsy search for the phrase shows at least 3 organic, non-ad page-1 listings from shops with under about 100 reviews, or ProofNotFluff already ranks in the top two pages (house rule, from the page-1 review counts above). Phrases that fail reachability become tags, not targets. Every workbook ships in both Excel and Google Sheets, tested in both. Each one includes a Start Here tab, prefilled sample data, a dashboard and a cross-sell tab pointing to the next step up the ladder. Build depth (linked tabs, saved scenarios, a printable output), because depth supports the higher price band and separates the shop from AI filler ([TableTemplates](https://tabletemplates.com/research/)). Aim for 40 to 60 strong listings by the end of Q1 2027, the range where the focused winners operate, and drop the 100-by-November volume target.

Raise a listing one price step only after it has 10+ sales at its current price and an average of 4.5 stars or better. Never show the old price after a raise (house rule). A bundle may state "separately $X" only when X is the components' actual current prices. A strikethrough sale is allowed only on a listing that has sold at its regular price for at least 30 days, with at least 5 sales at that price. The sale runs for no more than 14 days, with at least 60 days between sales on the same listing (house rule: deliberately stricter than Etsy's unquantified "substantial period," [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1450964704111)). Keep the favorited-item, abandoned-cart and Thank-you offers on ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108)). No agent changes a price, sale or offer without the owner's word.

### Reviews, within the rules

For digital items, Etsy's 100-day review window "begins when the buyer downloads the file(s)" ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000572708-How-the-Review-System-Works-for-Sellers)). The download path is therefore the review path. Every file set includes a one-page Start Here PDF and a Start Here tab. Each walks the buyer through downloading in a browser, opening the file in Excel or Google Sheets and getting a first result, and ends with one line: an honest review helps a small shop. The shop's "Note to buyers for digital items" carries the browser instruction too. One polite Etsy message a few days after download, offering help and inviting honest feedback with no incentive, is allowed ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/how-to-get-more-reviews-on-etsy/)). Under the shop's rules only the owner sends it. Forbidden without exception: any discount, freebie or refund tied to a review; friends or family buying in order to review; review swaps; extra accounts ([Etsy Shilling Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/shilling/243317364583)). Message response rate and case rate over three months are ranking inputs ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)). Owner replies within 24 hours and accurate specs (which prevent "not as described" cases) are part of the review engine.

| Monthly | COO | Visits, conversion, sales, reviews and review rate against the milestones; Google Search Console impressions and clicks |

Milestones taken from the comparables, not wishes. By early January 2027: first 10 sales and 3 to 5 visits a day. By April 2027 (month 6): 50 to 100 sales (MahoganySheets' pace), at least 5 reviews, and first Google clicks to the tool pages. By October 2027 (month 12): 150 to 300 sales and 15 to 30 reviews, with at least one listing selling 10+ a month. A listing live 60+ days with under 3 sales gets a retitle onto a better reachable phrase, then a one-step price cut 30 days later, then a retire recommendation to the owner. A listing that sells 10+ in 30 days earns a sibling on an adjacent phrase and a bundle slot. If month 6 lands below 30 sales despite clean listings, the binding constraint is exposure, not product. At that point the owner should revisit the paid-ads trade-off with real data in hand.

## Conclusion

The winners' advantage is mostly time spent being the obvious answer to a phrase, plus a price that makes buying easy. A new shop can't buy time, but it can choose phrases where being the obvious answer is still possible with zero reviews. In ProofNotFluff's niches, those are the trade-specific pricing tools that the generic giants serve badly and that new AI-assisted entrants are only now reaching. That window is closing: ReadyFormsStudio already ships a 15-tab cleaning system with two videos. The cleaning and service-pricing cluster should be deepened in the next few weeks, not in the spring. The other takeaway is that the shop's brand promise and Etsy's 2026 direction point the same way. "Proof, not fluff" means real screenshots, a working demo, honest specs and an honest price. The one place the shop has drifted from that promise, a sale against a price nobody paid, is also its main policy exposure. Fixing it costs nothing except giving up a badge that the evidence says does not move search.

The open questions are measurable from inside the shop. Does Google traffic sent to Etsy convert, or does it belong on an own checkout? Does the zero-review conversion rate sit well below the 3% to 4% mature-shop range? How much slower is the organic start than a small ads budget would have been? The crew's Monday numbers and the April 2027 checkpoint will answer all three better than any published case. They should drive the next set of decisions, including whether to revisit Etsy Ads.
