## The rules for every video in the test
- **One format per slot, rotating**, never the same format twice in a row on a platform and never the old "looks fine, break-even is" frame more than once a week:
  - **F1 Guess the price (H09):** show the job or the month in plain words, pause about 1.5 s on "What would you charge?" (or "Where does it go?"), then reveal the product's number. A real question, not bait: the answer comes in the same video.
  - **F2 Price menu:** 4 or 5 jobs, or 4 or 5 budget lines, each read with its number from the product's own example (trade presets, price sheet, budget categories). Built to be saved.
  - **F3 Myth vs math (H08):** one rule people repeat ("charge 3x your costs", "the 50/30/20 rule", "minimums are fine"), then the product's numbers.
  - **F4 Big number first (H10/H13):** frame one is a single huge number and at most 6 words; the proof follows.
- **Frame one:** one big number and at most 6 words, readable on a phone at a glance; motion inside the first half second. hook_say_short at most 6 words.
- **Length 15 to 22 seconds:** 3 rows plus the fix at most. Shorter beats complete; the walkthrough carries the detail.
- **A new example each time:** never the same headline number twice in 14 days (check promo/ and shorts/).
- **Broad topics speak to everyone:** no trade name in the hook, title or first caption line; the product line names it.
