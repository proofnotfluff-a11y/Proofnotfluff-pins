## Case 1: E-commerce finance toolkit, premium, off Etsy (Track C)

- What: a flagship product and a line behind it, built from what Todd ran at a fast-growing supplement brand: channel P&L across Amazon, TikTok Shop, DTC and retail; a 13-week cash flow that understands inventory, PO timing and factoring; an S&OP forecast that feeds the plan; a chargeback and dispute tracker; creator deal economics; a retail launch sea-vs-air order model. Each one a verified workbook with a Start Here, the Legal desk's Terms, a demo video and a kit, the way the crew already builds. Priced $49 to $199, not $2.99.
- Who buys it: founders and finance leads at $1M to $30M e-commerce brands, and the fractional CFOs who serve them.
- Evidence (checked Oct 7, 2026): ProjectionHub sells an "Ecommerce Financial Projection Template" at $99 on Gumroad (https://projectionhub.gumroad.com/l/ecommerce-financial-projection-template). Fractional CFO retainers for e-commerce brands run $3,000 to $5,000 a month under $5M revenue and $5,000 to $10,000 at $5M to $30M (eightx.co pricing guide, Mar 12, 2026, updated Jun 20, 2026: https://eightx.co/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-pricing-guide), which is what the buyer pays today for the thinking these tools package. Etsy demand for these phrases is under 20 US searches a month (eRank, Oct 7), so the channel is Google, LinkedIn and Gumroad, not Etsy. Still unchecked: Google search volume for "ecommerce financial model template" and neighbors, the number of competing Gumroad products and their ratings, and whether buyers in this segment prefer Google Sheets.
- Todd's unfair advantage: he built this function from zero at a real brand selling on exactly these channels. No template shop can say that. It only counts if the products carry his experience visibly (worked examples from real operating problems, written in his voice), which runs into the pen-name choice. Decision needed: sell under his real name and LinkedIn, or keep the alias and compete as another template shop.
- Cost: $0 to build. Gumroad takes 10% plus processing; Payhip 5% on the free plan (verify on their pricing pages before choosing). A Gumroad or Payhip account is Todd's signup (approved category, Oct 7).
- Todd's hours: 2 hours for the identity decision and the account; 1 hour per product to check the worked examples against his experience; 30 minutes a week approving LinkedIn posts if the real-name route is taken.
- Expected profit at 90 days: 20 to 40 sales a month at an average $79 is $1,400 to $2,800 a month gross, about $1,200 to $2,500 net of platform fees. That is 10 to 20 times the whole Etsy shop's realistic 90-day output at $2.99. Treat it as the hypothesis to test, not a forecast.
- Risk: no reviews or audience at launch; buyers here want proof (a credible author, a demo, a free sample). Mitigation: the free calculator site hosts the lead magnet (a free 13-week cash flow lite), and the first product ships with a full demo video.
- Smallest test: one flagship (the 13-week e-commerce cash flow with inventory and factoring), one free lite version on the calculator site with email capture, three LinkedIn posts a week through Typefully for 6 weeks (already connected), a Gumroad or Payhip listing at $79. Metric: sales and email signups. Check: 45 days after launch. Quest: approve-premium-line.

## Case 2: Productized finance service (Track D, Todd's call)

- What: a fixed-scope offer such as "finance setup for an e-commerce brand" (chart of accounts, channel P&L, cash flow, S&OP cadence, dashboards) at a fixed fee, or a fractional FP&A retainer. The crew builds every model and dashboard; Todd fronts the client.
- Evidence: the eightx guide above ($3,000 to $10,000 a month retainers). The non-compete from the previous employer applies to longevity-supplement brands for 12 months, so those clients are off the table; everything else is open.
- Why it is the biggest lever: one client at $3,000 a month equals about 1,300 Etsy sales a month at $2.99. The crew makes Todd's hour worth more than any template.
- Why it is not started: it costs Todd's time (roughly 5 to 10 hours a week per client) and conflicts with "hands off". Only his words start it. Quest: ask-services.
