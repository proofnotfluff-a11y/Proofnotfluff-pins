### A. Etsy search and policy
- A1 CONFIRMED. Etsy title guidance (Apr 27, 2026): under 15 words, noun first, then the top 3 descriptors for the category; no repeated words, no subjective words, no gift or occasion phrases, no sale or price words. Extra keywords belong in tags, attributes and the description because search now reads title, tags, attributes, description, first photo and reviews together. | https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697 | 2026-04-27 | second: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487 (2025-08-26)

- A3 CONFIRMED. Search and the home feed are personalized in real time from about 65M buyer profiles (about 30 interests each); Etsy says it ranks for "relevance and quality". | https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/etsy-etsy-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ | 2026-08-12 | second: https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/etsy-three-way-ai-bet-starting-pay-off/ (2026-08-06)

Some Etsy items can be bought through ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and Copilot (US, USD only, no coupons); no seller opt-in, no eligibility rules published, no fee stated. | https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34208252828695 | read 2026-10-05 | second: Q2 2026 call above
- A5 CONFIRMED. Etsy launched an app inside ChatGPT (May 5, 2026, beta): the shopper tags @Etsy, checkout happens on etsy.com. OpenAI's Instant Checkout (in-chat payment) ended about March 2026. ### D. Traffic that converts for digital downloads
- D1 CONFIRMED. Pinterest fresh pins: a new image to an existing URL keeps 64% of a first pin's distribution; the same image and URL again drops to 11%; about 20% of new pins get zero impressions in week one; distribution builds over about 90 days (Tailwind, 1M+ pins). Expect 3 to 6 months before meaningful organic traffic. | https://tailwindapp.com/pinterest-marketing/research/2025-benchmark-study-part-three | 2025-03-27 | second: https://craftybase.com/blog/pinterest-for-etsy-seller-handmade (2026-04)
- D2 UNCONFIRMED. Outbound clicks per 1,000 Pinterest impressions: static 4.8, carousel 4.1, video 1.6 (148K pins, 2025 to 2026). Results are concentrated: the top 1% of pins take over half of clicks. | https://gensumo.com/research/pinterest-marketing-benchmarks-report/ | 2026-03-21 |
