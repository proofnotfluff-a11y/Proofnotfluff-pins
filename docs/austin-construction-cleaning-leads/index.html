How to use this list

Start with the builders, not the buildings. A general contractor with several big jobs open hires subcontractors on each one, so getting on one builder's vendor list can put you in front of many projects.

Call the builder's main office and ask who buys cleaning for the project you name. Have the master permit number and the address ready.

Send a one-page capability sheet the same day: what you clean, crew size, insurance, equipment and two references.

Watch the stage. Permit issued is early, work under way means the City has extended a permit after an inspection or on request, and some permits finaled means parts of the project are already done.

Questions

Where does this data come from?

The City of Austin's Issued Construction Permits dataset on data.austintexas.gov. We pull it every Monday and keep commercial building permits issued in the last 24 months that are still Active, grouped into projects by the City's master permit, valued at $500,000 or more, with a business listed as general contractor.

Why is the valuation so big on some projects?

Austin records a development's valuation on each of its building permits, so a project's valuation can cover several buildings or phases. We show the largest valuation on any of its permits, once. Treat it as a size signal, not a budget or a contract value.

Why are some big projects missing?

Projects with no business listed as general contractor, houses, duplexes and three-unit buildings, carports, pergolas, canopies, enclosures, pools and other structures that are not buildings, parking garages, small sheds and booths, shell-only buildings, interior demolition, and roof, equipment, sign, elevator, site and exterior-only work are left out, and so are projects whose building permits are all closed. This free page shows a sample; the weekly list covers every project that passes the filter.

Do you contact the builders for me?

No. You make your own calls and emails. We never contact builders or anyone else on your behalf.

Does any builder here work with ProofNotFluff?

No. Builders appear because their names are on public City of Austin permits. None has endorsed or works with ProofNotFluff.
