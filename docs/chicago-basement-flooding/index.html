How this page is made

Every day we count two types of Chicago 311 service request from the City's public 311 Service Requests dataset: Water in Basement Complaint, the Department of Water Management's request for water that has backed up into a home or business, and Sewer Cleaning Inspection Request, for a sewer structure such as a catch basin that is full of dirt or debris or not draining.

We count each request by the day it was made and the community area on it, and keep only those counts. Requests the City marks as duplicates are left out.

12 months means the last 12 whole months, and the change compares them with the 12 months before. Days run on Chicago time, and each daily update counts through the day before. The downloads also include the current month so far.

Only counts are kept. No address, location, request number or anything a resident wrote is stored or shown, so the page cannot point to any home, building or block.

Questions

Which Chicago neighborhoods have the most basement flooding calls?

The table on this page ranks all 77 community areas by water in basement calls to 311 in the last 12 whole months, and it updates every day. A higher count can also mean more homes with basements, or residents who report more often.

What is a water in basement call?

A request to Chicago 311 under the City's Water in Basement service, which the Department of Water Management uses for water that has backed up into a home or business. The City asks where the water came from, such as a floor drain, toilet or shower, and says the service is not for water that came in through windows.

What is a sewer cleaning request?

A Sewer Cleaning Inspection request asks the Department of Water Management to clean a sewer structure, such as a catch basin at the curb, in the street or in the alley, that is full of dirt or debris or not draining. The City says catch basins on private property are the owner's responsibility.

Does a call mean a basement flooded at an address?

No. A call is one request to 311, and this page keeps no addresses, so it says nothing about any one home or building.

Which community area is my neighborhood in?

The City counts by its 77 community areas, which hold many neighborhoods: Bucktown and Wicker Park are in West Town, Wrigleyville is in Lake View, and Pilsen is in the Lower West Side.

Can I use these numbers?

Yes. Both downloads are free CSV files of the counts. Please name the City of Chicago 311 Service Requests dataset as the source.
