What counts as hauling and junk removal

A City contract joins this trade when its description uses any of these words: haul; hauling; junk; debris removal; debris hauling; refuse; dumpster; roll-off; waste removal, hauling or disposal; board-up; clean and seal. Purchases of goods, fleet upkeep, design and engineering, software, art, grants, leases, redevelopment deals, deals with other public bodies and airline-run airport terminal projects are left out. See the whole trade map.

  • Awards, not open bidsThis tracker lists contracts the City has already approved. To bid on new work, use the City's bid lists on this page.
  • Award totals are not unit pricesAn award is the most the City may spend over the whole contract, often years and many sites or items. It does not say what one job or one hour pays.
  • Changes are counted separatelyThe first entry for a contract is the award. Later changes (more money, less money, more time) are shown on their own and are never counted in award totals.
  • End dates are mostly on blanket contractsThe City records end dates mostly on blanket contracts (repeat purchases). Many standard contracts, such as most construction, end when the work is accepted, so they do not show as ending.
  • Sorted by the contract's own wordsA contract joins a trade when its description uses that trade's words. One contract can count in two trades, and work described in other words is missed.
