Minimum wage and paid leave

City of Chicago

From July 1, 2026, employers with 4 or more employees

Minimum wage
$17.05 an hour
Tipped workers
$12.96 an hour
Overtime minimum
$25.58 an hour
Tipped overtime
$21.49 an hour
  • Domestic workers and youth workers must receive at least the $17.05 minimum.
  • Paid leave for any reason: 1 hour for every 35 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period, usable from the 90th day of work. Up to 16 hours carry over if not frontloaded.
  • Paid sick and safe leave: 1 hour for every 35 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period, usable from the 30th day. Up to 80 hours carry over.
  • Both apply to employees who work at least 80 hours within any 120-day period.
  • When a job ends, employers with 51 or more employees pay out up to 56 hours of unused paid leave; employers with 1 to 50 do not.

From the City's notice, effective July 1, 2026 and last updated May 27, 2026. The rules are in Chicago Municipal Code 6-105 (minimum wage) and 6-130 (paid leave).

Questions

Are these the rates I should charge customers?

No. BLS counts the straight-time pay employers give employees, before overtime and premium pay and without benefits. It leaves out the self-employed, and a rate you bill a customer also has to cover your costs and profit.
