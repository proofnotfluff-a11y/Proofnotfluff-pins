Who is covered, and Chicago paid leave

  • Chicago: domestic workers and youth workers must receive at least the $17.05 minimum.
  • Cook County: covers employees over 18 who work in Cook County at least 2 hours in any two-week period, for an employer with 4 or more employees (or as a domestic worker), when the employer has a business facility or license in the County.
  • A town can opt out of the County rate or set its own minimum wage. Chicago sets its own, shown in the City of Chicago box.
  • Paid leave for any reason: 1 hour for every 35 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; employees can start using it by the 90th day after they start the job. Up to 16 hours carry over if the employer did not give all 40 hours up front.
  • Paid sick leave: 1 hour for every 35 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; employees can start using it by the 30th day after they start the job. Up to 80 hours carry over.
  • Both apply to employees who work at least 80 hours within any 120-day period. The ordinance has exceptions; check with the City's Office of Labor Standards.
  • When a job ends, employers with 51 or more employees pay out up to 56 hours of unused paid leave; employers with 1 to 50 do not.

The Chicago rules are in Chicago Municipal Code 6-105 (minimum wage) and 6-130 (paid leave).

How this page is made

Pay is the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics survey for May 2025, the Chicago-Naperville-Elgin metropolitan area, read from BLS's own metropolitan area file. It counts straight-time gross pay per hour, before overtime and other premium pay, with tips in and benefits out, and it leaves out the self-employed and owners and partners in unincorporated firms (BLS OEWS questions page). A percentile is the share of workers who earn less: at the median, half earn less and half earn more.

Read the rest of the method

Each trade matches one BLS occupation, except construction helpers, where BLS publishes a line per trade and the page shows each one. BLS publishes no Chicago figures for helpers for painters and for roofers, so those are not on the page.

Public works rates are the Illinois Department of Labor's Cook County prevailing wage schedule posted October 19, 2026. The total is the base rate plus every fringe the schedule lists (health and welfare, pension, vacation, training and other insurance). A blank on the schedule shows as none listed and counts as zero. The trade on this page and the line on the schedule are matched by the kind of work; on a real project the public body and the Department decide the classification.

A crew day is each person's hours times the hourly pay, plus 7.65% for employer Social Security and Medicare on those wages (IRS Publication 15 for 2026), plus the Chicago paid leave and paid sick leave the hours earn at 1 hour of each per 35 hours worked, valued at the same hourly pay. On public works the schedule's fringes are added for every hour, and hours past 8 on a weekday are paid at the overtime multiple of base pay it lists. You type your workers' comp, unemployment tax, benefits and other costs into the calculator as a cost per hour worked; the page has no rate of its own for those.

The minimum wages and leave rules are from the City of Chicago's notice effective July 1, 2026 and Cook County's notice of June 1, 2026. Every Tuesday a check re-reads the City's minimum wage page, the County notice, IRS Publication 15 and the state's prevailing wage page and flags any change to the minimum wages, the payroll tax rates or the prevailing wage posting date.

Questions

What does a construction laborer make in Chicago?

In the Chicago metro the median is $29.27 an hour (BLS, May 2025): half earn more and half earn less. The middle half earn $22.25 to $48.44. On Cook County public works the schedule posted October 19, 2026 sets a laborer at $53.00 base plus $39.11 of fringes. Which line applies depends on the work; check with the Illinois Department of Labor.

How do I work out what a crew day costs?

Multiply people by hours by hourly pay, then add 7.65% employer Social Security and Medicare, the paid leave the hours earn if the crew works in Chicago, and your own workers' comp, insurance and benefits per hour. With the page's example, 2 electricians for 8 hours at the $49.21 median cost $892.58 before your own workers' comp, insurance and benefits. An estimate, not tax or payroll advice; check with the IRS for payroll tax and the City's Office of Labor Standards for paid leave.

What is Chicago's minimum wage in 2026?

From July 1, 2026 it is $17.05 an hour for employers with 4 or more employees, and $12.96 for tipped workers, who must still reach $17.05 with tips. The overtime minimum is $25.58. These are from the City's notice for July 1, 2026; check with the City's Office of Labor Standards for your situation.

How much paid leave does a Chicago employee earn?

1 hour of paid leave for any reason and 1 hour of paid sick leave for every 35 hours worked, each up to 40 hours in a 12-month period, for employees who work at least 80 hours within any 120 days. That is from the City's notice for July 1, 2026; check with the City's Office of Labor Standards for your situation.

What is the prevailing wage, and when does it apply?

It is the hourly rate, plus fringes, that the Illinois Prevailing Wage Act requires contractors to pay workers on public works projects. Cook County's schedule lists base pay and each fringe per trade. Whether a job counts as public works, and which line applies to a worker, depends on the project and the work done; check with the Illinois Department of Labor.

Are these the rates I should charge customers?

No. These are wages paid to employees. A rate you bill also has to cover your crew's unpaid time, your vehicle, insurance, overhead and profit. The contractor and handyman hourly rate calculators work that out from your own numbers.
