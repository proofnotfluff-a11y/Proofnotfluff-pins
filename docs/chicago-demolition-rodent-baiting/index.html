Rodent control, in five steps

Form 480 lists rodent control from the Department of Streets and Sanitation among the approvals for every demolition permit applicant.

  1. A licensed pest control company baits the site.
  2. A rodent control affidavit is filled in.It is part of the rodent control approval.
  3. Streets and Sanitation inspects the site.
  4. Streets and Sanitation sends the approval by email.
  5. The approval is attached to the permit application.Page 2 of Form 480, the demolition permit application, is the checklist of approvals; the rodent control line has a box to show the Streets and Sanitation approval is attached.

From the Department of Buildings demolition permits page and Form 480 (revised Aug 29, 2021), read Oct 27, 2026. Our words; the City's pages are the rule.

The other approvals on Form 480
  • Public Health: the demolition notice of intent, filed through the City's online permit portal (every applicant).
  • Transportation: a public right of way permit, or written word that none is needed.
  • Buildings, for Water Management: a sewer cut and seal permit and its inspection, and a water disconnection permit and its inspection, or written word that they are not needed.
  • Water Management: a hydrant use permit or an approved other water source, such as a water truck.
  • Only for some jobs: a pre-permit inspection for complex demolitions, Housing for buildings with dwelling units, Planning and Development for historic buildings, Transportation for the downtown freight tunnel area, Public Health for flammable liquid tanks, and soil contamination reviews in two areas.

Dates to keep in mind

Idle application
120 days
An application with no activity for 120 days expires. One 90-day extension can be asked for before then.
Paying the fee
120 days
After the City says the permit is ready, the fee must be paid within 120 days; the permit certificate is issued at payment.
Starting work
6 months
Work must start within 6 months of payment and may not stop for more than 12 months in all.

How this page is made

The steps are our plain summary of the City's demolition permits page and Form 480, read Oct 27, 2026. The City's own pages are the rule; when they change, they win.

The permit counts are wrecking and demolition permits the City issued, from its Building Permits dataset, counted by issue date and by the community area of each permit's primary address. Counts only: no permit number, address or contact is kept.

The wait days are the City's processing time field, days from the City starting review to the issue date, for the 437 wrecking and demolition permits issued from March 2026 on, the first month in which the City's records stopped showing 0 days for most of them.

Questions

Does a Chicago demolition permit need rodent baiting?

Yes. The Department of Buildings lists rodent control from Streets and Sanitation among the approvals for every demolition permit applicant: a licensed pest control company baits the site, the affidavit is filled in, and the site passes a Streets and Sanitation inspection. Check with the agency for your job.

Who does the baiting?

A licensed pest control company the applicant hires. Streets and Sanitation then inspects the site.

How does the approval arrive?

By email from Streets and Sanitation. It is then attached to the demolition permit application, Form 480.

What do the permit counts on this page show?

How many wrecking and demolition permits the City issued, by week and community area, from the City's Building Permits records. The rodent control step comes before a permit is issued, so the counts show where that step was recently needed, not where baiting is happening today.

How often is the page updated?

The permit counts update once a week, on Monday mornings. We check the steps against the City's demolition permits page by hand; the date we last read it is on the page.
