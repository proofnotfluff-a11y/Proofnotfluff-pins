How this page is made

Every morning we pull the City of Chicago's public Transportation Department Permits dataset and keep the permits of type DOT_DMPSTR, the City's code for a dumpster permit, that have an issue date. The City lists a permit with several locations as several rows; we count each permit once in the city total.

Each permit is placed in one of the 77 community areas by the latitude and longitude the City gives for the start of its address range, matched against the City's community area outlines. Each permit counts on the day CDOT issued it, and every count runs through the day before that morning's pull. The year-ago column is the same 90 calendar days one year earlier.

Only counts are kept. We never request the applicant name, contact or street address fields. The City's map point is used only to place a permit in a community area and is not kept, and no permit number or address is stored or shown, so the page cannot point to any building, block or business.

Questions

Which Chicago neighborhoods have the most dumpster permits?

The table on this page ranks all 77 community areas by dumpster permits CDOT issued in the last 90 days and updates every morning. The map and the CSV download carry the same counts.

What does a dumpster permit count tell me?

Each permit is the City's approval to place a dumpster on a street or alley, so more permits in an area usually means more cleanouts, renovations and demolitions there. It does not say a dumpster was set out on any given day, and a dumpster on private property, like a driveway, is not part of this count.

Which community area is my neighborhood in?

The City counts by its 77 community areas, which hold many neighborhoods: Wicker Park is in West Town, Bucktown is in Logan Square, Wrigleyville is in Lake View, and Pilsen is in the Lower West Side.

Are dumpster permits up this year?

Check the Vs. a year ago tile at the top: it compares the last 90 days with the same 90 days a year earlier for the whole city. The Change column in the table does the same for each community area. Permits rise in spring and drop in winter, so compare a month with the same month a year before.

How much does a dumpster permit cost in Chicago?

CDOT sets dumpster permit fees in its yearly permit fee schedule. Fees can change, so check with CDOT before you apply.
