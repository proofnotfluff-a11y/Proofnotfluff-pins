Chicago cleaning bids

Is there a website to bid on post-construction cleaning jobs in Chicago?

By ProofNotFluff, run by a finance executive · Updated Oct 26, 2026 from City of Chicago building permits. Portals checked Oct 25, 2026.

The short answer

Only for public work, and even there rarely for the final clean of a building. The City of Chicago, the Chicago Housing Authority and Chicago Public Schools post their own contracts on free portals. On a building project, though, final cleaning is a section of the construction specifications the general contractor works from, so the builder decides who cleans.

The site that helps is the City's own permit data. This week it names the builder on 1,129 open commercial and apartment projects of $500,000 or more in Chicago, and 17 of them are near the end. Get on those builders' bidders lists, and you can be considered when they bid out the clean.

How to get invited to bid

  1. Pick builders, not websites

    List the general contractors with open jobs near you. Skender Construction LLC leads this week with 23 open projects, ahead of Executive Construction, Inc. (23) and JC Anderson Inc (23).

  2. Write to the main office

    Ask to be placed on the builder's bidders list for cleaning, and include your insurance, licenses, references and proof of what your crew can do, as the trade magazine Cleaning & Maintenance Management advises.

  3. Ask how the builder qualifies new trades

    Some builders want a form or a document set before they invite anyone. Ask the office what it needs, send it the same week, and ask who to call about cleaning on a named project.

  4. Use public awards to find more builders

    The City's Department of Procurement Services publishes bid tabulations, awarded contracts and a contract information search. The firm that won a City construction contract is the one to ask about cleaning on that job.

  5. Register once for the work agencies buy themselves

    Set up the City's iSupplier registration and the CHA Supplier Portal account, sign up for DPS Alerts, and check the CPS Contracting Opportunities page. These carry building cleaning and janitorial contracts when an agency buys them directly.

  6. Call before the end of the job

    A permit with partial occupancy approved is near the end, when the final clean gets bought. This week's count at that stage: 17.

  7. Know your price before the invitation comes

    A builder that invites you will want a number quickly. Work out your floor from your own costs first.

Why no website lists the final clean

The person who asked this on the Jobber Community forum got one substantive reply, and it named no website: the replier did not know of one and had contacted realtors and construction companies directly instead. That matches how the work is bought.

Final cleaning is written into the project's specifications. One public university's construction standard, section 01 77 00.1 Final Cleaning, says to "employ experienced workers or professional cleaners for final cleaning", which leaves the choice of cleaner to whoever runs the job. Bid software exists for builders, but it serves their side: ClockShark describes SmartBid as a tool that lets a builder send invitations to bid and prequalify subcontractors. The builder picks who is invited.

What to have ready for a public bid

A W-9 or tax ID in your legal business name for the City's registration, and compliance certificates for the CHA Supplier Portal. The City also runs an M/WBE certification program; SweptWorks notes that many organizations require supplier registration, and that minority-owned, women-owned or veteran-owned certifications can open set-aside contracts.

Read each solicitation's own instructions and deadline. The City's eProcurement guide says to check for addenda, because a quote's status changes to Resubmission Required when one is issued.

Questions

Is there a website to bid on post-construction cleaning jobs?

For public agencies' own contracts, yes: the City of Chicago, the Chicago Housing Authority and Chicago Public Schools post them on their portals. The final clean of a private building is usually bought by the general contractor, so get on the builder's bidders list instead.

Where does the City of Chicago post bids?

At chicago.gov/bids, which links a Bid Opportunity List PDF and the eProcurement bid opportunities. All Department of Procurement Services bids, RFPs and RFQs are advertised through the City's eProcurement system, reached through the iSupplier portal, and responding online needs a City vendor registration.

How do I find the builder on a Chicago project?

Look up the address in the City of Chicago's Building Permits data on data.cityofchicago.org. The contact marked as general contractor is the builder. This week 1,129 open projects of $500,000 or more name one.

Are paid lead or bid sites worth it?

Check what a won job would cost you first. The free cost per lead calculator turns a monthly lead spend and your close rate into the cost of each job you win.

Does a project on the list need a cleaner?

Not necessarily. A permit shows that work is planned or under way and who the builder is. It does not say who cleans the job or whether that work is already taken.
