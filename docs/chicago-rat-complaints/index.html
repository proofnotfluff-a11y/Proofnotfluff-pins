How the ranks are counted

Tied areas share a rank. Change compares the last 30 days with the same 30 days of 2025, rounded to a whole percent; within 1% shows as about the same, and under 10 complaints a year earlier as too few. Median days to close is for the requests the City closed in the last 30 days, from each request's created and closed dates; under 5 closed shows as too few. 3 of the last 30 days' complaints had no community area on the record and count only in the city total.

How these are counted

Pests: the inspection cited violation 38, which the City titles "Insects, rodents, & animals not present". Hood or ventilation: it cited violation 56, "Adequate ventilation & lighting; designated areas used", with an inspector note naming a hood, filter, exhaust, ventilation, vent or fan (56 also covers lighting, which does not count). Inspections are of places whose facility type names a restaurant, with a result of pass, pass with conditions or fail. A place inspected twice counts twice. Areas with under 10 inspections in 12 weeks show as too few. A result reflects what the inspector found at the time of that inspection, and the City notes its dataset may contain duplicates.

How this page is made

Every Monday we count the City of Chicago's 311 service requests of type Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint from its public 311 Service Requests dataset, by the community area on each request.

Counts cover whole days in Chicago time and end the day before the pull. The year-ago figures are for the same 30 calendar days one year earlier. Requests the City marks as duplicates are left out.

Only counts are kept. No address, location, request number or anything a resident wrote is stored or shown, so the page cannot point to any building, block or business.

The Rat Index divides each community area's complaints by its residents, from the City of Chicago's ACS 5 Year Data by Community Area (U.S. Census Bureau estimates the City allocates to community areas), and ranks all 77 areas. The same residents are used for every period, so a rank change comes from the complaints alone. Areas with few residents, like Fuller Park or Burnside, can move a lot on a few complaints.

Median days to close uses only the created and closed dates on the requests the City closed in the last 30 days. The yearly list covers October 1 to September 30 and comes out each October.

Restaurant pest and hood citations come from the City's Food Inspections dataset: inspections of places whose facility type names a restaurant, counted by the community area their location falls in. We count whether an inspection cited violation 38, and violation 56 with an inspector note naming a hood, filter, exhaust, ventilation, vent or fan. Names, addresses and notes are never kept or shown.

Questions

How do I report rats in Chicago?

Call 311, or make a rat complaint on CHI311 online or in the app. The City's Bureau of Rodent Control investigates reported rat sightings and baits rat burrows.

Which Chicago neighborhoods have the most rat complaints?

The Rat Index on this page ranks all 77 community areas by rat complaints to 311 per 1,000 residents in the last 30 days, and the bar chart shows the most complaints in total. Both update every Monday. A higher count can also mean residents there report more often.

What is the Chicago Rat Index?

Our weekly rank of Chicago's 77 community areas by rat complaints to 311 per 1,000 residents, from City records. Each area has its own page with its rank, how the rank moved, the last 12 weeks and restaurant pest citations.

Can I put the Rat Index on my website?

Yes, for free. Pick a community area in the badge section, copy the code and paste it into your page. The badge updates every Monday and links back here. You can also download all the numbers as a CSV.

Which community area is my neighborhood in?

The City counts by its 77 community areas, which hold many neighborhoods: Wicker Park is in West Town, Bucktown is in Logan Square, Wrigleyville is in Lake View, and Pilsen is in the Lower West Side.

Does a complaint mean there are rats at an address?

No. A complaint is one report to 311. It does not say rats were found, and this page keeps no addresses, so it says nothing about any one building or business.

Are there more rat complaints than a year ago?

The tiles at the top compare the last 30 days with the same 30 days a year earlier, and each area's page does the same for that area. The yearly list compares the 12 months to September 30 with the 12 months before.
