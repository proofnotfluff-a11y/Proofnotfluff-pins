Seeing rats where you live?

  1. Call 311, or report a rat complaint on CHI311 online or in the app.
  2. The City's Bureau of Rodent Control says it investigates every reported rat sighting: crews check the alley, replace damaged garbage carts and bait rat burrows.
  3. A property owner can also ask the City to inspect the outside of a building.

How this page is made

Every Monday we count the City of Chicago's 311 service requests of type Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint from its public 311 Service Requests dataset, by the community area on each request.

Counts cover whole days in Chicago time and end the day before the pull. The year-ago column is the same 30 calendar days one year earlier. Requests the City marks as duplicates are left out.

Only counts are kept. No address, location, request number or anything a resident wrote is stored or shown, so the page cannot point to any building, block or business.

Questions

How do I report rats in Chicago?

Call 311, or make a rat complaint on CHI311 online or in the app. The City's Bureau of Rodent Control investigates reported rat sightings and baits rat burrows.

Which Chicago neighborhoods have the most rat complaints?

The table on this page ranks all 77 community areas by complaints in the last 30 days and updates every Monday. A higher count can also mean residents there report more often.

Which community area is my neighborhood in?

The City counts by its 77 community areas, which hold many neighborhoods: Bucktown and Wicker Park are in West Town, Wrigleyville is in Lake View, and Pilsen is in the Lower West Side.

Does a complaint mean there are rats at an address?

No. A complaint is one report to 311. It does not say rats were found, and this page keeps no addresses, so it says nothing about any one building or business.

Are rat complaints up this year?

The tiles at the top compare the last 30 days with the same 30 days a year earlier, citywide and for each community area.
