How this page is made

Every Monday we count the City of Chicago's 311 service requests of type Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint from its public 311 Service Requests dataset, by the community area on each request.

Counts cover whole days in Chicago time and end the day before the pull. The year-ago column is the same 30 calendar days one year earlier. Requests the City marks as duplicates are left out when the dataset carries that flag.

Only counts are kept. No address, location, request number or anything a resident wrote is stored or shown, so the page cannot point to any building, block or business.

Questions

How do I report rats in Chicago?

Call 311, or report it on CHI311 online or in the app, and ask for rodent baiting. Give the address or the alley where you saw them.

Which Chicago neighborhoods have the most rat complaints?

The table on this page ranks all 77 community areas by complaints in the last 30 days and updates every Monday. A higher count can also mean residents there report more often.

Does a complaint mean there are rats at an address?

No. A complaint is one report from a resident. It does not say rats were found, and this page keeps no addresses, so it says nothing about any one building or business.

Are rat complaints up this year?

The tiles at the top compare the last 30 days with the same 30 days a year earlier, citywide and for each community area.
