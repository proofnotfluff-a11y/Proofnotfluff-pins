Seeing rats where you live?

  1. Call 311, or report a rat complaint on CHI311 online or in the app.
  2. The City's Bureau of Rodent Control says it investigates every reported rat sighting: crews check the alley, replace damaged garbage carts and bait rat burrows.
  3. A property owner can also ask the City to inspect the outside of a building.
