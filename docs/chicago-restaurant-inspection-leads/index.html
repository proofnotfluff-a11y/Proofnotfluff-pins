How to use this list

Pick your category. A pest violation is a pest control lead, a ventilation or hood violation is a hood cleaning lead, and a waste water or grease trap violation is a grease trap lead.

Call within a few days of the inspection date, during a quiet hour (2 to 4 pm suits most kitchens). Name the City's public record and its date, and leave any judgment out of it.

Offer what helps before the re-inspection: a service visit, a service log or a cleaning record the inspector can see.

Work the openings list early. A place that just had its license inspection is choosing every vendor for the first time.

Questions

Where does this data come from?

The City of Chicago Data Portal's Food Inspections dataset. We pull it every Monday and keep the last 30 days of inspections up to the newest one the City has published, because the City posts results a few days late.

What do the categories mean?

They are our reading of the City's violation codes: Pests is violation 38 (insects, rodents and animals) and any inspector note naming pests; Hood is violation 56 when it names a hood, filter, exhaust or grease; Grease trap and drains is violation 52 and any note naming a grease trap, interceptor or clogged drain; Cleaning is violation 49 and cleaning notes under 53, 54, 55 and 60.

Who is left out?

Schools, colleges, day care and other children's sites, hospitals, nursing, rehab and senior sites, shelters, mobile vendors, and places the City last marked out of business or not located are left out. So is a place whose business name reads as only a person's name: a first and last name, with or without LLC or Inc, or a title and a first name, such as "Chef Maria". A name with a business word or a possessive, such as "Garcia Bakery" or "Maria's Kitchen", stays. Names are checked against lists of common first names and surnames, so the check can miss one; if a business on the list goes by a person's name, write to hello@proofnotfluff.com.

Does a place on the list have a problem today?

Not necessarily. The list repeats the City's result on the inspection date. Many places pass a re-inspection within days, and the members' list shows that newer result next to the old one.

Do you contact these businesses for me?

No. You make your own calls and emails. We never contact anyone on your behalf, and no business listed has endorsed or works with ProofNotFluff.

Why doesn't this page show names?

On this public page we never name a business with a failed result. Members get the full list, licensed to their own business for its own sales calls.
