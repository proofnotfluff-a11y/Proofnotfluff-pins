Questions

Where does this data come from?

The City of Chicago Data Portal's Food Inspections dataset. We pull it every Monday and keep the last 30 days of inspections up to the newest one the City has published, because the City publishes results after the inspection date.

What do the categories mean?

They are our reading of the City's violation codes: Pests is violation 38 (insects, rodents and animals) and any inspector note naming pests; Hood is violation 56 when it names a hood, filter, exhaust or grease; Grease trap and drains is violation 52 and any note naming a grease trap, interceptor or clogged drain; Cleaning is violation 49 and cleaning notes under 53, 54, 55 and 60.

Who is left out?

Schools, colleges, day care and other children's sites, hospitals, nursing, rehab and senior sites, shelters, mobile vendors, and places the City last marked out of business or not located are left out. So is a place whose business name reads as only a person's name: a first and last name, with or without LLC or Inc, or a title and a first name, such as "Chef Maria". A name with a business word or a possessive, such as "Garcia Bakery" or "Maria's Kitchen", stays. Names are checked against lists of common first names and surnames, so the check can miss one; if a business on the list goes by a person's name, write to hello@proofnotfluff.com. The daily pest list also leaves out license inspections, the check the City does before it issues a food license.

Does a place on the list have a problem today?

Not necessarily. The list repeats the City's result on the inspection date. A place can pass a re-inspection soon after; the members' list shows any newer result next to the old one.

Do you contact these businesses for me?

No. You make your own calls and emails. We never contact anyone on your behalf, and no business listed has endorsed or works with ProofNotFluff.

Why doesn't this page show names?

On this public page we never name a business with a failed result. Members get the full list, licensed to their own business for its own sales calls.
