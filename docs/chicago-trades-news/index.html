City Council approves $7.3 million TIF for Altgeld Gardens community center rehab

The City Council approved $7.3 million in TIF funding and landmark status for the 1945 Up Top Building at 13100 S. Ellis in Altgeld Gardens. By The Hand Club For Kids plans a $25.8 million conversion into a community center. Construction is expected to begin in fall 2026 and finish in fall 2027.

What it means for you: A City funded landmark rehab on the Far South Side is starting soon. Check who the general contractor will be and whether the TIF carries City hiring or contracting requirements.

Foundry Park breaks ground on the old Lincoln Yards site

JDL Development and Kayne Anderson Real Estate broke ground on October 21 on Foundry Park, a $3 billion project along the North Branch of the Chicago River. Budget plan would raise Chicago's tax on equipment rentals and cloud software to 20%

Mayor Johnson's proposed 2027 budget raises the City's personal property lease transaction tax from 15% to 20%, worth about $234 million in 2027. That tax covers rented equipment, software and cloud computing; car leasing would stay at 15%. City Council budget hearings began October 26 and the Council has not voted.

What it means for you: If it passes, rented lifts and tools and software subscriptions such as scheduling, invoicing and office apps could cost more. Check which of your bills already carry the Chicago lease tax so you know what would change.

For: All trades

Sources:WBEZ, head tax returns in the budget planWTTW, Johnson's proposed 2027 budgetChicago Sun-Times, budget hearings

Tax and payProposedReported Oct 21, 2026

Chicago's 2027 budget plan brings back a per-worker tax on large employers

Mayor Johnson's proposed 2027 budget includes a tax of $25 a month for each employee at companies with more than 125 workers, which the City expects to raise about $100 million a year. Last year the Council rejected a version at $33 per worker. Council budget hearings began October 26, and the budget needs 26 of 50 votes to pass.

What it means for you: The tax only applies above 125 workers, so check your headcount if you are near that line. Larger contractors and cleaning or facility service firms should follow the budget hearings and the vote.

For: All trades

Sources:WTTW, Johnson's proposed 2027 budgetWBEZ, head tax returns in the budget planFox 32, budget hearings begin

Winter leans mild, with more sleet possible; parking ban starts December 1

NOAA's December to February outlook leans warmer than normal for the Chicago area with even odds on precipitation, as El Nino strengthens, and an NBC 5 meteorologist says that could mean more days of mixed sleet, snow and freezing rain. The City's overnight parking ban on 107 miles of main streets runs 3 am to 7 am from December 1 to April 1.

What it means for you: A milder winter could bring more icy mixed days instead of all snow, though snowstorms can still happen, so watch the forecasts and check your salt supply. Check where your trucks and trailers park overnight before the December 1 ban.

For: Landscaping and snow, Roofing

Sources:NBC Chicago, winter outlookCity of Chicago, Winter Snow Clearing

Social Security tax will apply to the first $192,300 of pay in 2027

The Social Security Administration raised the maximum earnings taxed for Social Security from $184,500 in 2026 to $192,300 in 2027. Medicare tax still has no earnings cap.

What it means for you: Owners and crew members who earn more than $184,500 will owe Social Security tax on more of their pay next year. Check that your payroll service and your 2027 estimated tax payments use the new number.

For: All trades

Rules and permitsStarts Dec 1, 2026Reported Oct 9, 2026

Chicago's updated construction codes are required for permits filed from December 1

The City Council passed ordinance SO2026-0024930 on June 17, 2026, fully updating Chicago's mechanical, fuel gas, energy and interim fire prevention codes to current model codes, with general changes to the building and rehab codes. The new codes have been optional since October 1 and are required for projects submitted from December 1, 2026, counted from the day the permit fee is paid.

What it means for you: Check which jobs will have their permit fee paid on or after December 1, since those must meet the new codes. HVAC contractors should note that all dwelling units now need ducted make up air, and the energy code moves to the 2024 IECC.

For: General contractors, HVAC, Electrical, Roofing

Sources:MAP Strategies, 2026 Chicago construction code updatesBOMA/Chicago, City advances construction code updates

Board President Toni Preckwinkle's $9.69 billion plan for 2027 closes a $550.8 million gap without a property tax increase, new fees or layoffs. It uses reserves, interest on federal relief money and cuts 50 vacant jobs.

SafetyStarts Jan 1, 2027Reported Oct 2, 2026

Illinois genetic privacy law expands to biomarker tests; drug testing questions raised

Public Act 104-0642 extends the Genetic Information Privacy Act to biomarker testing, so employers may not require genetic or biomarker tests as a condition of work or act on the results. A law firm reports the change starts January 1, 2027 and that some employers worry the wording could reach workplace drug tests.

What it means for you: If you run pre hire, random or post accident drug tests, ask your testing vendor or lawyer to review the program before January. The law does not mention drug testing by name.

For: All trades

Sources:Illinois General Assembly, Public Act 104-0642Amundsen Davis, did Illinois ban workplace drug testing

1099s for 2026 payments start at $2,000, not $600

Under the 2025 federal tax law, businesses send Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC only for payments of $2,000 or more made from January 1, 2026, up from $600, with inflation adjustments from 2027. Payment apps file Form 1099-K only above $20,000 and 200 payments.

What it means for you: When you send 1099s to subcontractors early next year, the cutoff for 2026 payments is $2,000. Keep collecting a W-9 from every sub.

For: All trades

City contractors and their subs face new conduct certifications; rules due November 24

An executive order signed August 26 directs the City's chief procurement officer to write rules, due by November 24, 2026, requiring firms seeking City contracts over $250,000 to certify no public integrity, discrimination or wage theft violation in the past five years. Primes must pass matching terms down to their subcontractors.

What it means for you: If you work as a sub on City jobs, expect new certification forms from your prime. Make sure your wage and hour records are in order now, since wage theft counts.

For: All trades

Sources:Skadden, Chicago procurement executive orderWTTW, executive order on City contractors

Rules and permitsStarts Jan 1, 2027Reported Aug 19, 2026

From 2027, job ads can ask for a driver's license only when driving is essential

A change to the Illinois Job Opportunities for Qualified Applicants Act, HB 4758, says that from January 1, 2027 a job posting may require a valid driver's license only when driving is an essential part of the job and a business necessity, and the posting must say why. It covers employers with 15 or more employees and employment agencies.

What it means for you: Review every job ad that asks for a driver's license before January. Check that driving is an essential part of that job and that the ad briefly explains why the license is needed.

For: All trades

Sources:Saul Ewing, Illinois employment law updateHinshaw, Illinois employment law changes for 2027Illinois General Assembly, Public Act 104-0776

New City review for data centers; a temporary pause is proposed

An executive order signed August 11 sends any data center that needs a new Planned Development through a review of power, water, backup generators, air, noise and neighborhood impact, and orders new air permit and noise rules. Mayor Johnson has also called on the City Council to pause new or expanded data centers until permanent rules are in place, and the Council has not voted on it.

Tax and payIn effect since Jan 1, 2026Reported Aug 6, 2026

Overtime on 2026 W-2s goes in Box 12, Code TT, for the federal overtime deduction

The IRS updated its guidance on the federal overtime deduction, worth up to $12,500 a year or $25,000 on a joint return, with a phase out at higher incomes. For 2026, employers report qualified overtime in Box 12 of the W-2 with Code TT, and the 2025 relief is not extended.

What it means for you: If you pay hourly crews overtime, set up payroll now to track the federally required overtime premium so January W-2s are right. Only overtime the federal rules require counts, not extra premiums from a union contract or company policy.

For: All trades

Sources:EY Tax News, IRS 2026 overtime guidanceJournal of Accountancy, IRS overtime FAQs

Rules and permitsStarts Jan 1, 2027Reported Aug 5, 2026

Illinois Attorney General gets new powers to investigate employers from January 1

HB 4725, signed July 31, 2026, lets the Attorney General's Workplace Rights Bureau issue subpoenas, require sworn written answers, inspect non public parts of a business, copy records on site and interview workers, starting January 1, 2027. Knowingly hiding or destroying records under subpoena can be a Class A misdemeanor.

What it means for you: Starting January 1, possible violations of Illinois labor and employment laws can lead to state subpoenas, on site inspections and worker interviews without a court order. Check that your timesheets, pay records and worker classification files are complete before January.

For: All trades

Sources:Amundsen Davis, Attorney General investigation powersSaul Ewing, Illinois employment law update

Illinois employers with more than 25 workers must pay for jury duty from 2027

The Governor signed HB 4844 on July 31, 2026, so employers must pay workers their regular rate for time on jury duty starting January 1, 2027. Employers with 25 or fewer employees still only have to give unpaid leave.

What it means for you: If you have more than 25 employees, review your handbook and payroll setup for paid jury duty before January. Smaller shops keep giving unpaid time off for jury service.

For: All trades

Sources:Illinois General Assembly, Public Act 104-0683Hinshaw, Illinois employment law changes for 2027Illinois General Assembly, HB 4844 statusKilpatrick Townsend, Illinois employers must pay for jury service

Chicago's minimum wage is $17.05 an hour

Chicago's minimum wage rose from $16.60 to $17.05 an hour on July 1, 2026, for employers with four or more workers, and the tipped wage is $12.96. Suburban Cook County is $15.40 and the Illinois minimum stays at $15.

How this page is made

Every morning we read the City of Chicago, Cook County, State of Illinois and federal pages that publish new rules, taxes, wage rates and licenses, plus Chicago's main news outlets, and pick the items that change how a trade business works or what it pays.

Every item is written in our own words and links the page it comes from. We prefer the government page itself; when an item rests on news reports, the source line says so. A second reviewer checks every number and date against its source before the brief is published.

Proposed means not passed: budget items, ordinances and bills can change or fail. Items stay on the page for about four months, or until their start date passes.

This is news, not legal, tax or financial advice. Before you change a price, a payroll setting or a filing, check the source and ask your accountant, lawyer or licensing office.
