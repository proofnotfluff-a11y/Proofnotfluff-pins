How the example adds up

Three lines of work come to $760.00: a 2-story house wash at $355, a 2-car driveway at $200 and gutter brightening at $205. A 10% discount takes off $76.00, leaving $684.00. Sales tax at an example 5% adds $34.20, so the total is $718.20. A 25% deposit of $179.55 books the job, and $538.65 is due when it is done. The three prices are the quotes the free pressure washing price calculator gives for its own example business, not market prices for your area. Whether a service is taxed, and at what rate, depends on where you work.

Deposits and cancelling. Rules on deposits, cancelling and the wording of signed estimates vary by state and city, especially for work sold at the customer's home. This template doesn't say what any law requires. Have a local attorney check your deposit, payment and cancellation terms before you use them with clients.

Questions

What should a job estimate include?

Your business and contact details, the customer and job address, an estimate number and date, each piece of work with its quantity and price, any discount, tax and deposit, how long the prices hold, your payment terms, and a place for the customer to accept. The template has all of these on one page.

Is an estimate the same as a quote or a contract?

People use the words differently. This template is a sample form to adapt, not a legal document written for your state or business. Whether signed terms hold up as an agreement depends on your state and on what they say. Have a local attorney review them before you use them with clients.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it, then File, Save as Google Sheets. It uses plain formulas only.
