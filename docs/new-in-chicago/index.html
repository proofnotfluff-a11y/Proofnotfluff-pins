How this page is made

Every business here got a new City of Chicago business license in the last 7 days, at an address our checks show as a business site. We read the City's Business Licenses data every week and keep one row per business and address.

Most are first licenses. Some are a business's new site, a license added at a site it already has, or a site licensed again.

Left out on purpose: renewals, licenses for one person's own work or for an event or a season, home and mailbox addresses, names that could be a person's full name, and food businesses with a failed City inspection on file. A business can be missing for many reasons, and being listed or missing says nothing about its inspections, quality or standing.

A license is not an opening date. Many places open a few weeks after the license, and a few never open. Check with the business before you go.

Questions

Is this every new business in Chicago?

No. It is the new City business licenses issued at business sites in the last 7 days, after the exclusions above. Some businesses, like many offices and online shops, need no City business license, so they are not here.

How often does it change?

Every week, from the City's own records. The date at the top says when the records were pulled.

Can a listing be removed or corrected?

Yes. Email hello@proofnotfluff.com with the name and address. If you run the business, or a listing shows your home or your name, we take it off before the next Monday page and keep it off the weekly email and the business list.
