How this page is made

Every business here got a new City of Chicago business license in the last 7 days at a business address. We read the City's Business Licenses data every Monday and keep one row per business and address.

Left out on purpose: renewals, licenses held by one person or for an event or a season, home and mailbox addresses, names that could be a person's, and food businesses with a failed City inspection on file.

A license is not an opening date. Many places open a few weeks after the license, and a few never open. Check with the business before you go.

Questions

Is this every new business in Chicago?

It is every new City business license issued at a business address in the last 7 days, after the exclusions above. Some businesses, like many offices and online shops, need no City business license, so they are not here.

How often does it change?

Every Monday, from the City's own records.

Can a business ask to be removed?

Yes. Email hello@proofnotfluff.com from the business and we take it off the next page.
