How it works

A load-size price list copied from another hauler only fits if your crew, your drive, your dump and your pay goal match theirs. This calculator builds each load's price from your own numbers instead.

It grosses your take-home up for self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of profit) and your income tax estimate, adds a year of business costs, and spreads the total over the hours customers pay for. That is your break-even rate. A load's floor is every crew member's time on site and drive time at that rate, plus vehicle cost per mile and the supplies and dump fee for that load. The quote adds your margin and rounds up to the next $5.

Bigger loads take longer to load and cost more to dump, so the price does not scale in straight fractions: the drive and the trip cost about the same for a 1/8 load as for a full one.

A worked example

The Junk Removal Pricing Calculator's example business wants $60,000 take-home with a 12% income tax estimate and $715 a month of costs, works 48 weeks of 50 hours and is paid for 60% of them: a break-even rate of $62.36 per paid hour. With 2 people, a 30-mile round trip and the dump fee counted in supplies, its price sheet quotes a 1/8 minimum load at $275, a quarter load at $370, a half load at $485, a three-quarter load at $600 and a full truck at $720. The half load's floor is $409.62 (1.5 hours on site each and $75 of supplies and dump fees), so $485 leaves $75.38 of profit. A full load is 2.6 times the price of a 1/8 load, not 8 times, because the drive costs the same.

Questions

How much should I charge for a full truck load of junk?

It depends on your pay goal, costs, crew, drive and dump fees. With the example numbers a full load for 2 people, 2.5 hours on site each and $150 of supplies and dump fees quotes at $720. Enter your own numbers to see yours.

Why is a half load not half the price of a full load?

Drive time, the vehicle and the trip to the dump cost about the same whatever the load size, so small loads carry more of them. In the example a half load quotes at $485 and a full load at $720.

What is a minimum load?

The smallest job you take, often a 1/8 truck: a few bags or one bulky item. The example prices it at $275 so a short job still pays the drive and both people.

Where do dump fees go?

Put dump, recycling and disposal fees in supplies for the load. Put the extra miles for each dump run in round-trip miles.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is a planning estimate. Ask your tax preparer for your real income tax rate.
