Policy

Refunds and cancelling

No refunds on lead lists or the workbook, because you get the data the moment you pay. Cancel a list any time and you won't be charged again. If a list doesn't arrive or a file won't open, email hello@proofnotfluff.com and we'll fix it or refund that month.

Cancelling a list

Each list is a monthly membership through Gumroad, our checkout. Cancel from the link in your Gumroad receipt or from your Gumroad library, any time, and you won't be charged again.

If something goes wrong

If a Monday list does not arrive, the members' app does not open or a file is broken, write to hello@proofnotfluff.com with your Gumroad receipt. We fix it, or refund that month.

Before you buy

Every list has a free sample of this week's records on its page, and the workbook has a free calculator with the same maths, so you can see what you get first.

Policy updated Oct 22, 2026. ProofNotFluff LLC, Chicago.
