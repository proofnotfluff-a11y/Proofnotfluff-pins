Policy

Privacy

We collect as little as we can: an email address when you ask us for an email, and work contact details for the businesses we write to about leads. We never sell any of it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up on this site for the free Monday jobs email, a waitlist or the cleaning price cheat sheet.
  • For businesses we email about leads: the business name, the decision maker's name and job title, and their work email, taken from public business records, the company's own website or an email finding service. We also keep your reply, such as a yes or a stop.
  • When you buy a list: Gumroad, our checkout, gives us your name, your email and what you bought. Gumroad takes the card; we never see your full card number.
  • Visits: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. The calculators run in your browser and send nothing. In the members' apps, contacted marks and notes stay in your own browser.

Why

  • To send the email you asked for and to answer you.
  • For businesses we email: to ask whether you want the free Monday email, with at most two follow ups, and to send it if you say yes.
  • For members: to deliver the list and run your membership.

Who holds it

  • Kit, our email provider, holds sign ups from this site and sends the Monday email to them.
  • Our CRM, a private database only ProofNotFluff can open, kept in our Claude workspace (run by Anthropic), holds the business contacts and whether each said yes or stop.
  • Google runs our inbox, so the emails you send us and our replies sit in Gmail.
  • Gumroad holds checkout and membership records.

Nobody else gets it. Our site code and a few encrypted contact records sit on GitHub, where only we hold the key, and the email finding service gets only what we look up: a business website and, at times, the owner's name. We never sell or rent your details, and never share them for anyone else's marketing.

How to stop

  • Reply "stop" to any email from us, or use the unsubscribe link at the foot of any email Kit sends. We take you off before the next send, and you won't get another one.
  • Members cancel in their Gumroad library (see Refunds and cancelling).
  • To see what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to hello@proofnotfluff.com. We keep only the note that you asked us to stop, so we never email you again.

Changes and tracking

If this policy changes, the date below changes and the new text goes on this page; we do not email about changes. This site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals, because it does not track you across sites in the first place. The Monday email form sends your address straight to Kit, which has its own privacy policy.
