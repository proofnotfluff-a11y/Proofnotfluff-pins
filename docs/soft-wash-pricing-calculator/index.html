How it works

A soft wash price copied from another company only fits if your crew, your drive, your chemical mix and your pay goal match theirs. This calculator builds the price from your own numbers instead.

It grosses your take-home up for self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of profit) and your income tax estimate, adds a year of business costs, and spreads the total over the hours customers pay for. That is your break-even rate. A job's floor is each person's time on site and drive time at that rate, plus vehicle cost per mile and supplies; put the soft wash mix, surfactant and any downstreamer chemicals in supplies.

The quote adds your margin and rounds up to the next $5. The example roof wash takes two people, so both people's time and drive time count, which is why it costs more than a house wash of similar hours.

A worked example

The Pressure Washing Pricing Calculator's example business wants $55,000 take-home with a 12% income tax estimate and $755 a month of costs, works 44 weeks of 45 hours and is paid for 65% of them: a break-even rate of $64.89 per paid hour. A 1-story house soft wash (2.5 hours on site, $30 of soft wash mix and surfactant, a 20-mile round trip) has a $250.69 price floor and quotes at $295. A 2-story house (3 hours, $45 of supplies) quotes at $355, and at $420 when the round trip is 40 miles. A 1-story roof soft wash with 2 people for 2 hours each and $70 of supplies has a $431.29 floor and quotes at $510.

Questions

How much should I charge to soft wash a house?

It depends on your pay goal, costs, crew and drive. With the example numbers a 1-story house soft wash needs $295 and a 2-story $355. Enter your own numbers to see yours.

Why does a roof soft wash cost more than a house wash?

In the example the roof takes 2 people for 2 hours each and more chemical, so it carries twice the labor and drive time of a one-person job: $510 against $295 for a 1-story house.

Where do soft wash chemicals go?

Put the mix, surfactant and any other chemicals used on that job in supplies for the job.

Does drive distance change the price?

Yes. Drive time is paid at your break-even rate and each mile at your vehicle cost. In the example a 2-story soft wash goes from $355 to $420 when the round trip grows from 20 to 40 miles.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is a planning estimate. Ask your tax preparer for your real income tax rate.
