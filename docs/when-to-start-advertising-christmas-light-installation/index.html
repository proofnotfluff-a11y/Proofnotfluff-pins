Christmas light marketing

When should you start advertising Christmas light installation?

By ProofNotFluff, run by a finance executive · Worked example from the free Christmas light installation pricing calculator. Checked Oct 29, 2026.

The short answer

Start in September with anyone you hung lights for last year, and start advertising to new customers in October. Jobber's guide says to rebook past customers as early as late August or early September and to begin marketing to new ones in October or early November, with little response until after Halloween. Service Autopilot's guide says to start marketing in September.

The reason is the calendar, not the ads. Google searches for "christmas light installation" run at about twice the October level in November, when many people want lights up for Thanksgiving. In the worked example, one two-person crew fits about 36 houses into the 18 weekdays before Thanksgiving, so a crew that waits for the November rush is selling into days that are already going.

A season calendar, month by month

  1. Late August and September: rebook last year's houses

    Call or text every past customer, offer the same date as last year and take a deposit. Jobber's guide suggests a small discount for booking early. Every rebooked house is a weekday slot you no longer have to sell.

  2. September: count your slots

    Time your crew, put your pace and an average house into the free calculator, and work out how many installs fit before Thanksgiving. In the example that is 36 at 8-hour days. That number is your sales target, not a guess.

  3. October: advertise to new customers

    Yard signs at the houses you already hang, a note to the customers you clean windows or gutters for, and paid ads if you use them. Jobber's guide runs social ads from October into early December. Sprinkler Warehouse's guide notes installer signs start showing up in early October.

  4. November: fill the gaps, then sell what is left

    Searches peak in November. By then, most of your pre-Thanksgiving slots should be booked, so sell the rest as after-Thanksgiving installs, and say so when you quote.

  5. January: hold the takedown days

    Takedown starts after New Year's in all three guides. In the example, the 36 houses take about 9 working days to bring down at 8-hour days. Book those days before you sell more installs.

What the thread says

The question was posted Sep 20, 2020 on Pressure Washing Resource by a member looking at installing lights that year, who asked what time of year to start advertising. One member replied to start soon, noting stores already had Christmas stock out, and said quite a few do lights in winter when their climate shuts down their other work. Another reply pointed to a member who writes about installing lights, and quoted that member's post: "2 weeks of short days and 1 week in January to take them all down will put another $4800 cushion after expenses."

No reply named a month. The guides below do, and they agree more than they differ.

What the guides say about timing

Jobber's guide (updated Nov 13, 2024): rebook past customers as early as late August or early September, market to new customers in October or early November, run social ads from October through early December, and expect the busiest stretch in the two weeks before and after Thanksgiving. Teardown can start as early as Jan 2.

Service Autopilot's guide (updated Aug 10, 2021): start marketing in September, though the bulk of orders may not come in until October and November; removals run after New Year's into mid-January. Sprinkler Warehouse's guide (published Oct 14, 2021): start advertising around mid-fall and plan your labor days backward from Thanksgiving.

When customers search

In Google Trends for the United States over five years, read Oct 26, 2026, "christmas light installation" peaks in November: in our comparison with four other trade terms, the five-year November average was 14.2 on Google's index against 6.5 in October, about twice. The index shows the shape of the year, not search counts. It says the people searching in November are mostly late: the crew that advertised in September and October should have sold most of the weekdays before Thanksgiving by then.

Why the slots, not the ads, set the date

Lights go up in a short window and come down in a shorter one. In the example, a crew working 6-hour days fits 18 houses before Thanksgiving and a crew working 10-hour days fits 54. Whatever your number is, the season can hold only that many installs, so the job of early advertising is to sell them at your price before the November rush, not to find more customers than you can hang for.

Questions

When should I start advertising Christmas light installation?

Rebook last year's customers in late August or September and start advertising to new customers in October. Searches peak in November, when the weekdays before Thanksgiving should already be mostly sold.

When do people book Christmas light installation?

Many want lights up by Thanksgiving; Sprinkler Warehouse's guide calls it the most popular day to turn lights on. Service Autopilot's guide says the bulk of orders may not come in until October and November, and Jobber's guide puts the busiest stretch in the two weeks before and after Thanksgiving.

How many houses can one crew hang before Thanksgiving?

It depends on your pace and house sizes. In the worked example, a two-person crew needs about 3.2 hours per house with the drive, so 8-hour days on the 18 weekdays before Thanksgiving fit about 36 houses.

When does takedown start?

After New Year's. Jobber's guide says as early as Jan 2, and Service Autopilot's says removals run into mid-January. In the example, takedown takes about 1.9 hours a house.
