# Copy review: the gate every outreach email passes

Todd, Oct 18, 2026, 4:24 pm: "Ensured emails are independently reviewed as well and are crafted to convert at the highest quality using best practices." This gate binds every outreach file (email/A1-OUTREACH.md, R1-OUTREACH.md, U1-OUTREACH.md and any later one) and every change to one. It sits on top of the outreach run's per-draft checker (coo/LEADS_DESK.md Run 2 step 5), which still checks each filled draft against the template and the week's data.

## Who reviews
A fresh subagent that did not write or edit the copy and has not seen earlier drafts of it. It gets the outreach file, the product's free page and launch record, and that Monday's data file, and it fills the templates itself for two or three realistic prospects before judging. It may research current cold-email and deliverability practice (web search and fetch of primary sources: mailbox-provider sender guidelines, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide, published studies) and cites what it relied on.

## What it scores (1 to 5 each)
1. **Why them, first line:** the reader sees in one line why this email is for their business.
2. **Value before the ask:** the email hands over something usable on its own (a real job, with its builder or business, that fits their area or trade).
3. **True and checkable:** every number, name and claim matches the week's data and the product's written promises; nothing inflated.
4. **Short and easy on a phone:** the body before the footer reads in under 30 seconds; short paragraphs; nothing the reader must decode.
5. **One clear next step:** one link, one action, the price stated plainly, low friction.
6. **Subject line:** specific, honest, plain words, no hype or false familiarity ("Re:", "Quick question"), short enough to read whole on a phone.
7. **Human voice:** sounds like a person who runs a small business; no em dashes, no filler, nothing that reads machine-written.
8. **Deliverability:** plain text, one link, no images, attachments, tracking or shorteners, no spam-trigger phrasing, nothing that invites a spam report.
9. **Compliance:** CAN-SPAM (sender identity, postal address, ad notice, working opt-out honored), no person's name or personal contact, the data source's terms respected (legal/README.md).
10. **Follow-up:** adds something new (a different job, a fresh count) instead of only bumping the first email, and says it is the last.

## The bar
No score under 4 and an average above 4.6 (the same bar as the design director). The reviewer returns a table (item, score, one-line reason), the average, PASS or FAIL, and for anything under 4.5 the exact rewrite it recommends. Fixes are applied, then a fresh reviewer re-checks only what changed. The verdict and date go at the end of the outreach file under "Review verdicts".

## When it runs
- Before a new outreach file is used for the first time.
- After any change to a template's subject or body (the per-draft checker alone does not cover a template change).
- Every 3 outreach weeks per product, together with the reply and payer counts from the CRM, so the copy is judged on results as well as craft.
