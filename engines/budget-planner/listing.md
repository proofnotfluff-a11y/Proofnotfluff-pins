## Description
This budget planner spreadsheet is a plain monthly budget for Excel or Google Sheets: plan your pay, bills, spending and savings once, type what really happened each month, and see what is left to spend.

On the Setup tab, type each paycheck and how often it comes (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, monthly, every 3 months or yearly) and the planner turns it into a monthly amount. Add your bills with their due days, your everyday spending lines and what you save, and Left to plan shows the income that has no job yet. On the Actuals tab, type what came in and what went out under each month. Pick a month on the Month tab and read Left to spend in the dark tile, every line against its plan with Over plan flagged in words, and your bills in due-date order marked Paid or Not paid yet. The Year tab lines up all twelve months against the plan.

Built to fix what buyers of other budget planners complain about:
- Too complicated: four working tabs, one job each, and one answer at the top of the Month tab.
- Formulas that don't work: every total recalculated with no error cells on the example, a blank copy and stress copies with huge, zero and missing amounts.
- Locked sheets that need the owner's permission: the yellow cells are yours, and every tab is protected without a password, so you can unprotect and change anything.
- Instructions only for Google Sheets: the Start Here guide covers Excel, Google Sheets and phones.
- Set-up time and sample data to clear: a blank copy comes with the line names in and every amount empty, beside a filled-in example.

What it does
- Left to spend for any month you pick, and income left after everything out
- Every bill, spending line and savings line against its plan, with what is left and Over plan or Not in the plan in words
- Bills sorted by due day with Paid, Paid, more than planned, or Not paid yet for the month
- Any pay schedule turned into a monthly plan; a month with a third paycheck goes on the Actuals tab as it really came in
- Yearly costs such as car registration planned as a twelfth each month
- Where each dollar of take-home goes: bills, spending, savings and left to plan, with in-cell bars
- The year: planned out, actual out, income and left over for every month, with bars for how far each month landed from the plan

Worked example included: January to September 2026 for a made-up household. January and July hold a third paycheck. January to September: $66,225 in, $56,912 out, $9,313 left over.

FAQ
- Do I need Excel? No. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
- I get paid every 2 weeks. Does it work? Yes. Pick Every 2 weeks on Setup and the plan uses 26 paychecks a year divided by 12. In a month with a third paycheck, type what came in on the Actuals tab.
- Can I rename or add lines? Yes. Rename, clear or fill any yellow line on Setup and the other tabs follow.
- Does it connect to my bank? No. You type the amounts, which keeps your bank details out of it.
