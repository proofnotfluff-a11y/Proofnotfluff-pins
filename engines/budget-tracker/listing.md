## Description
This budget tracker spreadsheet is a spend-first monthly tracker for Excel or Google Sheets: type each purchase, bill and paycheck once, and see what is left to spend this month, category by category.

On the 1 Budget tab, give each category a monthly budget: income, bills, everyday spending and savings, with Left to budget showing income that has no job yet. On the 2 Transactions tab, log one row per money in or money out: date, what it was, a category picked from a dropdown, and the amount. Pick a month on the 3 Month tab and read Left to spend in the dark tile, every category's budget, actual and what is left with Over budget flagged in words, and your five biggest everyday spends. The 4 Year tab lines up every category month by month, with out against the budget for each month.

Built to fix what buyers of other budget trackers complain about:
- Too complicated: one log to type in, one answer at the top of the Month tab, four working tabs with one job each.
- Formulas that don't work: every total recalculated with no error cells on the example, a blank copy and stress copies with huge, zero, minus and missing amounts.
- Locked sheets that need the owner's permission: the yellow cells are yours, and every tab is protected without a password, so you can unprotect and change anything.
- Instructions only for Google Sheets: the Start Here guide covers Excel, Google Sheets and phones.
- Set-up time and sample data to clear: a blank copy comes with the category names in and every amount empty, beside a filled-in example.

What it does
- Left to spend for any month you pick, and money in less money out
- Every category against its monthly budget, with what is left and Over budget, Not budgeted, Paid or Saved in words
- Your five biggest everyday spends of the month, pulled from the log
- Refunds typed as a minus amount in the same category
- A Rows to fix count that catches a row missing a date or category, or using a name not on the list
- Every category month by month for the year, with budget to date and what is left
- Out against the budget for each month, with in-cell bars

Worked example included: July to September 2026 for a made-up household, 99 transactions. Income is budgeted at $4,500.00 a month and bills, everyday spending and savings at $3,965.00, leaving $535.00 to budget. In September $3,797.41 went out, so the Month tab shows $167.59 left to spend and $662.59 of money in less money out, with Eating out at $214.00 against a $200.00 budget marked Over budget. July to September: $13,485 in, $11,575 out, $1,910 left over.
