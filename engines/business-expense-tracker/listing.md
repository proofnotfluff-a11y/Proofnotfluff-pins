## Description
A business expense tracker you can learn in five minutes, for makers, Etsy sellers, freelancers and other one-person businesses that file Schedule C. You type in one tab. The Summary tab fills itself in: income after refunds, inventory and materials, expenses by Schedule C line and profit before the inventory count, for every month and the year.

Built to fix what buyers of bigger trackers complain about:
- Totals that don't calculate: every total reads the whole column, and every tab is protected without a password so a stray keystroke can't overwrite a formula.
- Too many tabs to learn: you type in 1 tab. A Start Here tab and a short Start Here PDF replace the setup video.
- Files that won't open: one zip with plain .xlsx files, plus clear steps for Excel, Google Sheets and phones.

What it does
- 19 business expense categories named after the expense lines on the IRS 2025 Schedule C (Form 1040), checked October 7, 2026, each with a plain-words note on what goes there
- 3 income lines (sales, other income, refunds given) and a separate line for inventory and materials for resale
- Equipment purchases, owner draws and transfers between your own accounts are logged but kept out of profit
- A Checks box that counts rows missing a date or category, or using a category that is not on the list
- Profit by month with in-cell bars, and a "Counts as" column on every entry
- Rename any category and the dropdown and Summary follow
- The year updates on its own; type a year to look back
- 1,000 entry rows

Worked example included: three months of a made-up one-person candle shop. January shows a $165 loss on $858 of sales because of a $413 wax order and $350 studio rent; by March the quarter shows $4,209 of income after refunds and $1,386 of profit before the inventory count.

Depreciation, home office and inventory counts are not calculated; the guide says to hand those to your tax preparer.
