## Description
This cake order form spreadsheet quotes a custom cake from the tier sizes you pick, prints a one-page order summary for the customer and keeps every order, deposit and balance in one log, in Excel or Google Sheets. Made for home bakers and small cake businesses.

Type your prices once on the Price List: price per serving for buttercream and fondant, minimum cake price, deposit, rush fee, delivery base and per-mile fee, sales tax if you charge it, and your extras with their prices. On the Order Form, type the customer and dates, pick the serving style and finish, pick up to four tier sizes and add extras with quantities. Servings, cake price, extras, delivery, rush fee, tax, total, deposit and balance fill in on their own. The Order Log shows what is due in the next 7 days and what is still owed.

Built to fix what buyers of other cake order forms complain about:
- Canva links that confuse or don't open: this is the workbook itself, one .xlsx download, no Canva, no link to redeem, no account.
- Hard to edit and slow to figure out: every choice is a drop-down (sizes, finish, extras, pickup or delivery), a filled-in example file shows it working, and the Start Here tab walks you through three steps.
- Blank cells that throw errors into the price: every formula is blank-safe, so an empty tier, extra or date gives a blank or zero, never an error.
- Cups only, no millilitres: the Price List carries a cups-to-millilitres card for US cups, tablespoons and teaspoons, with a cell for your own amount.

What it does
- Servings per tier from the built-in size table: 24 sizes (round, square, sheet and heart) with party and wedding serving counts from Wilton's serving guide, every number editable
- Cake price from servings times your price per serving, with your minimum cake price applied to small cakes
- Up to 6 extras per order picked from your own price list, priced each, per tier, per cake or per dozen
- Delivery from a base fee plus miles, a rush fee when the event is inside your rush window, sales tax at your rate
- Deposit to book and balance due, in the tiles at the top and on the printable order summary
- Order Log with balance, days to go and a plain-words status for every order (Booked, Due this week, Past date, Paid in full, Done)
- Checks that flag a size or extra typed twice and log rows missing a customer, date or total

Worked example included: a made-up home bakery's three-tier wedding cake, 10 in, 8 in and 6 in round in fondant. 74 wedding servings at $6.00 a serving is $444.00; 12 sugar flowers, fresh flowers arranged and a box and board add $98.00; delivery 18 miles one way is $28.00; the event is 36 days out, so no rush fee. Total $570.00, deposit $285.00 to book, balance $285.00 on the event date. The example log holds 8 orders: 2 due in the next 7 days, $852.00 of deposits received, $723.00 still owed.
