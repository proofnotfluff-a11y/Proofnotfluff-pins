## Description
This client tracker spreadsheet shows a one or two person service business who to follow up today, what is quoted and unpaid, and which clients bring the revenue, in Excel or Google Sheets.

List each client once on the Clients tab: name, type (Lead, Active or Past), where they came from, first contact and the next date to get in touch. Log every job and quote on the Jobs tab: date, client and service picked from lists, amount, status (Quoted, Booked, Done or Lost) and whether it is paid. Type the date on the Today tab and read the rest: follow-ups due with the days overdue and the service each client last asked about, open quotes by value, this month's jobs done, revenue, unpaid balance and new leads, top clients by revenue and where your clients come from.

Built to fix what buyers of other client trackers complain about:
- A pipeline to set up before it is useful: there is none. Two lists feed one dashboard, and the first thing you see is who to call.
- Follow-ups buried in a table: the Today tab opens on the number of follow-ups due, most overdue first, with a flag in words on every client row (Due today, Overdue 3 days, Not yet).
- Formulas lost when the sample data is cleared: formulas live only in locked white columns, so you can select the yellow example cells and press Delete. A blank copy is included too.
- Google Sheets only, or a file that breaks in Excel: one plain .xlsx, made for Excel 2016 and later, that uploads to Google Sheets as it is. No link to a web app, no sign-up.
- A dashboard that reads the clock and shifts overnight: you type the as-of date, so the file shows the same numbers on any day and in any app.

What it does
- Follow-ups due on the date you type, most overdue first, with the service each client last asked about
- Open quotes ranked by value, with the client and the service
- This month's jobs done, revenue, unpaid balance and new leads, plus the same figures all time
- Top clients by revenue with in-cell bars, and a count of clients by source (Referral, Google, Facebook, Nextdoor, Yard sign, Repeat, Other)
- Per client: jobs done, revenue to date, unpaid, last job, last asked about, days to next contact and the follow-up flag
- Per job: your usual price for the service beside what you quoted, the unpaid amount, and a check that names what is missing (a date, a client not on the list, a Done job with no paid answer)
- A Services tab with your standard prices and typical hours that feeds the Jobs dropdown
