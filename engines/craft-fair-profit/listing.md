## Description
A craft fair profit calculator for makers who sell at fairs, holiday markets and pop-ups: it adds up what a show day really costs, works out what each piece earns, and tells you how many pieces you must sell to break even before you pay the booth fee.

The booth fee is usually less than half of the day. In the example inside, a $150 booth costs $315.60 once organizer extras, 60 miles at 76 cents, parking, meals, a share of the tent and display, and other costs are counted. Five products blend to $6.24 of contribution a piece after materials, packaging, your making time and the card fee, so the show takes 51 pieces, $705.84 of sales, to break even. The 3x rule says $450 is a good day; this show needs $706 just to cover its costs.

WHAT IT DOES
- 1 Show Costs: every cost of the day in one total, with bars that show where the money goes, plus your card reader's fee as a cost per $1 of sales
- 2 Products: up to 15 products with materials, minutes to make, packaging, price and sales mix; labor, unit cost, card cost, contribution, margin and a (materials + labor) x 2 price check for each
- 3 Break-Even: pieces to sell, sales needed, pieces per hour, the 3x rule check, pieces for the profit you want, how many to bring, and a what-if table for six booth fees
- 4 Show Log: profit, profit per hour and a 3x check for every show this season

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Craft-Fair-Profit-Calculator.xlsx with 4 working tabs (1 Show Costs, 2 Products, 3 Break-Even, 4 Show Log) plus Start Here and Terms

WORKS IN
Works in Microsoft Excel for Windows and Mac, Google Sheets (upload to Google Drive, open, then File, Save as Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros, no add-ons, no sign-up. The example show and products are invented; type over them with your own.

HOW YOU GET IT

FAQ
Does it handle sales tax? It treats every price as pre-tax and shows the pre-tax part of a tax-included price. It does not tell you what your state requires.
Can I change the card fee? Yes. Type your own reader's percent and flat fee; the default is Square's in-person 2.6% + 15 cents.
How many products? Up to 15, blended by the share of pieces each one sells.

Figures: IRS business standard mileage rate 76 cents a mile for July 1 to December 31, 2026 (IRS Standard Mileage Rates page, IR-2026-29, checked Oct 6, 2026); Square in-person fee 2.6% + 15 cents (squareup.com/us/en/fees, read Oct 1, 2026); booth fee ranges and the 3x rule from The Craft Map's Craft Fair Booth Fees Guide (Feb 13, 2026).
