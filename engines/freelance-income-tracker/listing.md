## Description
A freelance income tracker for Excel and Google Sheets: type one row each time you send an invoice or get paid, and the Dashboard shows what came in this year, month by month, what clients still owe you, your top clients and how much to set aside at a rate you choose. Made for freelancers, contractors and side hustles who found full bookkeeping templates too much.

WHAT IT DOES
- One entry tab with six columns: date, client, what it was for, amount, method, paid on. Leave Paid on blank until the money arrives.
- Income counts in the month the money arrived, so a December invoice paid in January lands in January.
- Still owed: every unpaid row lists itself on the Dashboard with how many days it has waited. Rows older than the days you choose show as Late.
- Set aside at your own rate, split into the four IRS estimated tax payment periods with each due date (IRS, checked Oct 6, 2026). The file does not tell you what rate to use.
- Top clients and each client's share of your year.
- Monthly goal: your average month against the goal you type, and the months that reached it.

BUILT TO FIX WHAT BUYERS OF OTHER TRACKERS COMPLAIN ABOUT
- Totals that stop calculating: every total reads all 300 log rows, and a Rows to fix check counts any row with an amount but no client or date, or a client that is not on your list.
- Too complex: one entry tab, four settings, no setup steps.
- Locked or hard to change: client and payment-method lists are yours to edit, and sheets are protected without a password so you can unprotect any time.
- Video-only help: a written Start Here tab and a Start Here PDF.
- Dates that change: date columns accept only real dates and show the month by name (Oct 1, 2026), so you can check each one at a glance.

FAQ
Does it do expenses or profit? No, it tracks income only. Pair it with an expense tracker to see profit.
Does it calculate my tax? No. It multiplies income received by the set-aside rate you type. For your own rate, ask a tax professional.
How many rows? 300 in the log, 25 clients and 8 payment methods.
