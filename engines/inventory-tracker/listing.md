## Description
This inventory tracker spreadsheet keeps stock on hand, reorder alerts, unit cost and profit per unit for a small product business (makers, resellers and small shops), in Excel or Google Sheets.

List each item once on the Items tab: SKU, materials, labor and packaging cost per unit, selling price, your fee rate, starting stock and the reorder point you choose. Log every stock move on the Stock Log tab: made or received, sold, customer return, damaged or lost, and count fixes. The workbook keeps units on hand for every item, flags what to reorder, and the Dashboard shows the stock value at cost, sales and profit for any dates you pick, the reorder list, top sellers, best profit per unit and the items that tie up the most cash.

Built to fix what buyers of other inventory spreadsheets complain about:
- Formulas lost when the sample data is cleared: formulas live only in locked white columns, so you can select the yellow example cells and press Delete. A blank copy is included too.
- Too basic, with no cost or profit per unit: unit cost is split into materials, labor and packaging, and profit per unit comes after your fee rate.
- Google Sheets only, or a file that breaks in Excel: one plain .xlsx, made for Excel and checked in Google Sheets by Drive upload.
- New rows that the dashboard ignores: every total reads all 150 item rows and all 2,000 stock log rows, and a notes column on each tab holds your own details.

What it does
- Units on hand per item from starting stock plus every logged move
- Reorder now, Out of stock or OK status at the reorder point you set for each item
- Unit cost (materials + labor + packaging and other) and profit per unit after fees
- A different price on one sale, such as a wholesale order, goes on that log row and the profit uses it
- Sales value and profit from sales for every row, or for a From and To date you pick
- Top sellers, best profit per unit and most money in stock, each with in-cell bars
- Checks that count item rows and log rows missing a SKU, a date, a type or a quantity

Worked example included: July to September 2026 of a made-up candle and soap maker with 10 items. The lavender candle costs $7.50 a unit to make ($4.20 materials, $2.00 labor, $1.30 packaging) and sells for $24.00, so after 10% fees it makes $14.10 a unit. Across all 10 items the shop holds $824.60 of stock at cost, sold 504 units for $7,262.00 and $4,023.60 of profit, and 5 items are at or under their reorder point. September alone shows $1,494.50 of profit from 186 units.
