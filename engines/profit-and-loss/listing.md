## Description
This profit and loss template turns one simple log of money in and money out into a monthly and year-to-date P&L statement for a small business, exact to the cent, in Excel or Google Sheets.

You type one row per sale or expense on the Entries tab. The Statement tab builds a full profit and loss statement for the month you pick and for January to that month: net revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses by line, net profit, and gross and net margin. The Month by Month tab shows the whole year side by side.

Built to fix what buyers of other P&L templates complain about:
- Files that won't open on a Mac, iPad or phone: one zip with plain .xlsx files and clear steps for Excel, Excel for Mac and iPad, and Google Sheets.
- PDF forms you can't add to: this is a live log with 1,000 rows, and every total updates as you type.
- Reports that hide the exact numbers: every total shows dollars and cents.
- Formulas that break in Excel set to another language: no text-format codes in any formula.
- Instructions you must read before it makes sense: a filled-in example file and a short Start Here guide.

What it does
- Statement for any month and the year to date, with % of revenue on every line; prints on one page
- 22 cost and expense lines named after the IRS 2025 Schedule C (Form 1040) lines, checked October 8, 2026, each with a plain-words note on what goes there
- Optional stock counts (start of year and end of the month shown) turn the year-to-date cost of goods sold into stock at the start plus purchases less stock at the end, the same way Schedule C Part III works it out
- Equipment, owner draws and transfers between your own accounts are logged but kept out of profit
- A Checks box that counts rows missing a date or category, or using a category that is not on the list
- Net profit by month with in-cell bars, best month and loss months
- Rename any category and the dropdown, the Statement and the month table follow

Worked example included: January to September of a made-up one-person T-shirt printing shop. September shows $3,325.00 of net revenue and $513.01 of net profit, down from $1,361.91 in August. January to September shows $29,240.00 of net revenue, $10,750.00 of cost of goods sold after two stock counts, $18,490.00 of gross profit and $5,814.34 of net profit, a 19.9% net margin.

FAQ
- Do I need Excel? No. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
- Can I use it for last year? Yes. Type the year at the top of the Statement tab.
- Do I need to count my stock? No. Without counts, cost of goods is what you bought for the products in that period.
- Is this tax advice? No. The categories help you organize; your tax preparer decides the final line for each item.
