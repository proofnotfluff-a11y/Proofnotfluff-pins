## Description
This savings tracker spreadsheet tracks savings goals and sinking funds in Excel or Google Sheets: type each deposit once and see, for every goal, what is saved, what is left, and whether your pace gets you there.

On the 1 Goals tab, list up to 20 goals and sinking funds with a target, an optional target date and anything already saved. On the 2 Deposits tab, log one row each time money goes in: date, a goal picked from a dropdown, and the amount (money taken out is a minus amount). The 3 Progress tab shows saved so far across all goals in the dark tile, then every goal with saved, left, percent done, an in-cell bar and its plan in words. A goal with a target date shows the amount it needs each month and On track or Behind. A goal with no date shows the month it is done at your current pace, so no goal is forced to have an end date.

Built to fix what buyers of other savings trackers complain about:
- Balances and percents that fail in the Google Sheets phone app: every formula is a plain sum, count or date with no scripts, charts or add-ons.
- Google Sheets only: one .xlsx made for Excel that opens in Google Sheets too, with a Start Here guide for both.
- Goals forced to have an end date: the target date is optional on every goal.
- Not enough instructions, and formulas that break: a Start Here tab and PDF with three steps and a worked example, each yellow cell explains itself when selected, and every tab is protected without a password so stray typing can't break a formula.

What it does
- Saved, left and percent done for up to 20 goals and sinking funds
- The monthly amount a dated goal needs, with On track or Behind in words
- The finish month at your pace for a goal with no date
- Saved this month, taken out this month, and how much your dated goals need a month
- The last six months of saving, month by month, with in-cell bars
- An as of date: blank shows today, or type a date to look back
- A Rows to fix count that catches a deposit missing a date or goal, or using a name not on the list

Worked example included: six made-up goals and 30 deposits from May to October 2026, as of Oct 15, 2026. $5,990 is saved of $12,220 in targets, with $6,230 still to save. The emergency fund has no date: $3,000 saved at $250 a month shows done Oct 2027. Holiday gifts needs $150 a month to reach $800 by December but saves $100, so it shows Behind. The summer trip needs $175 a month and saves $250, so it is On track. October's deposits come to $945.00.

FAQ
- Do I need Excel? No. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
- Can I use it on my phone? Yes, in the Google Sheets app once the file is in your Google Drive. Unzip on a computer first; phones often can't open a .zip.
- Does every goal need a date? No. Leave the date blank and the tracker shows the month you finish at your pace instead.
- Does it connect to my bank? No. You type the amounts, which keeps your bank details out of it.
