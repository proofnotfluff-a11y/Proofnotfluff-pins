## Description
A junk removal pricing calculator that tells you what every single item, truck load, cleanout and tear-down has to cost so it pays you, for owner-operators and small hauling crews.

A half-load price copied from another hauler misses the hours nobody pays for (driving, estimates, slow weeks), the dump fees, the second person on the truck and self-employment tax. In the example inside, pay divided by all hours says $25.00 an hour. The real break-even is $62.36, and a half truck load quoted at $350 falls $59.62 short of covering your pay and costs. The calculator quotes it at $485.

WHAT IT DOES
- Finds your break-even and target rate per paid hour, with unpaid hours, monthly costs and self-employment tax included
- Quotes any job from a dropdown: adjust hours, crew size, dump fees and miles for this customer, and get a price rounded up to the next $5, the deposit to collect and the profit inside it
- Counts drive time for every person on the truck and the vehicle cost per mile, dump runs included
- Prices all 19 example jobs at once on a printable price sheet
- Tracks finished jobs and flags the ones below your floor

FAQ
Are the job prices or dump fees market rates? No. The example times, crew sizes and dump fees are starting points. Change them to your real jobs and your local dump's rates and every price follows.
I run a box truck. Is 76 cents a mile right for me? Maybe not. The IRS rate is set for cars, vans, pickups and panel trucks, and a box truck usually costs more per mile. Type your truck's real cost per mile (fuel, tires, repairs, insurance) in Vehicle cost per mile and every quote uses it.
