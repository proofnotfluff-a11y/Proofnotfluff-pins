## Description
A pressure washing pricing calculator that tells you what every driveway, house wash, roof, deck and storefront job has to cost so it pays you, for solo operators and small crews.

Most owners price a driveway by what the last company charged. That misses the hours nobody pays for (driving, quoting, rain days), the equipment that wears out, and self-employment tax. In the example inside, pay divided by all hours says $27.78 an hour. The real break-even is $64.89, and a 2-car driveway quoted at $100 falls $67.80 short of covering your pay and costs. The calculator quotes it at $200.

WHAT IT DOES
- Finds your break-even and target rate per paid hour, with unpaid hours, monthly costs and self-employment tax included
- Quotes any job from a dropdown: adjust hours, crew size, supplies and miles for this customer, and get a price rounded up to the next $5, the deposit to collect and the profit inside it
- Adds drive time and vehicle cost to every quote
- Prices all 15 example jobs at once on a printable price sheet
- Tracks finished jobs and flags the ones below your floor

FAQ
Are the job prices market rates? No. The example times and supplies are starting points. Change them to your real jobs and every price follows.
Does it do square-foot bids? It prices by time, crew, supplies and travel. For square-foot pricing, enter the hours a job of that size takes you.
