# Description
Founding price for the first 10 buyers, then $14.

Most service owners set prices by dividing the pay they want by the hours they work. That misses three things: the hours you are not paid for (driving, quoting, invoicing), the business costs that keep running every month, and self-employment tax. In the example inside, that guess says $24 an hour. The real break-even is $54.80.

This calculator is built for time-based service work, not products. Set it up once in about 10 minutes, then price any job in seconds.

WHAT IT DOES
- Finds your break-even and target rate per paid hour, with unpaid hours, monthly costs and self-employment tax included
- Quotes any job: pick it from a dropdown, adjust for this customer, and get a price rounded up to the next $5, the deposit to collect and the profit inside it
- Adds drive time and vehicle cost to every quote, the two costs most quotes leave out
- Prices every job type at once on a printable price sheet
- Tracks finished jobs and flags the ones below your floor
- Checks your current price: enter it and see if it covers your pay goal and costs
