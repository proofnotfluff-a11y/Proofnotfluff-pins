## Description (v3 wording; the shop run applies only the phrase swaps queued in scoreboard legal/7)

Shipping cost calculator spreadsheet for Etsy, eBay and online sellers who ship in the US: see what one parcel really costs, with the box, filler, tape, label and your packing time added to the postage, and whether the shipping you charge covers it.

What's inside (5 working tabs, plus Start Here and Terms):
- 1 Parcel Costs: one row per product, up to 30. True cost per parcel, shortfall against the shipping you charge, and a check on every row.
- 2 Dim Weight: box size and packed weight in, the weight USPS, UPS and FedEx bill out (USPS divisor 139 above one cubic foot; UPS and FedEx 139 on every box).
- 3 Free Shipping: test free shipping over three thresholds against your own order history; verdict and recommendation per 100 orders.
- 4 Services: every product priced on USPS Ground Advantage (commercial and retail), Priority Mail commercial and UPS Ground Saver at its zone and billable weight, cheapest first, plus two columns for your own quotes. A switch adds the USPS temporary holiday prices (Oct 4, 2026 to Jan 17, 2027).
- 5 Dashboard: net profit per order after marketplace fees, product cost and the full parcel cost, with Losing money and Thin flags.

The example: a 10-product sample shop. The mug charges $4.95 shipping on a parcel that costs $13.19 to send; shipping one of each product costs $139.51 and brings in $93.60.

Formats: one .xlsx built for Excel and Google Sheets (upload to Google Drive, then File, Save as Google Sheets), checked in LibreOffice. No macros, no add-ons, no sign-up. It doesn't print labels, buy postage or connect to any carrier.

How you get it: Instant download. Download from a web browser on your computer or phone (Etsy, You, Purchases); the Etsy app can't download digital files.

FAQ
