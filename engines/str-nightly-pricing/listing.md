# Description
A nightly rate spreadsheet for Airbnb and VRBO hosts: it turns your last 12 months (or a starter curve for your market) into a rate for every month, prices local events, checks every weekly and monthly discount against a floor you set, and shows the rate that keeps your payout whole under Airbnb's single 15.5% host fee.

Most hosts set one nightly rate, then stack discounts on top. In the example inside, a 10% weekly discount on a quiet month would bill $103.50 a night against a $115 floor; the worksheet flags it and charges the floor instead. The same example cabin needs $213, not $185, to keep its old payout after the fee change.

WHAT IT DOES
- A nightly rate for every month from your own history, or one of five starter curves (beach, mountain, urban, lake, rural)
- Event premiums priced per window, with the uplift if those nights book
- Weekly (7+ nights) and monthly (28+ nights) discounts checked against your floor, with every breach flagged
- Host fee reset: the rate that keeps your old payout, and what changing nothing costs per night and per year
- One rate table to copy into your listing calendar

WHAT'S INSIDE
- STR-Nightly-Pricing-Worksheet.xlsx with 6 working tabs: 1 Your Property, 2 Seasonality, 3 Events, 4 Stay Discounts, 5 Fee Reset, 6 Rate Table, plus Start Here and Terms
- Start Here guide as a PDF in US Letter and A4
- LICENSE-AND-DISCLAIMER.txt

TESTED COMPATIBILITY
Works in Microsoft Excel for Windows and Mac, Google Sheets (upload to Google Drive, open, then File, Save as Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros, no add-ons, no sign-up. Example numbers are included; replace them with your own.

HOW YOU GET IT

FAQ
Does it pull live market prices? No. It prices the nights you decide to sell from your own numbers. No live data and no sync with Airbnb or VRBO.
