## Description (paste as plain paragraphs; no links)
If something happened to you tomorrow, could your family find the will, the passwords, the insurance policies and the person to call first? Most families can't. This binder fixes that in a few evenings, by hand, one page at a time.

Everything They'll Need is a 42-page organizer you write in. It is not a will and not a legal document. It is the map your family would need on the hardest week of their lives: who to call, where things are, what you want, and what to do next.

WHAT'S INSIDE
Start here: the one page your family reads first, with the people to call and where the important things are.
About me and my people: the facts the hospital, funeral home, bank and court will ask for, your family, and your key contacts (executor, agents, attorney, doctor).
Health: medical information, medications, and where your directive, health care proxy and HIPAA release are kept, plus what matters to you.
Important documents: a checklist of where every original is, and the four documents most people need, explained in plain words so you know what to ask an attorney about.
Money: accounts, retirement and investments, a beneficiary check-up with the four common mistakes that send money to the wrong person, insurance, debts, bills and autopays, taxes and business, and the money families most often miss.
Home and property: the house, utilities, vehicles and the things that matter.
Digital life: getting into devices, password manager emergency access, legacy settings and online accounts.
People and pets who count on me: children, dependents and two pet cards.
Final wishes: what you'd like, your obituary facts, and the people to tell.
In my own words: two pages for a letter.
For my family: step lists for the first 72 hours, the first month and the first year, a calls and claims log, and the estate details page.
Keeping it current: a yearly review, an update log, a wallet card and a refrigerator door note for emergency responders.

WHY THIS ONE
Every page is spaced for handwriting, with a Page updated line in the footer so your family can see how current it is.
It tells you to write where things are, not the secrets themselves: last 4 digits and a location, never full passwords or account numbers.
Every rule and figure in the book names its agency source and the date it was checked.
Written by a finance executive, built for families.

Made with AI assistance and reviewed and tested by the shop owner.
Information and planning tool only, not legal, tax, financial or other professional advice. Results are estimates based on your inputs.
This is an organizer, not a legal document. It is not a will, trust, power of attorney, health care directive or beneficiary form, and filling it in has no legal effect.
