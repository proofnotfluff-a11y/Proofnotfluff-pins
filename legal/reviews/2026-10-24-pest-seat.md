### The "at most 3 at a time" promise and Gumroad
- Gumroad's own help pages read today (articles 82, 192, 139, 278) render their text in the browser; only their summaries came through. Article 192: a customer cancels "From your receipt" or "From your library (if you have a Gumroad account)". Article 139: the creator cancels from the Sales dashboard, "Cancel Subscription". A guide built from Gumroad's open-source code (mintlify.wiki/antiwork/gumroad/features/subscriptions, a secondary source) says a cancelled member keeps access until the period ends by default, with an optional setting to remove access at once, and a failed payment keeps access for a 5-day grace period. Nothing read today shows a Gumroad setting that caps a membership tier at 3 members.
- FIX 8 (medium), so the cap stays true:
  1. Todd checks the seat product's tier settings for a member or sales limit and sets it to 3 if Gumroad offers one. If it does not, the seat button comes off the page (site/pages.json seat.taken = 3) the moment a third seat sells, and a fourth buyer who gets through is refunded in full the same day and never gets the password.

  3. One shared password is how the page is locked, so a company that leaves still knows it. When a seat ends (paid month over, or refund), the password changes before the seat is sold again, and the remaining members get the new one through the Gumroad content.
