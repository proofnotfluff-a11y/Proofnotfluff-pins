# E-commerce brands narrowly beat hosts and trades

ProofNotFluff should build its next products for small US e-commerce and consumer brands. On one weighted rubric they score **31 of 40**, against **28 for short-term rental (STR) hosts** and **27 for home-service businesses**; the raw, unweighted scores are 23, 21 and 20 of 30. This is a close call, not a landslide. E-commerce wins on money and file fit: Amazon's all-in cut alone runs **39% to 60% of revenue** ([Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/amazon-all-in-take-rate)), and more of these owners' pains are pure spreadsheet problems. Free reach counts twice and scored 3 in every niche on thin data, so two opposite one-point moves there would hand the win to STR hosts. The first three products form one ladder: a free payout gap calculator feeding the **$79 13-Week E-commerce Cash Kit**, which is already built; a free PO cash calculator feeding a **$49 SKU buy plan** ($99 with the kit); and a free channel profit calculator feeding a **$39 margin and price ladder workbook**, all sold from proofnotfluff.com through Gumroad. The risk all three niches share is bigger than the gap between them: the strongest visible sales signal for any finance spreadsheet built for these three audiences was a shop with 25 sales in six months. The next 30 days of page visits and kit sales should confirm or flip this call.

## Conclusion

The scan turns a niche question into a proof question. All three niches have frequent, costly pain and the same crowding shape, so the niche call is close. What separates any winner from the zero-sale shops in these scans is reach and visible proof, not the audience. E-commerce is the better first bet for one more reason the rubric does not capture: each sale is worth more. One $79 kit equals two to four sales of the $19 to $39 files that suit hosts and trades, so the e-commerce ladder needs far fewer buyers to prove itself on a site with no traffic history.

The rule changes that create demand also make files go stale. DD+7, TikTok's reported fee rise, three tariff regimes, Airbnb's 15.5% and Vrbo's 12% all landed in about 14 months. A dated, verified fee table is both the brand's proof and its largest upkeep cost. Two cheap moves follow. Schedule a monthly fee and tariff check before adding more products. And leave the live STR and trade pages as they are, as the comparison group that will confirm or flip this call within a month.
