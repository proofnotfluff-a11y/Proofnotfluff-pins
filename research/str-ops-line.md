## 1. Who cleans and how long

- Flat rate per turnover by bedroom count beats hourly (2 experienced hosts agree; ranges they gave: studio/1BR $60 to $85, 2BR $100 to $140, 3BR+ $140 to $200+). https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Help-with-your-business/Cleaning-Service-Questions/m-p/2178499
- Vet with references, insurance and a paid trial clean inspected like a real turn (vendor: Hostfully). https://www.hostfully.com/blog/how-to-find-airbnb-cleaners/
- Clean alongside a new cleaner once to learn real timing (a few hosts). https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Community-cafe/Paying-for-a-Cleaner-Cleaning-Service/td-p/1595465
- Bench of three: one primary (about 80% of turns) plus two backups; confirm 24 hours ahead; "starting now" text on arrival; give exact times ("checkout 10, guests 3, start by 10:30") (vendor: Properly). https://getproperly.com/blog/airbnb-cleaner-no-show-what-to-do
- Timing by size (one operator's labor guide): 1 to 3BR 2 to 3 hours with 1 to 2 cleaners; 4 to 5BR 3 to 4 hours with 2 cleaners; 6 to 8BR 4 to 5 hours with 2 to 3. https://redawning.com/pm/post/airbnb-cleaner-kissimmee
- Keep at least 4 hours between checkout and check-in (vendor: Hostfully). https://www.hostfully.com/blog/vacation-rental-turnover-cleaning/
- Linen: 3 sets per bed (on the bed, in the wash, spare); 3 hosts agree. https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Ask-about-your-listing/Linens/m-p/1789755
- Hot tub every turn: clean, test, balance (top-voted BiggerPockets reply; 15 to 30 minutes; one host pays cleaners $25 a turn for it); drain and refill every 2 to 3 months in that thread. https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/530/topics/1263435-airbnb-hot-tub-headaches
- Bad clean: reply to the guest fast, offer a re-clean, never blame the cleaner to the guest, check whether the checklist or the time allowed was the real problem before firing anyone (vendor: Properly). https://getproperly.com/blog/guest-complained-about-cleaning
- Do NOT use: Guesty's "40% of hosts had a no-show" (no source given).

## 2. What cleaners miss (checklist lines)

1. Hair: drain, shower walls, pillows; lint-roll sheets (most repeated). https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Help/5-star-cleanliness-expectations-amp-HAIR/m-p/1166250
2. Under every bed, inside every drawer. https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/clean-like-a-pro-with-these-expert-tips-663
3. Coffee maker, toaster, microwave: grounds and crumbs (same Airbnb page).
4. Fridge emptied of guest food, shelves wiped. https://www.houfy.com/blog/vacation-rental-turnover-cleaning-checklist-2026
5. Ceiling fan blades; 6. baseboards (Turno's top overlooked spots). https://turno.com/blog/top-5-most-overlooked-places-by-airbnb-cleaners/
7. Knobs, switches, remotes, thermostat (4 sources). https://www.guesty.com/vacation-rental-guide/cleaning-vacation-rentals/
8. Smoke and CO alarms tested. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/514
9. New lock code for the next reservation. https://www.airbnb.ca/help/article/3478
10. Thermostat preset; 11. grill grates and propane. https://www.houfy.com/blog/vacation-rental-turnover-cleaning-checklist-2026
12. Trash to the curb when pickup falls before the next checkout (cleaner owns it; guests are hit or miss). https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Help/What-do-you-do-about-garbage-collection/m-p/540531
13. Photograph damage on arrival, before cleaning. https://www.airbnb.com/help/question/264
14. Hot tub: free chlorine at least 3 ppm or bromine at least 4 ppm, pH 7.2 to 7.8, logged each turn (CDC; one CDC page says pH 7.0 to 7.8, so print 7.2 to 7.8). https://www.cdc.gov/healthywater/swimming/materials/infographic-inspection.html and https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-swimming/about/home-pool-and-hot-tub-water-treatment-and-testing.html. Drain interval formula (gallons / 3 / average bathers per day) from Jacuzzi: https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/SIGNS-ITS-TIME-TO-DRAIN-AND-REFILL.html
15. Consumables counted against par; an opened backup bin triggers a reorder (2-bin system). https://strspecialist.com/airbnb-inventory-that-never-runs-out-par-levels-bins-and-reorder-rules
- Moved or missing decor (hair dryers) slips past even good cleaners. https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Community-cafe/missing-messed-up-household-items/m-p/1549076
- Sauna: wipe benches each turn, mild cleaners only, deep clean every 2 to 3 months (single manufacturer source). https://peaksaunas.com/blogs/news/how-to-clean-a-sauna
- Par examples: 3 bath towels per guest, 3 toilet rolls per bathroom per stay, 10+ hangers per closet. https://strspecialist.com/airbnb-inventory-that-never-runs-out-par-levels-bins-and-reorder-rules
- Do NOT claim "cleanliness is the #1 cause of bad reviews" as a statistic: no source has data.

## 3. Guest messages and timing

| # | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Booking confirmed, right away | Thanks, tone, what comes next |
| 2 | 3 to 7 days out | Guidebook, house rules, directions, parking |
| 3 | 24 to 48 hours out | Full check-in details (Airbnb itself releases check-in details and codes 48 hours before: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/41) |
| 4 | Morning of arrival | Short, code first |
| 5 | Mid-stay, stays of 3+ nights only, once | Catch problems early (hosts split on this) |
| 6 | Evening before checkout | Checkout time and a short task list |
| 7 | 1 to 3 days after checkout | Thanks and review request |

Sources: https://airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/simplifying-communication-650, https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-guest-messaging-templates/, https://hospitable.com/pre-check-in-message-examples, mid-stay debate https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/How-often-do-you-message-guests/m-p/1385196

Airbnb rules to respect:
- Scheduled messages can skip last-minute bookings unless the override is on. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2897
- Response rate is inquiries answered within 24 hours over 30 days. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/430 ; Superhost: 90% response rate, 4.8+ rating, under 1% cancellations, reviewed quarterly. https://www.airbnb.com/superhost
- No contact info or off-platform payment before booking. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2799
- No reviews in exchange for anything of value, no pressure on reviews. https://www.airbnb.com/support/article/548?topic=253 (so never write "I'll leave you a great review too")
- Review window 14 days after checkout. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/995
- Checkout tasks: keep them light (trash, lights, lock up); hosts agree laundry and vacuuming cross the line; Airbnb already sends a reminder the day before. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1926 , https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3501 , https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Ask-about-your-listing/Unreasonable-Tasks-for-Guests/m-p/1739644
- Quiet hours, parking and trash restated before arrival. https://www.minut.com/blog/airbnb-message-templates
- Early check-in: decide the day before; free when the night before was empty, a fee when it adds cleaning cost, bag drop otherwise. https://hello.pricelabs.co/a-how-to-guide-on-dealing-with-early-check-in-requests/

## 4. Damage: from discovery to payment

1. Document before repair or disposal: photos or video, estimates, receipts. https://www.airbnb.com/help/question/264
2. Ask the guest through the Resolution Center within 14 days of checkout; the guest has 24 hours to respond; then escalate under Host damage protection. https://www.airbnb.com/help/question/264
3. Airbnb terms (updated Aug 1, 2026): pursue the guest and notify Airbnb within 14 days of checkout; file the payment request form within 30 days; evidence must not be altered, including with AI; claims can be denied for lack of reasonable care. https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2869
4. Not covered: wear and tear, normal checkout cleaning (laundry, dishes, trash), currency, acts of nature, mold or mildew, consumables. Covered: damage up to $3M, art and valuables, pet damage, deep cleaning for stains and smoke, lost income. https://www.airbnb.com/aircover-for-hosts
5. There is NO Airbnb rule requiring a report before the next guest checks in (a vendor blog says so; the terms do not). Present it as best practice only.
- Vrbo: refundable deposit held 7 or 14 days, the claim window matches the hold. https://help.vrbo.com/articles/Does-HomeAway-Payments-automatically-issue-refunds-to-travelers ; card-on-file damage claims within 14 days. https://help.vrbo.com/articles/What-happens-if-my-card-on-file-is-charged-for-property-damage
- Direct: card holds drop in 1 to 7 days, place them near arrival. https://support.lodgix.com/en/articles/3323762-pre-authorizations-authorization-holds ; Superhog via Hospitable Direct Premium: claims within 10 days, $70 deductible, timestamped before photos. https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/7879266-direct-premium-damage-protection
- What gets claims paid (forums): timestamped before and after photos every turn (most repeated), original receipts, facts not accusations; why they fail: called wear and tear, filed late, no before proof. https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Host-guides/Airbnb-Damage-Compensation-Guide-Be-Confident-You-Are-Protected/m-p/2242841 , https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Help/Airbnb-host-guarantee-a-joke/m-p/1066341 , https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Community-cafe/Issues-with-Airbnb-Resolutions-Center/m-p/1604924

## Product spec (draft)

STR Turnover System, Excel + Google Sheets + printable PDF:
- Cleaner checklist by room with the 15 commonly-missed lines, hot tub and sauna steps, photo checkpoints, and a "report damage before cleaning" step.
- Turnover timing planner: checkout and check-in times, bedrooms, cleaners, hot tub yes/no gives a start-by time and a go/no-go for same-day turns.
- Guest message scripts for the 7-step schedule, with Airbnb-safe review request wording.
- Damage claim log: per incident, the checkout date auto-calculates the deadline for each platform (Airbnb 14 and 30 days, Vrbo 7 or 14, Superhog 10), with an evidence checklist and a wear-and-tear note field.
- Restock sheet with par levels and the 2-bin reorder trigger.
- Cleaner bench sheet: primary plus two backups, rates, confirm-24-hours log.
