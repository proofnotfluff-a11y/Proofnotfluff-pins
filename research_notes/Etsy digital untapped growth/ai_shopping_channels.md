# AI Shopping and Answer Engine Channels for Etsy Sellers (status as of Oct 6, 2026)

Scope: how Etsy listings (especially digital downloads) get surfaced and sold through ChatGPT, Google AI Mode/Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity; which listing attributes matter; GEO evidence for a small free-calculator website; documented small-seller examples. Research done Oct 6, 2026. Labels used: [PRIMARY] = company's own blog/help/transcript; [PRESS] = trade/news reporting; [VENDOR] = SEO/AI-visibility vendor content (treat as claims); [BACKGROUND] = older than 2025.

## 1. OpenAI ChatGPT: Instant Checkout, ACP, and the Etsy app in ChatGPT

### Takeaway
ChatGPT Instant Checkout (launched Sept 29, 2025 with Etsy as first partner) was wound down around March 2026 after weak sales volume; OpenAI pivoted to product discovery with merchants' own checkout, and Etsy replaced it on May 5, 2026 with a beta "Etsy app in ChatGPT" (tag @Etsy) that sends shoppers to Etsy.com to buy. Individual Etsy sellers cannot submit their own feed to OpenAI; Etsy controls what is syndicated, and at launch inclusion was tied to the seller's participation in Etsy Offsite Ads. Etsy says agentic traffic is still under 1% of total traffic (Feb and Aug 2026), but higher-intent and higher-AOV.

### Cited Findings
**Timeline and status**
- Sept 29, 2025: OpenAI announced Instant Checkout + Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP, built with Stripe), with US Etsy sellers live first and Shopify merchants "coming soon"; Etsy stock rose ~16% that day. [PRIMARY/PRESS] — [OpenAI: Buy it in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/); [CNBC, Sept 29, 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/29/chatgpt-instant-checkout-etsy-shopify.html)

Named sellers interviewed (Marie Amms, Jennifer Cupp, Fran Hurst, Bernice Ma) gave opinions, not sales figures. [PRESS] — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-free-onsite-ads-boost/)

No sales figures disclosed. [PRESS] — [Shopifreaks, May 6, 2026](https://www.shopifreaks.com/etsy-launches-beta-app-in-chatgpt-for-conversational-shopping-despite-prior-instant-checkout-integration-falling-short/)

**Etsy help center (buyer-facing)**
- Etsy help article (undated; title "Purchasing Etsy Items Through ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Copilot"): "Only shoppers in the United States can purchase Etsy items through ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Copilot"; only "some Etsy listings" are available (no exclusion list given); guest checkout with Etsy; USD only; Etsy gift cards and coupons cannot be used. No seller-side fee or control information. [PRIMARY] — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34208252828695-Purchasing-Etsy-Items-Through-ChatGPT)

**Offsite Ads (the gating program at launch)**
- Etsy Offsite Ads: 15% fee on attributed orders for shops under $10,000/yr, 12% at/over $10,000; per-order fee capped at $100; 30-day attribution window; shops that have never hit $10,000 in a 365-day period may opt out; once a shop hits $10,000 it must participate for the life of the shop. Channels listed: Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Bing, Etsy publishing partners, Google Display Network. The page does not mention ChatGPT, AI, Copilot, Gemini, or digital listings. [PRIMARY] — [Etsy Help: How Etsy's Offsite Ads Work](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work)

**How ChatGPT picks products (OpenAI help center)**
- ChatGPT product results use "Structured metadata from first-party and third-party providers (e.g., price, product description)" plus the model's own prior reasoning, user context (stated preferences can exclude items), and safety policies. Merchant list ranking considers "availability, price, quality, and whether they are the maker or primary seller of that item." Merchants can apply for direct product feeds. No mention of Etsy or paid placement. [PRIMARY] — [OpenAI Help: Shopping in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-in-chatgpt)
- OpenAI's current feed reference (viewed Oct 6, 2026) lists relevance-adjacent optional fields title, description, categories (taxonomy), and seller (name, policy links); "optional fields enrich relevance." No review-count or digital-goods fields appear in the current reference. [PRIMARY] — [OpenAI Commerce Feed Reference](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed)
- Vendor guidance (Sept 30, 2025) stresses machine-readable attributes (materials, dimensions, compatibility), media, and policy/trust info (returns, seller identity) and "plain-text clarity." [VENDOR] — [WordLift](https://wordlift.io/blog/en/openai-product-feeds)
- Exploding Topics says Etsy "remains integrated with ACP for improved product discovery in ChatGPT" and cites a consumer survey (41.08% "skeptical," 33.10% "suspicious" of autonomous purchase tools; median acceptable autonomous spend $0). Survey provenance not verified. [VENDOR/PRESS] — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/agentic-commerce-protocol)

### Inferences
- For a tiny Etsy shop, there is no direct "submit to ChatGPT" lever: Etsy holds the feed. The levers are (a) whatever Etsy uses to pick "some Etsy listings" for syndication (at launch, Offsite Ads participation), and (b) listing data quality, since OpenAI ranks on structured metadata, price, description and maker status. Etsy sellers are by definition the maker, which matches OpenAI's "maker or primary seller" criterion.
- Offsite Ads is a pay-on-attributed-sale fee (15% for a shop this size), not ad spend. Whether that conflicts with the owner's "no paid Etsy Ads" rule is the owner's call; ProofNotFluff (under $10k) can currently choose. On a $2.99 sale the fee is about $0.45. It is unverified whether opting out still removes listings from AI surfaces after the March 2026 changes.
- Because the Etsy app in ChatGPT is Etsy's own search surfaced in chat, Etsy-native relevance factors (title, tags, attributes, category, description, price filters like "under $X") likely drive it. This is reasoning, not documented.
- AI-agent traffic is under 1% of Etsy traffic (Aug 2026), so even full inclusion is a small channel today; the case for it is higher intent/AOV and low competition among tiny sellers.

### Gaps
- No primary Etsy or OpenAI source states whether digital downloads were or are eligible for ChatGPT checkout or the Etsy ChatGPT app. Common sense suggests guest checkout with physical shipping assumptions, but this is unverified.
- No current (post-March 2026) Etsy statement on whether Offsite Ads participation still governs inclusion in ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot surfaces, or whether AI-originated sales are charged Offsite Ads fees.
- No Etsy seller handbook page on AI shopping was found in this session.
- The earlier (Sept 2025) OpenAI feed spec was widely described as including review and search-eligibility fields; I could not confirm that against the current spec, which does not show them.

## 2. Google: AI Mode, Gemini, UCP checkout, Shopping free listings

### Takeaway
Since Feb 11-12, 2026, US shoppers can buy Etsy items directly inside Google AI Mode and the Gemini app via Universal Commerce Protocol checkout (Etsy and Wayfair were the first live merchants). Google's Merchant Center ban on ebooks/digital books applies to Shopping ads only, not to free listings, but no source confirms whether Etsy pushes digital-download listings into Google's shopping graph or UCP checkout.

### Cited Findings
- Feb 11, 2026: Google made Etsy and Wayfair items buyable in AI Mode and Gemini without leaving Google; Vidhya Srinivasan (VP/GM Ads & Commerce): "Agentic commerce is no longer just a concept, it's reality." No eligibility details, fees, or digital-item mentions. [PRESS] — [Retail Brew, Feb 11, 2026](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2026/02/11/google-makes-etsy-and-wayfair-items-shoppable-within-agentic-ai-search)
- Feb 12, 2026: "UCP-powered checkout is rolling out now, letting US shoppers buy items from Etsy and Wayfair, right in AI Mode in Search and the Gemini app"; Shopify, Target, Walmart "coming soon." [PRESS] — [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ucp-powered-checkout-in-ai-mode-40922.html)
- Google's UCP merchant onboarding lives in Merchant Center developer docs (for merchants with their own feeds; Etsy sellers do not control Etsy's feed). [PRIMARY] — [Google: Getting started with UCP](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp)
- Google Merchant Center "Unsupported Shopping content": "eBooks and digital books (not including audiobooks) are not allowed" (PDFs, ePub, MOBI), and "This policy only applies to Shopping ads. It does not apply to free listings." [PRIMARY] — [Google Merchant Center Help](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150006?hl=en)
- Etsy's buyer help page confirms Gemini and Google AI Mode as purchase surfaces for "some Etsy listings," US only. [PRIMARY] — [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/34208252828695-Purchasing-Etsy-Items-Through-ChatGPT)
- Etsy's Offsite Ads page lists Google among Offsite Ads channels (paid Shopping ads, not free listings). [PRIMARY] — [Etsy Help: Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work)

### Inferences
- Spreadsheets/templates are not "ebooks," so the ebook ban may not even apply, but Google's broader treatment of downloadable products in Shopping is unverified here. Free listings are not blocked by that policy.
- For long-tail queries ("cleaning business pricing calculator spreadsheet"), the more reliable Google path for a digital seller is organic web results (the Etsy listing page and the owner's own GitHub Pages site), which also feed AI Overviews/AI Mode citations (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No source found on whether Etsy digital downloads appear in Google Shopping free listings, AI Mode product panels, or UCP checkout.
- No data on Etsy sales volume via Google AI Mode/Gemini.

## 4. Listing attributes that matter to AI shopping agents

### Takeaway
The only primary, documented ranking inputs are OpenAI's: structured metadata (price, description), availability, price, quality, and maker/primary-seller status, plus the user's stated constraints. For Etsy listings this metadata comes from Etsy's own listing data, so title, description, category/attributes, and price are the seller-controlled inputs. Review effects are asserted by vendors but not documented by OpenAI or Google.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI: products chosen using "Structured metadata... (e.g., price, product description)"; merchants ranked on "availability, price, quality, and whether they are the maker or primary seller." [PRIMARY] — [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-in-chatgpt)
- OpenAI feed reference: title, description, categories, seller metadata are optional fields that "enrich relevance." [PRIMARY] — [OpenAI Feed Reference](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed)
- TechCrunch: ChatGPT shopping now shows comparisons of images, prices, features and reviews. [PRESS] — [TechCrunch, Mar 24, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-plans-to-make-chatgpt-more-like-amazon-arent-going-so-well)
- Etsy (Oct 2025): ChatGPT surfaces items "that wouldn't be found higher up in the traditional Etsy search." [PRESS quoting Etsy] — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/how-etsy-sellers-feel-about-the-new-chatgpt-checkout-integration/)
- Etsy Q1 2026: Etsy uses "AI-generated buyer profiles" and ML that "better understands what a buyer is looking for"; Q2 2026: AI to "better understand our inventory so that we can surface the most relevant inventory." [PRIMARY] — [Alpha Spread Q1](https://www.alphaspread.com/security/nasdaq/etsy/investor-relations/earnings-call/q1-2026); [Fool Q2](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/etsy-etsy-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

### Inferences
- Natural-language descriptions that state who it is for, what problem it solves, the inputs/outputs, file format (Excel and Google Sheets), and price-tier constraints ("under $5") map directly to how people prompt agents ("a pricing calculator for my cleaning business under $10"). This is reasoning from OpenAI's stated use of descriptions and user constraints.
- A $2.99 price is an advantage when agents filter on budget; 0 reviews may hurt if review data is shown/used, but no primary source quantifies this.
- Etsy's own statement that ChatGPT surfaces items not high in Etsy search suggests relevance-to-prompt can beat sales history, which favors a new shop with very specific listings.

### Gaps
- No Marketplace Pulse, Digiday or eMarketer analysis with concrete ranking data was retrieved in this session.
- No primary evidence on the weight of reviews, images, or Star Seller status in any AI agent's Etsy selection.

## 5. GEO for a small free-calculator website

### Takeaway
Google ranking is still the strongest single predictor of AI Overview citation (76% of cited pages rank top 10, per Ahrefs, July 2025), and off-site brand mentions (especially YouTube) correlate with AI brand visibility far more than backlinks or content volume (Ahrefs, Dec 2025, 75k brands). No rigorous study found on whether calculator/tool pages get cited.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (July 21, 2025; 1.9M citations from 1M AI Overviews): 76.10% of cited pages rank in Google top 10; 9.50% rank 11-100; 14.40% rank below 100. Top citation averages position 2. [VENDOR STUDY, large sample] — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-rankings-ai-citations)
- Conflicting/later framing: some 2026 coverage says only 38% of AI Overview citations come from top-10 pages (DesignRush summary of an Ahrefs update). Not verified at source; may reflect a later Ahrefs re-run. [PRESS] — [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/ai-overview-citations-drop-ahrefs); contradicted by the July 2025 figure above
- Ahrefs (Dec 12, 2025; 75,000 brands; ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews): YouTube mentions are the strongest correlate (~0.737); branded web mentions 0.656-0.709; branded anchors 0.511-0.628; branded search volume 0.352-0.466; Domain Rating 0.266-0.326; "very weak correlations" for link metrics; "almost no relationship between content volume... and AI visibility"; ChatGPT "less influenced by established brand dominance" than AI Mode. [VENDOR STUDY] — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations); press release May 26, 2026 [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260526119691/en/Across-75000-Brands-YouTube-Mentions-Are-the-Strongest-Signal-of-AI-Visibility-New-Ahrefs-Report-Reveals)

### Inferences
- For the GitHub Pages calculator site: ranking organically for long-tail "X calculator" queries is the main path into AI Overviews; earning mentions on YouTube, Reddit, and niche forums (organic, not fake) is the main path into ChatGPT brand recall. ChatGPT being less brand-dominated is good for a new site.
- Calculator pages should include crawlable text (method, formula, worked example, assumptions) because LLMs cite text, not JavaScript output. This is reasoning, not a study finding.
- Ensuring AI crawlers (OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot) are not blocked is a prerequisite; I did not retrieve the primary crawler docs in this session.

### Gaps
- No credible study found specifically on citation rates of interactive tool/calculator pages. Most 2026 "what gets cited" articles in results were low-quality vendor blogs and were excluded.
- Semrush, BrightEdge and Profound 2025-2026 studies were not retrieved in this session.
