# Underused on-Etsy growth levers for a new digital-download shop (as of Oct 6, 2026)

Scope note: ProofNotFluff context (3 weeks old, ~20 listings, 0 reviews, all $2.99, no Etsy Ads). Basics already covered elsewhere are excluded. Every item is labeled ETSY (Etsy's own statement: help center, policy, SEC filing, earnings call), VENDOR (eRank, Marmalead, other tool or course sellers), or FOLKLORE/THIRD-PARTY. Research ran on Oct 6, 2026. Etsy search pages (etsy.com/uk, /au, etsy.de) could not be fetched by the research tools (the fetch was blocked), so the page-1 competitor review counts the brief asked for are NOT collected. See Gaps.

## 1. International demand: translations, non-US GMS share, localized editions

### Takeaway
Etsy machine-translates every listing automatically, and a seller's own translation overrides the machine version only in languages the seller marks as "fluent". Etsy does not say anywhere that a manual translation ranks better. About a quarter of Etsy marketplace GMS comes from buyers outside the US (2025, buyer-based definition). Whether localized UK, Canada, Australia or Germany calculator editions are an open niche is still untested, because the competitor counts could not be pulled.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY: "Listings are also automatically translated. Shoppers searching the site will see listings in their preferred language." The page tells sellers to "Toggle on additional languages that you are fluent in", and "Any manual translations you provide will override automatic translations." The help page has no date (footer © 2026), and it does not list the languages that can be added or say whether manual translation affects search. [Etsy Help: How to Translate Your Shop and Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343048)
- ETSY (SEC 10-K for FY2025, filed 2026): **26% of Etsy marketplace GMS came from buyers outside the United States in 2025** (74% domestic). The 10-K says nothing about translation. It names the core geographies as the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Canada and Australia (in its Seller Census methodology). [Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm)
- THIRD-PARTY (Marketplace Pulse, using Etsy's broader definition): "international" GMS was **45.0% in Q3 2024**, unchanged from Q3 2023. The definition counts a sale as international when either the seller's billing address or the buyer's shipping address is outside the US. Etsy's CEO had stated a target of 50%. [Marketplace Pulse: Etsy International Sales Percentage](https://www.marketplacepulse.com/stats/etsy-international-sales-percentage)
  - Reconciling the two: 26% means non-US *buyers*, which is the number that matters for a US seller targeting non-US shoppers. The 45% figure also counts non-US *sellers*, including UK sellers selling to UK buyers. The two figures do not conflict.
- ETSY (Q2 2026 shareholder letter, Aug 2026): "Both U.S. and non-U.S. buyer GMS grew year-over-year". The letter gives no percentage for international share and notes that currency tailwinds were fading. [Etsy Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)

### Inferences
- Etsy already machine-translates the listings, so a UK or Australian buyer can find a US-built calculator today. The real lever is *content* localization, not language. That means separate listings whose titles and tags use local terms ("self assessment", "sole trader", "VAT", "GST/HST", "ABN", "BAS") and whose files contain local tax logic. English-speaking markets need no translation at all. Germany is the only one of the four that needs German copy, and Todd can only enter a manual German version honestly if he can vouch for it ("fluent" toggle).
- Non-US buyers are about 26% of GMS, so a US-only shop is missing roughly a quarter of buyer demand. Tax-specific calculators also carry correctness risk: a wrong UK VAT threshold produces "not as described" complaints. Each localized edition needs a dated source for every rate it uses.

### Gaps
- **The page-1 review counts on etsy.com/uk, etsy.com/au and etsy.de for "cleaning business pricing", "self assessment tax spreadsheet" and "sole trader spreadsheet" were not collected.** Etsy search URLs were blocked by the fetch tool. Todd (or a browser session) needs to run these by hand: open each search in an incognito window with the region set, and record the review count of each page-1 shop.
- No Etsy source lists which languages can be added manually. The supported-language list is visible in Shop Manager > Settings > Languages and was not verified here.
- No Etsy statement found on whether a seller-written translation improves ranking in that language versus the machine translation. Treat any claim either way as folklore.
- No public data on the non-US share for the digital-download category specifically.

## 2. Etsy features and surfaces (feed, search, Picks, featuring, Star Seller evolution, AI and Creativity rules)

### Takeaway
Etsy's 2026 direction, in its own words, is fresher, personalized app feeds that suppress items the buyer has already seen, plus search that "moves beyond surfacing what has historically been most popular." Both statements help new listings in principle. Etsy's Picks and editorial featuring are chosen by Etsy staff on quality, reliability and "elevated photography". Sellers cannot apply. For a digital shop, photo presentation and shop completeness are the controllable inputs.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY (Q2 2026 shareholder letter, Aug 2026): "Improvements to feed freshness, including suppressing over 80% of previously viewed and engaged listings, drove gains in feed favoriting, listing views, and new searches." The letter also says search "continued to improve real-time personalization in Search while evolving our approach to quality, moving beyond surfacing what has historically been most popular." Buyer profiles now cover "more than 65 million buyers," with "three times more signals than earlier this year" and "about 30 interests and preferences per buyer." [Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)
- ETSY (Q2 2026 earnings call, CEO Kruti Patel Goyal, Aug 2026): "We're really improving the freshness of our feed... reducing the prevalence of things that were recently viewed or engaged"; buyers are "starting more new shopping missions"; she cited "better discovery and matching... richer buyer profiles... improving the content that we can show you in home feeds, in push notifications." On AI shopping agents: "traffic from agentic experiences is still less than 1% of our overall traffic... higher intent, higher average order value." [Etsy 2Q26 transcript](https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/transcripts/ETSY-2Q26-transcript)
- ETSY (Q2 2026 letter): launched a beta "Gifting Assistant" that helps buyers find gift ideas "through natural conversation". [Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)
- ETSY (Q2 2026 letter): Etsy is "kicking off work to evolve our 'Star Seller' program," citing "new insights about Star Sellers who, in addition to great customer service, also provide distinctive inventory." In other words, Star Seller may begin weighing inventory distinctiveness. No criteria change has been announced. [Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)
- ETSY (FY2025 10-K): "by year end, the vast majority of push and email content was personalized for each user." [Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm)
- ETSY (figure cited by vendor Marmalead): "By Q3 2025, 46 percent of marketplace GMS came through the Etsy app." Marmalead's further claims (ranking driven by a quality score built on clicks, favorites, add-to-carts and purchases; Etsy AI rewriting titles) are VENDOR interpretation with no dated Etsy announcement behind them. [Marmalead: Etsy algorithm 2026 (vendor)](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)
- ETSY (help center, undated): Etsy's Picks badges are chosen by "Etsy style experts" on three criteria. "Quality: The item comes from a shop which may be well-rated by buyers"; "Reliability: The shop gives a great customer experience, such as shipping orders on time"; "Style: The item has elevated photography and presentation." Also: "Sellers don't pay or apply to have their products considered," and badges "are reviewed daily." [Etsy Help: What are Etsy's Picks?](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16594038248855-What-are-Etsy-s-Picks)
- ETSY (Seller Handbook, "Tips for getting featured on Etsy", timestamp Jan 17, 2025): the merchandising team looks for striking, editorial-style photos, strong shop ratings and customer service, complete shop info (policies, location, branding), originality, and no IP problems. Star Sellers have "an increased chance of being featured in Etsy buyer marketing." The channels named are Finds emails, Editors' Picks, Featured Shops and social media. [Etsy Seller Handbook: Tips for getting featured](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/tips-for-getting-featured-on-etsy/44104253845)
- THIRD-PARTY (Listadum blog, Jul 19-20, 2026, interpreting Etsy's Creativity Standards): "Designed by you" covers original designs sold as digital downloads. Reselling someone else's files or templates does not count, even with a commercial license. AI involvement must be disclosed in the description. Stand-alone AI prompt bundles are not allowed. A major tightening hit template, scan and supply categories in June 2025. These points come from the blog's reading of the standards, not from quoted Etsy text. [Listadum: Etsy Creativity Standards 2026](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)

### Inferences
- The feed now suppresses over 80% of already-seen listings and search is "moving beyond... most popular". Together these suggest Etsy is deliberately rotating fresh inventory into feeds. A shop with 0 reviews and steady new listings is better placed than it would have been in 2024. Shipping new, distinct listings on a regular schedule likely beats batch-dumping them, since each listing is new inventory in a feed that favors freshness. This is an inference: Etsy has not said new *listings* get feed priority, only that previously seen items get suppressed.
- Etsy's Picks and Editors' Picks weight "elevated photography" and shop reliability. For a spreadsheet shop that means editorial-style cover images (a styled desk or laptop scene, not a raw screenshot), complete shop policies and an About section, and fast message replies. These are the only controllable inputs, because sellers cannot apply.
- The Gifting Assistant and "gift" surfaces probably matter little for small-business calculators. They may matter for household-money items (budget planners for a new homeowner, a graduate, a wedding).
- Agentic shopping traffic (ChatGPT, Gemini) is under 1% of Etsy traffic but high-intent. Clear, literal descriptions (what the calculator does, inputs, outputs, Excel or Sheets) are what an agent can parse. This is speculative as a lever right now.

### Gaps
- No Etsy primary source found in this pass on: shop updates, Collections (seller-curated), gift mode mechanics, featured-listing slots, video in search results, or any 2026 instant-download rule change. These were not verified; do not state them as fact.
- The exact "more new and diverse listings" wording from the Q2 2026 call was not found. The closest Etsy wording is the feed-freshness and "moving beyond... most popular" language quoted above. The transcript summary may have paraphrased it.
- Etsy's own Creativity Standards page was not fetched. AI-disclosure requirements need checking against the Etsy page before any listing copy relies on them.

## 3. Conversion at $2.99: offers, bundles, price perception

### Takeaway
Etsy provides four discount tools: promo codes, sales, discounted bundles of up to three listings, and automated targeted offers for abandoned carts, favorites, interested shoppers and thank-you. Etsy publishes no conversion results for any of them. No credible data was found on whether $2.99 helps impulse buying or hurts perceived quality on Etsy.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY (help center, undated): there are four discount methods: promo codes, sales, discounted bundles and targeted offers. Promo codes can be "any percentage off between 5% and 75% off the list price." Only the highest discount applies when sales overlap. Discounted bundles hold "up to three listings," "Listings can only be part of one discounted bundle at a time," and "Bundle discounts will not stack with other sales." Targeted offers are sent automatically by email:
  - Abandoned cart: item in cart for 2+ hours; once every 2 days; expires in 60 days.
  - Favorited item: once every 2 days; expires in 60 days.
  - Interested shopper: high engagement; once every 30 days; expires in 7 days, single use.
  - Thank you: after order completion; expires in 1 year.
  The page gives no performance data and no statement on whether digital items qualify. [Etsy Help: How to Set Up Sales and Discounts](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108)
- THIRD-PARTY (Growing Your Craft, updated Oct 27, 2024): bundle offers appear "next to the 'add to basket' button" on the listing page and apply automatically in the cart. The discount range is 5% to 60%. "The offer will only show up if you're not already running a sale on that listing." A separate bundle listing can also target extra keywords. [Growing Your Craft: Etsy product bundling](https://growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-product-bundling-increase-revenue)

### Inferences
- At $2.99, the main use of discounted bundles is raising average order value. A "3 calculators for one business" bundle at 20-30% off makes about $6-7 instead of $2.99, and it shows next to the add-to-basket button on each member listing. Each listing can belong to only one bundle, so bundles should be built around a buyer persona (cleaning business, maker, household budget).
- Targeted offers cost nothing until they convert. Turning on thank-you (repeat purchase) and favorited/abandoned-cart offers is low-risk. The January 2026 truthful-pricing rule, covered in earlier research, should be checked against any "sale" framing, but targeted offers do not change list price.
- Bundle offers do not show while a sale is running on the listing. That is another reason not to run a shop-wide sale if bundles are the chosen lever.

### Gaps
- No Etsy-published or credible third-party data found on conversion lift from targeted offers or bundles.
- No credible evidence found on whether very low prices ($2.99) hurt perceived quality on Etsy, or on impulse-buy elasticity for digital downloads. Any claim here is folklore.
- Not confirmed whether digital-only listings can join discounted bundles or receive targeted offers (Todd can check in Shop Manager > Marketing > Sales and discounts).

## 4. Repeat buyers, cross-sell inside the file, shop email, and link rules

### Takeaway
Etsy's Seller Policy bans adding buyers to an email list without their consent, sending unsolicited commercial messages, and any content that "encourage[s] or facilitate[s] an off-platform transaction." A "Start Here" tab inside the file that points to other Etsy listings is not addressed explicitly. Linking to Etsy listings appears consistent with policy. Linking to an off-Etsy store is the risk.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY (Seller Policy, 2.c.i Messages): "you may not use this information for unsolicited commercial messages or unauthorized transactions. Without the buyer's consent... you may not add any Etsy member to your email or physical mailing list." Prohibited uses of Messages include "Facilitating or directing off-platform transactions." [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- ETSY (Seller Policy, 1.c and 2.a): sellers must "Not facilitate off-platform transactions"; content may not "Encourage or facilitate an off-platform transaction." [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- ETSY (Help, undated): the "Thank you" targeted offer goes to past buyers and stays valid for 1 year. It is Etsy's own built-in repeat-purchase email. [Etsy Help: Sales and Discounts](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108)

### Inferences
- A "Start Here / More tools" tab that links to the shop's own Etsy listings or shop sections keeps the transaction on Etsy and does not appear to breach the quoted policy text. Linking to a Gumroad or personal store, or offering "buy direct and save", would breach 1.c/2.a.
- Etsy's own repeat-buyer channels are the thank-you targeted offer and followers receiving shop updates. Building a private email list needs explicit buyer opt-in (for example, an opt-in in the file that the buyer chooses to take), and even then Todd should not message buyers with marketing through Etsy Messages.

### Gaps
- No Etsy text found that explicitly addresses links *inside downloadable files*. The policy wording above is the closest. The 2024 EcommerceBytes article on Etsy restricting buyer marketing could not be retrieved (the site has closed).

## 5. Legitimate review velocity

### Takeaway
For digital items, the 100-day review window starts when the buyer downloads the file, not at purchase. Etsy bans shilling and anything that "falsely inflate[s] a shop's review score". One no-incentive follow-up message is common, accepted practice (third-party guidance). Etsy itself does not explicitly endorse it in the text retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY (Help: How to Leave a Review): "The order must be within a 100-day review window, starting from the order's estimated delivery date." "For digital items, this window begins once you download your file(s)." Buyers "can edit the star rating, photograph, and the written note any number of times during the 100-day review period," but cannot edit while a case is open or after the seller has responded. [Etsy Help: How to Leave a Review](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013197687)
- ETSY (Seller Policy, 3.a Reviews): reviews "may not: Include shilling or otherwise falsely inflate a shop's review score; or Undermine the integrity of the Reviews system." [Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/)
- THIRD-PARTY (Growing Your Craft, updated Apr 24, 2025): one polite follow-up via Etsy Messages "a few days after... (or after a reasonable time for digital downloads)" is acceptable. Do not ask for a specific star rating. "No incentives permitted." [Growing Your Craft: Etsy shilling](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/what-is-etsy-shilling)

### Inferences
- The window starts on download, so a buyer who never downloads can never review. An order-confirmation note or follow-up that gets the buyer to *open and use* the file (with a quick-start step) both cuts "can't open it" complaints and opens the review window. This is the most direct legitimate lever on review rate.
- If the buyer can edit for 100 days but a seller reply freezes the review, Todd should not reply to a mixed review quickly. Fix the problem in Messages first so the buyer can raise the stars.

### Gaps
- No Etsy-published data on review rates for digital downloads. The roughly 1 in 10-15 figure from earlier research remains folklore-grade.

## 6. Star Seller for a digital shop

### Takeaway
The criteria are 95% of first messages answered within 24 hours, 95% on-time shipping with tracking (when required), a 4.8+ average rating, and at least 5 orders and $300 in sales over the past 3 months, checked on the 1st of each month. At $2.99, the $300 floor means about 100 orders in 3 months. Etsy says the badge does not affect search ranking, but Star Sellers get "an increased chance of being featured" in Etsy marketing.

### Cited Findings
- ETSY (Help: What is the Star Seller badge): "reply to 95% of first messages in a thread within 24 hours"; "95% of orders ship on time, with valid tracking or a shipping label purchased on Etsy (when required)"; "4.8+" review average; minimum 5 orders and $300 in sales "over the past 3 months"; "Star Seller eligibility is reviewed on the 1st of every month." "Star Seller badges do not directly impact your search ranking." [Etsy Help: Star Seller](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403058372503)
- ETSY (Seller Handbook, Jan 2025): Star Sellers have "an increased chance of being featured in Etsy buyer marketing." [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/tips-for-getting-featured-on-etsy/44104253845)
- ETSY (Q2 2026 letter): the Star Seller program is being "evolved," with interest in "distinctive inventory." [Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000079/q226shareholderletter.htm)

### Inferences
- $300 ÷ $2.99 ≈ 101 orders per rolling 3 months. At the current price, Star Seller is out of reach until volume reaches about 34 orders a month. Bundles that raise order value lower that order count. The 24-hour message rule is the criterion Todd fully controls today.
- Etsy states the badge does not affect search ranking. Its value is buyer trust plus a higher chance of Etsy marketing features.

### Gaps
- The help page does not say how digital-only orders are scored on the shipping criterion (likely auto-fulfilled, but not confirmed).
