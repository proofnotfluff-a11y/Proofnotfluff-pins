# Off-Etsy Channels for a New Spreadsheet/Calculator Shop (ProofNotFluff), as of October 2026

Research date: October 6, 2026. Scope: under-used channels outside Etsy for low-price ($2.99) Excel/Google Sheets calculators aimed at small service businesses (cleaners, detailers, lawn care, photographers), makers (craft fair sellers, bakers, candle makers) and households. Excludes paid ads, fake engagement, spam, agent posting on Reddit, or anything that breaks community rules.

## Ranked set of under-used channels (synthesis of everything below)

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence points to Google-indexed free calculator pages (one per trade question) that link to the matching paid file, plus Etsy Share & Save links so self-driven Etsy sales get a 4% fee refund. Second tier is owner-led participation in trade communities and trade podcasts/educators, then a cheap owned checkout (Payhip free plan) to unlock affiliates. General marketplaces (Gumroad Discover, Creative Market, Google Workspace Marketplace, KDP logbooks) show weak or no discovery evidence for this product at this price.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (Ryan Law, published July 16, 2026, updated Aug 12, 2026) argues free tools resist AI Overviews because an AI answer "can't hand the user the working converter or the interactive calculator"; examples include Gusto's hourly paycheck calculator (~17,800 visits/mo) and Shopify's profit-margin calculator (peaked above 20,000 visits/mo, now "several thousand") — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)
- Etsy Share & Save refunds 4% of transaction fees on sales within 30 days of a click on a seller's unique shop, listing or section URL shared on the seller's own channels (social, websites, blogs, email); a later Offsite Ads click overrides it; sellers in Etsy's Affiliate Program or Creator Co are ineligible (terms last updated Feb 20, 2024) — [Etsy Share & Save terms](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/etsys-share-save-terms-program-terms/1162874007996)
- Craftybase (maker inventory SaaS) runs free maker calculators (cake pricing, candle fragrance load, Etsy fee and pricing calculators, wholesale price calculator) plus free bakery costing and craft show spreadsheets as a funnel to a 14-day trial, showing competitors already target ProofNotFluff's maker queries with free tools — [Craftybase cake pricing calculator](https://craftybase.com/cake-pricing-calculator)
- Payhip's free plan charges 5% per sale (Plus $29/mo at 2%, Pro $99/mo at 0%), includes a built-in affiliate program, and has no marketplace (review published June 16, 2026, updated July 7, 2026) — [Financial Binder](https://financialbinder.com/payhip-review-sell-digital-products/)

### Inferences
Proposed ranking for ProofNotFluff (my judgment from the evidence in later sections):
1. **Free calculator pages on the GitHub Pages site, one per buyer question** ("house cleaning price calculator", "cake pricing calculator", "craft fair profit calculator", "candle cost calculator", "debt snowball calculator"), each linking to the matching Etsy listing through a Share & Save URL. Best evidence of compounding traffic; timeline is months (see SEO section).
2. **Owner-led participation in trade communities** (Facebook groups like ZenMaid Mastermind, r/LawnCarePros, CakeCentral, detailing forums), answering pricing questions with a free tool link where the rules allow. Fast to test, but weak conversion evidence; a 2019 detailing forum spreadsheet "went mostly unnoticed".
3. **Trade podcasts, educators and directories** (Cleaning Business Life podcast, ISSA Residential education, The Craft Map's 7,500+ fair directory and newsletter). Pitch free tools or guest spots. No conversion data, but these are concentrated audiences that already talk about pricing.
4. **Owned store on Payhip (free plan) with affiliates** for trade educators and coaches, since Etsy has no seller-run affiliate program. Only worth it once a partner exists.
5. **YouTube long-form "how to price X" walkthroughs** that use the free calculator on screen. Plausible, but I found no hard data for small channels.
6. **Low priority / skip for now:** Gumroad Discover (30% fee, reportedly shows products only after a first sale), Creative Market (50% commission, design-focused), Google Workspace Marketplace (reportedly no traffic), KDP low-content logbooks (saturated, tighter rules), TikTok (no evidence found for business-tool sellers).

### Gaps
- No source gave conversion rates for Google-to-Etsy-listing traffic specifically for $2 to $5 spreadsheets.

## Trade communities: where these buyers gather, promotion rules, and whether shared free tools convert

### Takeaway
Each buyer group has identifiable peer communities (cleaning Facebook masterminds and podcasts, lawn care subreddits and forums, detailing forums, cake forums, craft fair directories), but I found no hard evidence that a shared free tool converts to paid template sales there. The one detailing case found shows a free pricing spreadsheet was largely ignored.

### Cited Findings

- **Lawn care:** r/LawnCarePros had about 8,000 members (+3,000, or 45%, in a year); members often discuss estimating and invoicing software (Service Autopilot, Yardbook, Deeplawn) and pricing (GummySearch snapshot updated Sept 17, 2026) — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/r/LawnCarePros)
- **Detailers:** In an AutoGeekOnline thread (Aug 28, 2019), detailers shared free Excel pricing and time-estimate spreadsheets (V2 by Nicolas@Autowerx, V2.5 update). The original was described as having "criminally went mostly unnoticed", and one commenter said a calculator might be easier for non-Excel users — [AutoGeekOnline](https://autogeekonline.net/threads/good-spreadsheet-for-calculating-prices-for-your-packages-and-services.117801). Meguiar's forum also hosts detailing menu pricing threads — [MeguiarsOnline](https://www.meguiarsonline.com/forum/general-auto-detailing-discussion/detailing-4-dollars/14993-your-thoughts-on-my-detailing-menu-prices)

- **Reddit norms (owner posting only):** The common 90/10 guideline still applies in 2026, with some subs closer to 99/1; Reddit's content policy asks users to "post authentic content into communities where you have a personal interest"; the usual advice is 2 to 4 weeks of participation before any promotion, answering existing threads rather than starting promotional posts, and disclosing affiliation (July 2, 2026) — [Redship](https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules-2026)

### Inferences
- These communities are where ProofNotFluff's buyers ask "what should I charge". The owner's real credentials (finance VP, Superhost operator) make him a believable helper, but every group's pinned rules must be read before posting a link.
- The 2019 detailing thread suggests a raw spreadsheet dropped into a forum gets little traction. A browser calculator with a worked example probably fits better (the commenter's point), which supports leading with the GitHub Pages calculators, not file downloads.
- Podcasts and The Craft Map are partner-type targets (guest spot, free tool feature, newsletter mention), not posting venues.

## Programmatic SEO with free calculators: evidence, timelines, ranking factors, AI Overviews, conversion to Etsy

### Takeaway
Calculators and tools still pull search traffic in 2026 and are described as a defense against AI Overviews, which cut the top result's CTR by 58% across 300,000 keywords. Tool traffic can also fade (Shopify's and Kapwing's tools both fell from their peaks). One 2025 template seller who has its own store reported more revenue and a higher conversion rate there than on Etsy, mostly from organic search (self-reported).

### Cited Findings
- Tool page traffic examples (Ahrefs estimates, July 2026): Omni Calculator margin calculator 49,169 visits/mo; Gusto hourly paycheck calculator ~17,800/mo; Shopify profit-margin calculator peaked above 20,000/mo and now gets "several thousand"; Kapwing meme maker peaked above 150K and now gets ~10,500; Venngage templates hub ~11,000/mo — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)
- Ahrefs says an AI-built deck material calculator cost "$1.24 and about a minute", so tools are now cheap to build and competition will rise. The article gives no conversion data — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)
- AI Overviews: Ahrefs' December 2025 study of 300,000 keywords found the top-ranking page's CTR was 58% lower than a December 2023 baseline (34.5% in its study eight months earlier); position 2 lost about half its clicks and position 10 nearly 20%. No separate figure for tool or calculator queries — [Shopifreaks summary of Ahrefs](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ai-overviews-now-reduce-top-ranking-pages-clickthrough-rates-by-58-according-to-new-ahrefs-research-analyzing-300000-keywords/)
- CraftedCharts (digital chart/template seller, self-reported): Etsy 2025 was ~31,000 views, 1,116 units, 3.7% conversion, €7,717; its Shopify store was ~30,000 sessions, ~1,400 units, ~4.7% conversion, €9,165.87; Shopify traffic was "almost 90% organic search" with no paid ads; prices €4 to €9 — [CraftedCharts Etsy vs Shopify](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/etsy-vs-shopify-for-digital-products). Caution: the same article says the full Shopify catalog only went live in December 2025, which doesn't square with a full year of Shopify data. Treat the comparison as approximate.
- CraftedCharts Etsy history (self-reported): revenue €817 (2022), €1,248 (2023), €3,523 (2024), €7,717 (2025); catalog grew from 48 to 132 products; about 81% of Etsy visits came from Etsy itself; conversion fell from 4.39% to 3.66% in 2025, which the seller blames on Google and Etsy algorithm changes around September 2025 — [CraftedCharts 3 years of data](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/3-years-of-etsy-shop-data-2022-2025)
- Competitors already run free calculators on maker queries (Craftybase: cake pricing, candle fragrance load, Etsy fees, wholesale price; Calculate.co.nz and Calculover cake pricing calculators) — [Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/cake-pricing-calculator); [Calculate.co.nz](https://www.calculate.co.nz/cake-pricing-calculator.php); [Calculover](https://calculover.com/food-cooking/baking/cake-pricing/)
- Cleaning price calculators are offered as templates by form builders such as involve.me, so that query space is also contested — [involve.me](https://www.involve.me/template-categories/price-estimation/cleaning-price-calculator)

### Inferences
- For ProofNotFluff, the play is narrow, intent-matched calculator pages, one per trade question, each a working tool plus a short worked example from the owner's real finance experience, linking to the paid file through a Share & Save URL. Big generic terms ("margin calculator") belong to Omni and Shopify.
- Timeline: earlier research found one seller going from 800 to 10,000 clicks/mo over 10 months. Combined with Ahrefs' note that tool traffic decays (Shopify, Kapwing), plan for 6 to 12 months and keep pages updated.
- CraftedCharts' figures (self-reported) suggest search visitors to an owned store can convert at about 4.7%, at least as well as Etsy's own traffic. That supports eventually pairing the free site with an owned checkout, not only Etsy links.

### Gaps
- No public data on what share of Google visitors who land on a free calculator click through to an Etsy listing and buy.
- No 2025 to 2026 study isolating AI Overview impact on "calculator" queries specifically.
- How many pages to build: no source gave a page count tied to results for small sites.

## YouTube long-form search ("how to price house cleaning", "how to price cakes"): evidence for small channels driving store clicks

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous 2025 to 2026 data on small channels driving Etsy or store sales from long-form "how to price" videos. The best direct evidence is old (Better Sheets 2020 to 2022, mostly via AppSumo). Shorts-vs-long-form comparisons online are unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- Better Sheets (Andrew Kamphey, self-reported): $100,000 top-line from April 2020 to May 2022 selling Google Sheets tutorials and templates; 85% of revenue came through AppSumo's marketplace, 13% through Gumroad, and Twitter under $1,000 in attributed sales. A free template pack reached 34,000 downloads by May 2022 — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/100k-revenue-in-2-years-selling-google-sheet-tutorials-faefa26dfb)
- Better Sheets newsletter (June 11, 2021): a small seller's "Job Indecision Calculator" made $241 from 34 sales via the Indie Hackers marketplace; other sheets were sold on Gumroad or used as newsletter lead magnets — [Better Sheets Substack](https://bettersheets.substack.com/p/your-next-1000-a-month-side-hustle)
- A comparison claiming long-form converts views to subscribers at 1 to 3% vs 0.1 to 0.5% for Shorts is unsourced editorial, not data — [TubeCore](https://tubecore.xpanddigital.io/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-longform)
- A Udemy course on "how to price your detailing services correctly" shows paid demand for detailing pricing education — [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/how-to-price-your-detailing-services-correctly/)

### Inferences
- ProofNotFluff's own finding (Shorts sent no Etsy visits) fits the general view that Shorts don't drive clicks out. Long-form search videos that demo the free calculator, with a Share & Save link in the description and pinned comment, are a reasonable low-cost test, but expect slow results.
- The Better Sheets AppSumo result suggests that a marketplace for business buyers can beat social for spreadsheets. AppSumo's current fit for $2.99 trade calculators is untested.

### Gaps
- No named 2025 to 2026 small channel with numbers on clicks or sales from "how to price X" videos.
- No official YouTube documentation retrieved on search traffic share for small channels.

## Partnerships, affiliates, bundles and newsletter swaps

### Takeaway
Etsy has no seller-run affiliate program, but Payhip's free plan includes affiliates (seller sets the rate, 30-day cookie, seller pays affiliates). Trade educators, podcasts and directories exist that could feature a tool, though I found no documented results for template sellers doing this.

### Cited Findings
- Payhip affiliates: the seller sets the commission; affiliates join with the seller's unique signup code; 30-day last-touch attribution; Payhip doesn't pay affiliates (sellers pay them using monthly reports with PayPal details); Payhip cites "over 130,000 sellers" — [Payhip Help](https://help.payhip.com/article/214-becoming-an-affiliate)
- Payhip fees: Free plan $0/mo + 5%; Plus $29/mo + 2%; Pro $99/mo + 0%, plus ~2.9% + $0.30 processing; affiliates included on the free plan; no marketplace (June/July 2026) — [Financial Binder](https://financialbinder.com/payhip-review-sell-digital-products/)
- Etsy Share & Save (4% fee refund on own-channel links) can't be combined with Etsy's Affiliate Program or Creator Co — [Etsy terms](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/etsys-share-save-terms-program-terms/1162874007996)
- The Craft Map already promotes third-party maker tools (Soaply, WickSuite) and affiliate links and runs a vendor newsletter, so it's a plausible partner — [The Craft Map](https://www.thecraftmap.com/blog/where-to-sell-handmade-items-locally)
- In an Indie Hackers thread (March 31, 2026), a commenter said affiliate outreach worked better than waiting for Gumroad Discover; this is anecdotal, with no numbers — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/gumroad-has-zero-organic-discovery-im-cross-listing-my-data-product-everywhere-e9c4d8b629)

### Inferences
- At $2.99, a 30% affiliate commission is about $0.90 per sale, too small to motivate partners. Partnerships fit better as bundles (e.g., a cleaning pricing kit at a higher price on Payhip), free tools given to an educator's audience, or a guest spot on a trade podcast.
- Fee math at $2.99 (my calculation from the cited fees): Payhip free ≈ 5% ($0.15) + processing (~$0.39) ≈ $0.54, about 18% of price; Gumroad direct ≈ 10% + $0.50 + processing ≈ $1.19, about 40%.

### Gaps
- No named trade educator or YouTuber in cleaning, detailing, lawn care, baking or candles was confirmed as recommending third-party spreadsheet tools.
- No data on newsletter swaps for template sellers.

## Other marketplaces with built-in buyer traffic (2026)

### Takeaway
None of the alternatives shows strong discovery for a new $2.99 spreadsheet seller. Gumroad Discover charges 30% and reportedly needs a first sale before listing; Payhip has no marketplace; Creative Market takes 50% and is design-led; the Google Workspace Marketplace is reported to have essentially no template traffic; KDP low-content is saturated with tighter rules.

### Cited Findings
- **Gumroad:** direct sales 10% + $0.50 (≈12.9% + $0.80 with Stripe); Discover sales a flat 30%, which applies even when the seller drove the traffic; fees aren't refunded on refunds (updated June 8, 2026) — [Checkout Page](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)
- **Gumroad Discover access:** one seller reported "26 products on gumroad. zero organic views. zero from gumroad discover" and said Discover "only shows products after your first sale" (self-reported, March 31, 2026) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/gumroad-has-zero-organic-discovery-im-cross-listing-my-data-product-everywhere-e9c4d8b629)
- **Gumroad traffic:** a Similarweb-based analysis says Gumroad's traffic is mostly direct (40.55% of desktop) and branded search, and concludes buyers arrive "because you already sent them"; no Discover sales share given (Aug 13, 2026) — [Roo's Newsletter](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/gumroad-traffic-2026-real-data)
- **Gumroad seller earnings:** an independent dataset of 146,271 products and 43,884 sellers estimates the median seller earns about $72/month (July 29, 2026) — [DEV / InsightRaider](https://dev.to/insightraider/almost-every-gumroad-stat-youve-read-traces-to-one-2021-blog-post-here-are-7-that-dont-3poi)
- **Payhip:** no marketplace; "Both require you to drive your own traffic" — [Financial Binder](https://financialbinder.com/payhip-review-sell-digital-products/)
- **Creative Market:** sellers earn 50% per sale; no exclusivity; about 10 million members; mainly fonts, graphics and themes (article Oct 17, 2024, so may be dated) — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/creative-market-marketplace-breakdown/)
- **Google Workspace Marketplace:** Better Sheets says "I don't think there's really any traffic on the google workspace marketplace" for templates (undated course lesson) — [Better Sheets](https://bettersheets.co/tutorials/explore-your-spreadsheet-selling-options)
- **AppSumo:** drove 85% of Better Sheets' revenue in 2020 to 2022 (self-reported, older data) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/100k-revenue-in-2-years-selling-google-sheet-tutorials-faefa26dfb)
- **Amazon KDP low-content:** categories hold "hundreds of thousands" of titles; Amazon added upload limits and AI-content disclosure; low-content books get no free ISBN and no Look Inside; niche planners for small business owners and medium-content workbooks still work (Naomi Jane Johnson, Dec 15, 2025) — [Substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon). The Authors Guild reported a KDP daily upload cap tied to the AI policy — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/)

### Inferences
- For a 3-week-old shop, a second marketplace mostly adds upkeep. A Payhip store makes sense as a checkout for owned traffic and affiliates, not for discovery.
- A KDP "cleaning business job log" or "craft fair sales log" could be a medium-content side test, but evidence points to saturation; low priority.

### Gaps
- Microsoft templates (Microsoft Create) and Notion's gallery: I found no 2026 source on third-party seller access or traffic. Google Sheets' template gallery isn't open to independent sellers as far as I could find; unverified.
- TPT: not researched (not relevant to these buyers).
- Couldn't load Gumroad's Discover help article body, so eligibility rules are second-hand.

## Pinterest 2026 changes and TikTok for business-tool sellers

### Takeaway
I found no official 2026 Pinterest change aimed at small product-pin accounts. Community reports describe a sharp distribution drop around May 8, 2026, and Pinterest's 2026 creator news centers on Amazon storefront linking. I found no evidence that TikTok drives sales for business-tool spreadsheet sellers.

### Cited Findings
- Around May 8, 2026, established Pinterest creators reported sudden drops in impressions and outbound clicks (one account down 90%; another from 1M+ monthly views to ~100K). A Pinterest Community Manager reportedly confirmed an outage with "unexpected" results. The poster is a Pinterest Pioneer, not staff (May 22, 2026) — [Pinterest Business Community](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/pinterest-numbers-dropped-read-this-before-you-spiral/45856)
- In 2026, Pinterest added Amazon Storefront linking for creators — [Pinterest Newsroom](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/en-au/news/amazon-storefront-linking/)
- An older but often cited claim, "$40,000 per month selling spreadsheet templates via Pinterest", appears as a blog repost; I couldn't verify the date or numbers — [Goodreads blog repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25460887-40-000-per-month-selling-spreadsheet-templates-via-pinterest)

### Inferences
- Pinterest remains a slow, search-like channel. Given the owner-approval step, a small number of fresh vertical pins per week pointing at the free calculators (not straight at Etsy) is the low-effort version.
- TikTok: no evidence either way for this product type. Treat it as an untested experiment, not a priority.

### Gaps
- No official Pinterest documentation retrieved on 2026 product pin or outbound click changes for small accounts.
- No 2025 to 2026 case study found of a business-tool or spreadsheet seller growing through TikTok.

### Takeaway
Few named, numbered 2025 to 2026 examples exist. The best is CraftedCharts (organic search to an owned Shopify store, 2025, self-reported); the rest are older (Better Sheets via AppSumo, 2020 to 2022) or anecdotal with no numbers.

### Inferences
- The evidence leans toward search (Google, plus Etsy's own search) as the channel that actually moves template sales. Social channels show weak attributed sales (Better Sheets' Twitter: under $1,000).
