# Vertical (trade/craft-specific) spreadsheet editions on Etsy: which phrases a new shop can reach, Oct 2026

Method and measurement caveats (read first)
- All Etsy page data below was measured on 2026-10-06 by fetching public `etsy.com/market/<phrase>` pages (logged-out, server-rendered, US English). Each fetch returned only the top 4 to 8 listings of page 1, not the full 48 to 64. So "page 1" in this file means the top ~8 server-rendered slots. Positions for a logged-in, personalized buyer will differ.
- The number in parentheses on an Etsy search card is the shop's total review count, not the listing's. Below, "rev" means shop review count. A "?" means the card showed no count, which usually means a shop with zero or very few reviews.
- Every market page showed "5,000+ items". That is Etsy's display cap, so it says nothing about how crowded a phrase is.
- The fetch tool's small model wrote the page summaries. Ad labels and badges are as it reported them and may sometimes be missing. Direct curl to etsy.com and rankhero.com was blocked by this environment's proxy (HTTP 403), so I could not fetch pages raw.
- Etsy phrases have little public keyword data. RankHero publishes pages only for broad terms. For "pricing calculator" and "cleaning business" its volumes (18,100; 14,800; 9,900; 12,100; 8,100; 6,600) are exactly Google Ads Keyword Planner bucket values, even though RankHero labels them "Etsy". Treat them as third-party estimates of uncertain origin, not Etsy search counts. eRank, Kupkaike and Marmalead had no public per-phrase pages I could reach. MakerWords shop pages were reachable, but none of its keyword pages for these phrases surfaced.

## Q1. Which 25 to 40 phrases combine real demand with page-1 slots held by small shops (under ~100 reviews) or by printables rather than working calculators?

### Takeaway
The service trades are the most open. Cleaning, pressure washing, junk removal, window cleaning, lawn/landscaping, pet sitting, food truck and auto detailing all have page-1 slots held almost entirely by shops with 0 to 75 reviews, often by printables, Canva forms or contracts rather than working calculators. A few maker niches (3D printing, laser engraving, soap, embroidery, vinyl decals) are also open, and 3D printing shows the strongest demand signal: four Bestseller badges on page 1. Broad or popular craft phrases (cake, crochet, jewelry, woodworking, candle, catering) are partly taken by three or four mega-shops with 3,300 to 9,300 reviews that already sell a $3.29 to $3.67 edition for each craft. Personal-finance phrases (debt snowball, mileage log) are closed.

### Inferences
- The best next 25 to 40 editions, roughly in order: house/residential cleaning, move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning bid, pressure washing, soft wash/house wash, window cleaning, junk removal, lawn care, landscaping, 3D printing, laser engraving, soap, food truck, pet sitting, dog walking, mobile/auto detailing, handyman, embroidery, vinyl decal/Cricut, photography, nail tech, florist, amigurumi/crochet, candle, permanent jewelry, sticker, craft fair, cookie, notary/loan signing, tutor rates, catering, plus a quarterly estimated tax calculator timed for December–January. Carpet cleaning, pool service, dog grooming, lash tech, hair salon and painting are good next tests, but I did not measure them (see Gaps).
- Low-review page-1 slots are not proof of demand. Many of these trades have no Bestseller badges and only 1 to 10-review sellers, which fits low demand just as well as an open niche. The strongest demand evidence is in 3D printing (4 Bestsellers), embroidery (a $24.99 Bestseller), handyman/estimate (Bestsellers on generic forms), notary (675 favorites on one listing) and cleaning (the RankHero proxy plus many sellers entering).
- The listing-ID clustering suggests these service-trade niches filled with new, probably AI-assisted calculator listings in 2025–2026. "Small shops on page 1" today may mean many small shops competing, so being first is no longer possible. Winning will depend on per-trade specificity (quote logic such as square footage, panes, truck-load fractions, filament grams) and on photos.
- Where page 1 is held by printables or Canva price lists (auto detailing, cookie, pet sitting, photography, lawn bids), a working calculator is a clearly different product. Those phrases may convert better for a calculator than phrases where three near-identical calculators already sit.

### Gaps
- No reliable Etsy-native monthly search volume for any of these phrases. RankHero covers only broad heads, and its numbers look like Google Ads buckets. eRank, Marmalead and Kupkaike per-phrase public pages could not be found or fetched.
- I did not measure market pages for carpet cleaning, pool service, dog grooming (listings exist), lash tech, hair salon, massage, personal trainer, music teacher, virtual assistant, bookkeeping pricing, event planner, daycare, consulting rate, quilt, pottery, sewing, knitting, resin (listings exist), print on demand (one listing found), bakery pricing spreadsheet, painting estimate (URLs found but not fetched), 1099 tracker or sinking funds. The 15-call research budget was the limit.
- Only the top 4 to 8 slots were visible per page. Positions 9 to 48 are unknown.
- Ad labels come from a model summary and may be incomplete. Some pages reported no ad flags at all, which is unlikely to be literally true.

## Q2. At $2.99, how does price compare to page-1 prices in each phrase?

### Takeaway
$2.99 is at or below the bottom of almost every page 1, but it is not unique. The mega-shops sell trade editions at $3.29 to $3.67 (shown as "50% off" $6.59 to $7.35), and a few listings sit at $1.00 to $2.57. Small-shop niche calculators in service trades usually sell for $6 to $20. So $2.99 wins on price only against those small shops; the incumbents in maker niches undercut or match it.

### Cited Findings
- Mega-shop edition pricing seen across verticals: $3.67 (from $7.35) for crochet, jewelry, woodworking and candle editions on shops with 3.5k reviews ([crochet](https://www.etsy.com/market/crochet_pricing_calculator), [jewelry](https://www.etsy.com/market/jewelry_pricing_calculator_template), [woodworking](https://www.etsy.com/market/woodworking_pricing_calculator), [candle](https://www.etsy.com/market/candle_making_calculator_spreadsheet)); $3.29 (from $6.59) for cake, baking and mileage editions with 3.5k reviews ([cake](https://www.etsy.com/market/cake_price_calculator), [cookie](https://www.etsy.com/market/cookie_pricing_sheet), [mileage](https://www.etsy.com/market/mileage_tracker_excel)); $3.38 (from $7.35) for SavvyandThriving's stem count calculator ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4329861921/stem-count-calculator-spreadsheet)).
- Lowest prices seen: $1.00 "Pricing Calculator Spreadsheet" from a 9.3k shop ([woodworking](https://www.etsy.com/market/woodworking_pricing_calculator)); $1.59 sticker calc ([sticker](https://www.etsy.com/market/sticker_cost_calculator)); $1.65 woodworking template; $1.69 craft calc ([cricut](https://www.etsy.com/market/pricing_spreadsheet_for_cricut_decals)); $2.06 permanent jewelry calc ([jewelry](https://www.etsy.com/market/jewelry_pricing_calculator_template)); $2.57 soap fillable PDF ([soap](https://www.etsy.com/market/soap_cost_calculator)).
- Small-shop service-trade calculators: cleaning $3.50–$11.24 for most, up to $39.97 for a website widget ([cleaning](https://www.etsy.com/market/cleaning_price_calculator)); pressure washing $7.49–$19.99, plus an $84.95 app ([pressure](https://www.etsy.com/market/pressure_wash_estimate_excel_template)); junk removal $6.13–$18.74 ([junk](https://www.etsy.com/market/junk_removal_pricing_sheet)); lawn/landscaping $6.97–$27.99 ([lawn](https://www.etsy.com/market/lawn_care_bid_template)); food truck $5.96–$18.00 ([food truck](https://www.etsy.com/market/food_truck_spreadsheet)); auto detailing Excel calc $14.65 ([detailing](https://www.etsy.com/market/auto_detailing_pricing_template)).
- Maker niches: 3D printing $4.99–$19.99 ([3D](https://www.etsy.com/market/3d_printing_cost_calculator)); laser $3.49–$17.99 ([laser](https://www.etsy.com/market/laser_engraving_pricing_calculator)); embroidery $3.45–$29.99 ([embroidery](https://www.etsy.com/market/embroidery_price_calculator)).
- RankHero's broad "pricing calculator" price distribution: 37% under $10, 58% at $10–$25, median $12.99 ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/pricing-calculator)).
- Two new shops with uniform low prices ($2.99 and $3.49) ranked top-10 for inventory keywords per MakerWords but had 0 and 1 sales after 6 months ([IntentionalSheets](https://www.makerwords.com/shop/intentionalsheets), [PlannedToThrive](https://makerwords.com/shop/plannedtothrive)).

### Inferences
- In the service trades (cleaning, pressure washing, junk removal, lawn, detailing, food truck), $2.99 is 50 to 80% below the small shops already there, so price is a real edge. In maker niches held by $3.29 to $3.67 incumbents, it is only about $0.30 to $0.70 cheaper than a shop with thousands of reviews, which is not enough to beat their social proof.
- Many incumbents show "50% off" strike-through prices. A flat $2.99 with no strike-through can look worse value on a search card than "$3.67 (was $7.35)", even though it is cheaper. That is a design and positioning choice for the caller, not something measured here.
- The MakerWords examples show that a low price plus a good rank does not guarantee sales, at least for generic inventory trackers.

### Gaps
- Conversion data by price point for these niches is not public.
- MakerWords pages do not give the date of their data.

## Q3. Are there documented Etsy shops (named, with public sales counts) that grew by publishing many trade- or craft-specific editions of one tool? What happened to them?

### Takeaway
Yes. The clearest case is SavvyandThriving: about 46–47k sales, 3.4–3.5k reviews, around 460 listings, and a catalog MakerWords describes as mostly industry-specific spreadsheet versions. Its editions show up on page 1 across florist, notary, craft fair and candle searches. Two smaller edition-style shops show the slower path. CraftCalculators has 53 material-specific calculators since 2018 for about 2,000 sales and 197 reviews. NicheToolkits, which opened in 2026, has 269 trade toolkits for 113 sales and 5 reviews in 5 months. Edition catalogs work at scale, but the payoff took years, or so far shows little conversion per listing.

### Cited Findings
- **SavvyandThriving** (Gwen, Atlanta GA): 46.3k sales, 3.4k reviews, 4.8 stars, per its stem-count listing page on 2026-10-06 ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4329861921/stem-count-calculator-spreadsheet)). ## Q4. Any Etsy policy limits on near-duplicate listings or keyword-spam editions? (current wording and date)

### Takeaway
I found no Etsy rule that bans or caps trade-specific editions of one tool. The Seller Policy (updated Oct 5, 2026) bans duplicate shops and actions that manipulate search. The Creativity Standards (Jun 10, 2025) require original seller-made digital content and AI disclosure. Etsy's search guidance (Aug 2025) says new listings get only a "small, temporary boost" and that making new listings just for that boost "isn't an effective search optimization strategy". The title guidance (Apr 27, 2026) asks for titles under about 15 words without repeated words. So editions are allowed, but each one should be genuinely different, not a copy with only the keywords changed.

### Cited Findings
- Seller Policy, "Last Updated: October 5, 2026": sellers must "Not create duplicate shops or take any other action (such as manipulating clicks, carts or sales) for the purpose of shilling, manipulating search or circumventing Etsy's policies." The fetched summary found no explicit clause on duplicate listings, keyword stuffing or tags in this policy ([Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers)).
- Creativity Standards, "Last Updated: Jun 10, 2025": digital downloads qualify as "Original content created by the seller, sold as a digital download"; "Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is created with the use of AI"; "AI prompt bundles" do not qualify. The summary found no duplicate-listing language ([Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)).
- "How Etsy Search Works" (Seller Handbook, dated August 2025): "When a new listing is created, it gets a small, temporary boost in search results"; "Regularly renewing your items or creating new listings just to get this small boost isn't an effective search optimization strategy"; "Diversifying your inventory and trying lots of different relevant tags on your listings gives you the opportunity to match with more searches"; exact keyword matches "may be ranked higher". It also says shops with strong review ratings, message response rates and low case rates over the past three months get prioritized ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487)).
- "New Guidance for Listing Titles" (April 27, 2026): "Consider using less than 15 words"; "Try not to repeat words"; avoid "repetition of aspirational or gifting keywords and phrases". It says Etsy's search now evaluates the full listing, so sellers should "focus on clarity and relevance" rather than pack in keywords ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)).
- Etsy Help "Copying a Similar Listing": "Copying creates a brand new listing with all of the info from the copied listing in place" and "The new listing will have a new URL". No cap or prohibition on near-identical listings is stated ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000337307-Copying-a-Similar-Listing)).
- Third-party (Marmalead blog, March 21, 2022): "you never want two listings with the exact same photos, titles, descriptions, keywords, etc." because "you're not doubling your chances of getting found in search. Not at all actually." It recommends separate listings for real variations, each optimized differently ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-keyword-strategy-copying-your-etsy-listings/)).
- Etsy Community threads on duplicate listings in search returned 404 when fetched ([thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Managing-Your-Shop/Identical-Duplicate-Listings-Detrimental-to-Search-Results-for/td-p/131474027?nobounce)).

### Inferences
- Policy risk for honest trade editions (different presets, inputs, quote logic, photos and copy) looks low. The real risks are (a) a "manipulating search" judgment if listings differ only in keywords, and (b) search treating near-identical listings as redundant. Neither is spelled out, but both are consistent with Etsy's emphasis on relevance and listing quality.
- The AI disclosure rule applies if the build engine uses AI to make the spreadsheets or listing images. The caller should check that each edition's description carries the disclosure.
- Etsy's own guidance plays down listing churn ("small, temporary boost"). That favors fewer, better trade editions with real differences over dozens of thin ones.

### Gaps
- I could not find current Etsy wording that explicitly addresses near-duplicate listings, search de-duplication or keyword-spam editions. Either it does not exist in public policy or it was not surfaced. The relevant Etsy Help "Reasons a Listing May Be Removed Under Etsy's Creativity Standards" article returned 404.
- Etsy staff statements on how search handles near-duplicates within one shop (for example, shop-diversity limits per results page) were not found.
