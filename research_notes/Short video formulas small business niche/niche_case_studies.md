# Short video formulas in the small-business money and spreadsheet-tools niche: case studies

Research date: Oct 15, 2026. About 21 tool calls. Items dated before 2024 are marked [PRE-2024]. Few sources from 2024 to 2026 profile named creators in this exact niche with hard numbers. Most press coverage is from 2020 to 2023, and 2024 to 2026 material is mostly listicles, aggregator stats pages and platform policy news. Treat the thin coverage as a finding in its own right.

## 1. Which accounts in adjacent niches grew on Reels, TikTok or Shorts, and how (followers, cadence, format, length, hook, face or faceless, series, selling)?

### Takeaway
The best-documented case is still Miss Excel (Kat Norton). She is face-on and highly personal: green screen over Excel, dance trends, one 14-second tip, daily posting. Free videos feed $44 to $997 courses and corporate training. No source I found documents a faceless spreadsheet or pricing-calculator account with real numbers. The documented faceless or low-face wins in adjacent niches come from trades businesses using before/after transformation videos (SB Mowing, Yard Maintenance Geelong), not from talking-head finance tips.

### Cited Findings

**Miss Excel / Kat Norton (Excel tips, TikTok and Instagram, face-on)**
- Launched @miss.excel on TikTok in June 2020. Her first video was 14 seconds, posted about 48 hours after a friend suggested "What if you put your Excel tips on TikTok?" Her 4th video got 100K views. After video 6, an IT company CEO asked her to train his staff, which became her first corporate-training lead. — [Starter Story, Feb 10 2024 (figures "estimated from public sources")](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)
- First viral post was 3.4M views, about 3 weeks after launch, taking her to 100K followers. Early growth came 100% from TikTok. She posted daily, then expanded to Instagram. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)
- Followers per Starter Story: 950K Instagram and 939K TikTok. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown). Favikon lists her at "over 1 million" (platform unspecified). — [Favikon, 2025](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-excel-office-productivity-influencers-worldwide)
- Format: Excel tips delivered with TikTok dance trends, using a ring light ($30), green screen ($30 to $40) and iPhone, and she edited everything herself. Starter Story calls dancing the "one-of-one" angle nobody else used. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)
- [PRE-2024] She recorded about 10 clips before launching, then posted one video a day. She used a 12-foot green screen and filmed in varied locations (desert, pool, home), sometimes in costume. Her stated promise: "You're going to learn something about Microsoft Excel, and you're going to have a little fun doing it." Followers in Nov 2022: 870K+ TikTok, 650K+ Instagram. — [Binghamton University News, Nov 28 2022](https://www.binghamton.edu/news/story/3978/how-to-excel-in-business-miss-excels-journey-from-business-student-to-social-media-star)
- How she sells: 10 online courses ($44 to $497) and bundles ($497 to $997) on Thinkific, plus corporate training, keynotes, free webinars and a newsletter. Her first course launched about 5 months in, and she later made $100K in one day from courses. Starter Story estimates revenue at $242K/month, but it is internally inconsistent: it says "$2M annual revenue in 2023" and also "$2.9M/year". — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)
- Stated lessons: launch courses before they are perfect, and build new ones in 5 to 6 days. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)

**Budgeting / cash stuffing**
- ohhyoubudget (Toronto personal-finance creator; budgeting, sinking funds, debt; sells her own budgeting and debt templates): 86,600 TikTok followers (2.6 posts/week, 6.2% engagement) and 23,318 Instagram (4.9 posts/week, 4.3% engagement) as of Oct 8 2026. **Trades businesses (lawn, yard, pressure washing)**
- SB Mowing (lawn care; YouTube plus short-form): about 40M total followers and about 3B annual views (the author calls these "conservative estimates"). One featured video has about 23M views. The format is time-lapse before/after cleanups of neglected properties, done free as a good deed, with a pressure-washing spinoff. The writer's thesis is that lawn care is a commodity, so the content becomes the value: "give people what they want to see rather than what you want to tell them". The article does not say whether the account is faceless. — [Why Is This Interesting (Colin Nagy), Dec 3 2024](https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-viral-yard-cleanup-edition)
- Yard Maintenance Geelong (Ben Webster, Australia; TikTok): 51K followers, 398K likes, 1.6M views on its top video. Format: short videos documenting each yard transformation, using trending sounds, voiceover explaining techniques and equipment experiments, and location pin tags to reach local customers. Results: inbound local leads plus brand deals (Munns fertiliser, Cougar Footwear, Printed Workwear). — [TikTok for Business success story, undated (c. 2025/2026)](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/ar/inspiration/yard-maintenance-geelong)

### Inferences
- The two most strongly documented growth stories (Miss Excel and SB Mowing) share one trait: a single, instantly recognisable format repeated daily. Miss Excel uses a person dancing in front of a spreadsheet; SB Mowing uses a mess turning into clean. The format is the brand.
- Template sellers with documented revenue (ChartSheets, YourFrugalFriend) cite Pinterest, SEO and YouTube tutorials rather than viral short video. For a $2.99 product, search-intent channels may be the proven sales path, with short-form video serving reach and trust.
- Trades content wins by showing the job (transformation, satisfying process), not the spreadsheet. A pricing-calculator brand could borrow the mechanism: show a real job scenario and then the number reveal, rather than leading with the tool.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024 to 2026 press profile with numbers for a faceless spreadsheet or Excel account, a junk-removal or pressure-washing pricing educator, a freelancer-rate educator or an Etsy-seller educator. Searches returned TikTok discover pages, tool-vendor blogs (Zipper, Zoho) and listicles without named accounts or numbers.
- No source gave posting cadence or typical video length for the Favikon-listed Excel creators.
- The AOL/Yahoo piece "Meet the TikTok Money Experts Who Popularized Cash Stuffing" could not be fetched (fetch error), so the named cash-stuffing creators and their formats are unverified.
- Miss Excel's specific hook wording and on-screen text conventions are not described in any source fetched.

## 2. Which formats repeat across winners?

### Takeaway
The repeating formats in documented cases are short single-tip tutorials with a strong persona device (Miss Excel's green screen and dance), transformation and before/after videos with voiceover explaining the work (yard and lawn), and a free tip leading to a lead magnet and then a paid product. The other formats in the brief (POV text, rate reveals, comment replies, stitches, carousels) were not documented with named examples and numbers in the sources I could reach.

### Cited Findings
- Green screen over the software, plus a trend (dance or audio), in a roughly 14-second single tip, posted daily. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown); [Binghamton News, 2022, PRE-2024](https://www.binghamton.edu/news/story/3978/how-to-excel-in-business-miss-excels-journey-from-business-student-to-social-media-star)
- Transformation time-lapse (neglected property to clean) as the core structure of a channel reported at about 40M followers. — [Why Is This Interesting, Dec 2024](https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-viral-yard-cleanup-edition)
- Transformation, trending sound, voiceover explaining technique and kit, and a location tag produced local leads and sponsorships for a small gardening business. — [TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/ar/inspiration/yard-maintenance-geelong)
- "Quick, humorous Excel tips and shortcuts", "daily free tips" and "short Excel tips" are the repeated descriptors for the large Excel accounts. — [Favikon, 2025](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-excel-office-productivity-influencers-worldwide)
- Finance faceless accounts are described as using charts, screen recordings, Canva and CapCut. The source flags YMYL/regulatory risk and gives no named accounts or data ("the description is the format pattern, not a specific account endorsement"). — [faceless.my, Apr 2026](https://faceless.my/tiktok/faceless-tiktok-accounts/)

### Inferences
- "Satisfying transformation" and "number reveal" are structurally the same: tension (a mess, or a price that looks fine) followed by resolution (clean, or the real number). ProofNotFluff's "$40 looks fine, you need $74.65" hook already has the reveal structure. What is missing compared with the winners is a visible real-world stake (a job, a person, a lawn) and a recognisable persona device.

### Gaps
- No sourced view counts for "how much I charged for this job", rate-reveal, comment-reply, stitch, or hands-only keyboard formats in this niche. Searches surfaced only TikTok tag and discover pages, which were not scraped per the constraints.

## 3. What earns trust and follows in professional niches?

### Takeaway
In the documented cases, trust came from showing real work (actual properties, actual Excel skills), daily consistency in one format, and free value given before the ask. Miss Excel's first business lead came from video 6, purely from demonstrating competence.

### Cited Findings
- Daily posting of real skill demos led to an inbound corporate-training lead after the 6th video. — [Starter Story, 2024](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/miss-excel-breakdown)
- Free videos act as a sample of the paid courses. — [Binghamton News, 2022, PRE-2024](https://www.binghamton.edu/news/story/3978/how-to-excel-in-business-miss-excels-journey-from-business-student-to-social-media-star)
- Free good-deed work shown in full; "the content became the value" for a commodity service. — [Why Is This Interesting, Dec 2024](https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-viral-yard-cleanup-edition)
- Voiceover explaining technique and equipment experiments (showing expertise, not just the result) produced inbound local leads and brand deals. — [TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/ar/inspiration/yard-maintenance-geelong)
- Most top Excel creators pair short tips with free resources (eBook, mini-course, newsletter, daily tips) before paid products. — [Favikon, 2025](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-excel-office-productivity-influencers-worldwide)

### Inferences
- For a faceless brand, "showing work" means real scenarios with specific, checkable numbers and the working shown. ProofNotFluff's free web calculators fit the "free sample" role that Miss Excel's free videos and the Favikon creators' free eBooks play.

### Gaps
- No 2024 to 2026 survey or platform data specific to trust signals (credentials, comment replies) in small-business finance content was found.

## 4. Which faceless finance or business accounts succeeded, and what did they use instead of a face? Is there evidence on AI avatars or AI voice?

### Takeaway
I found no sourced named faceless finance or spreadsheet account with documented growth from 2024 to 2026. The closest documented "low-face" successes are trades transformation channels that use the work as the visual and a human voiceover. The evidence on AI is cautionary. YouTube tightened monetisation against mass-produced, repetitive content from July 15, 2025. A TikTok study found mandatory AI disclosure labels reduce engagement and identification with the creator, and surveys show most adults avoid AI-influencer content.

### Cited Findings
- Low-face substitute: the job itself as the visual, plus a human voiceover explaining technique, gave 51K followers and 1.6M views on the top video. — [TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/ar/inspiration/yard-maintenance-geelong)
- YouTube, effective July 15, 2025: updated YPP guidelines aim to "better identify mass-produced and repetitious content". Templated, highly repetitive formats (especially Shorts) will be reviewed. Gulf News lists among those likely affected "automated channels that use AI to mass-produce videos with generic voiceovers" and "faceless channels lacking a distinct human voice or creative direction". Possible penalties: video demonetisation, removal from YPP, or suspension. Note that the "likely affected" list is the reporter's interpretation, not YouTube's wording. — [Gulf News, Jul 9 2025](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- A TikTok AI-disclosure study (Carney, Riveros, Tully; MSI Report 24-154) analysed TikTok responses before and after AI disclosure became mandatory, plus experiments. It found that the "mere presence" of AI disclosures "can reduce audience engagement with content and identification with creators". Effect sizes are not on the summary page. — [Marketing Science Institute, Dec 20 2024](https://msi.org/working-paper/made-with-ai-consumer-engagement-with-media-containing-ai-disclosures)
- Consumer wariness: 63% of Gen X and Boomers would avoid AI-influencer content, and 51% of Gen Z and millennials are reluctant (YouGov, via eMarketer). 32% see generative AI as a negative disruptor in the creator economy, up from 18% in 2023 (Billion Dollar Boy). Sample sizes are not given in the article. — [Communicate Online, Dec 22 2025](https://communicateonline.me/news/youtube-tests-ai-creator-avatars-as-consumers-remain-wary-of-virtual-influencers-2/)
- Faceless finance formats are described generically (charts, screen recordings, Canva/CapCut), with no named accounts or numbers. — [faceless.my, Apr 2026](https://faceless.my/tiktok/faceless-tiktok-accounts/)

### Inferences
- ProofNotFluff's current setup (code-rendered demos plus a TTS voice, highly templated) matches the profile that the 2025 YouTube policy and the TikTok disclosure research flag as risky for reach and trust. A real human voice, varied real-world scenarios, and a distinct creative device (character, hands, a recurring series name) are the lower-risk substitutes for a face.
- Treat 2026 "faceless AI avatar $10K/year" guides (e.g. Barchart-syndicated content, which could not be fetched) as promotional, not evidence.

### Gaps
- No sourced case found of a named AI-avatar or AI-voice business/finance account that grew, with its labelling and audience response documented.
- No sourced named faceless budget account ("budget with me" hands-only cash stuffing) with follower data and format specifics was retrieved; the AOL cash-stuffing profile failed to load.
- I did not search for a YouTube clarification that AI voiceover alone is permitted. Gulf News does not include one.
