# Cold email research, Oct 22, 2026 (growth-research Phase A)

Full write-up: the Claude Doc "ProofNotFluff growth research, Oct 22, 2026". Sources are named by publication only. No full email is copied here; structures are described in our words.

## Decisions (Todd, Oct 22, 2026, live chat)
- "A and B, test 1": templates.md carries variant A (bare observation and permission ask) and B (adds "I follow city building permits for commercial cleaning crews." or "exterior cleaning crews") for every trade and stage.
- Copy gate change approved: Variant line, one template per trade, stage and letter, `variant` on every draft, fixed split on the prospect id, follow-ups keep the arm.
- "Yes, two follow-ups": at most two, 5 business days apart, each with a lead the prospect was never sent. Oct 19 and 20 batches still get none.
- Held for test 2: variant C, the ask ends "plus a few more jobs like it?" (on a yes we owe a few free jobs).

## Test 1 plan
- A vs B, split by sha256 of the prospect id (pnf_rules.assign_variant). Subject, ask and follow-ups identical.
- Read human replies per first email (follow-up replies count to the first email's arm) 2 to 3 weeks after the last send. A winner needs a lead of 3 or more human replies; otherwise keep A.
- At 1 to 6% reply rates, about 100 owners per arm separates only a big gap. The speed limit is named owners from the finder, not senders.

## Baseline
- Grow Surely, Oct 16, 2026 (6.07M emails): human replies per email, cleaning and janitorial 1.2% (0.18% positive), restaurants 1.0%, home services and trades 0.6%. https://growsurely.com/blog/cold-email-reply-rate-benchmarks
- Our 61 first emails of Oct 19 and 20 (40 to role inboxes, about 115 words plus an ad footer, price, link) would expect under one reply at that rate.

## Patterns, with the sources that carry them
- Name the record in plain words in line 1: BuildZoom blog (Apr 2025), BounceZero (Jul 2026), Deepline (Jul 2026), Permitpub (Jul 2026); Hunter 2026, two personal details 5.6% vs none 3.6%.
- No pitch in the first email: Gong with Outbound Squad (Jul 2025, 25M plus emails), pitching cuts replies up to 57%. Anti-pattern: an 800-email plumber campaign with a feature pitch and 0 replies (Jul 2025 newsletter).
- Under about 50 words: Smartlead benchmarks (850M plus), under 50 words 1.47% vs 100 to 149 words 1.03%; Lavender (Feb 2026) 0 to 50 words plus 23%. Hunter 2026 finds length matters little.
- One yes or no or permission ask: Gong Labs (304,174 emails), interest CTA beats a time ask on first touch; SalesBread case on the Mailshake blog (Feb 2026), yes or no plus why-them took replies from 9.8% to 18% (local owners, per-arm counts not given).
- Small batches and follow-ups: Hunter 2026 (31M emails sent in 2025), 21 to 50 recipients 6.2% vs 500 plus 2.4%, tracking off 7.4% vs on 4.4%, three messages 6.8% vs one 3.3%; Belkins (7.5M emails, 2025), 58.6% of replies from steps 2 to 6.
- Free, concrete offer by permission: Deepline (list from inspection records, offered by permission), Growth Engine X via Cold Outbound (Jul 2025, free list then a weekly subscription ask).
