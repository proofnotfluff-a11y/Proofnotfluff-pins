## 1. Reach: what can and cannot be raised
- Single-video virality is not predictable before posting. MusicLab: the same song landed very differently across 8 identical worlds (https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/research/papers/years/2006/salganik2006a.pdf, 2006). Twitter cascades: the best models explain under half the variance (https://www.arxiv.org/abs/1602.01013, 2016).
- What lifts odds on average: emotional strength, practical use, surprise (https://cssh.northeastern.edu/pandemic-teaching-initiative/wp-content/uploads/sites/43/2020/09/What-Makes-Online-Content-Viral.pdf, 2012; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10541009, Sep 2023, found total emotion strength mattered more than arousal).
- Winners show early: early reshare speed predicted cascade doubling about 78% of the time (https://phys.org/news/2014-04-scientists-photos-viral.pdf, 2014). So read each post at a few hours and day 1, remake breakouts.
- Volume lifts the tail, not the median: Buffer, 11.4M TikTok posts, each account against itself: 90th percentile 3,722 to 14,401 views from 1 a week to 11+ a week; median flat at 459 to 506 (https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/, Oct 8, 2025). Instagram: reach per post about 24% higher at 10+ posts a week, diminishing returns (https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/, Aug 13, 2025).
- Shorts count every replay as a view since Mar 31, 2025; judge on engaged views (https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/).
- Professional data Shorts: 50 UK newsroom data videos all used a voice; 33 had a chart; average just under 84 s (https://onlinejournalismblog.com/2026/08/10/how-data-journalism-is-done-on-tiktok-six-key-takeaways-from-watching-50-videos/, Aug 10, 2026). No dated growth numbers found for a faceless local data account.

## 2. What the platforms punish
- YouTube monetization policy (Jul 15, 2025) names image slideshows, templated storylines, scrolling text with little narrative and generic AI templates as inauthentic, channel-wide (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392). Spam policy bars AI churn of near-identical videos; a few variations are fine (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973). Jan 2026 CEO letter: cutting the spread of low-quality repetitive content (https://au.variety.com/2026/digital/news/youtube-channels-using-ai-tools-reduce-slop-neal-mohan-letter-32190/).
- Meta (Mar 13, 2026): original means produced by the account owner; borders, captions or speed changes on someone else's clip are low value; mostly unoriginal accounts can lose recommendations (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/).
- TikTok: reposts and other apps' watermarks are kept out of For You (https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards); 1.3B+ videos labeled AI and a user control to see less AI (https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en, Nov 19, 2025).
- Instagram's AI-generated profile label applies only to accounts featuring an AI-made person (https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label, Aug 31, 2026).

## 8. Deep check: is the structure dated? (Oct 27, 2026, evening; Todd: "I don't want to go down the path of building a dated structure")
Five more agents (about 200 pages; raw files data/work/social-engine/F to J) and an independent checker on the 14 load-bearing sources (data/work/social-engine/K_check.md: 10 hold, 4 hold in part, 0 wrong). Verdict: the core (own data, story finder in code, Remotion, code gates, fresh reviewers, learning only from real results) is current and ahead of published pipelines; six parts were dated and changed in the blueprint.
- Variants and judging: generate 3 to 5 hooks, pick two at a time. Persona panels picked real winners 34.6% of the time vs 49.2% for a plain prompt and 30.4% chance (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25010); absolute scores tied in 67% of pairs and captured 21% of the gain vs 61.2% for pairwise (https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12520v1). Send test is now a no-persona pairwise check against a reference video, used to cut the weakest, and calibrated against results after 20 to 30 posts.
- Render critic: a critic watching the render helps (removing it dropped Code2Video's scores, https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01174v1); layout checks in code beat a vision critic on geometry (https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.18116.md). Claude reviews 5 frames a second plus the word-timed transcript; Gemini with sound is optional at about $0.045 a 45 s video until Dec 31, 2026 (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing).
- Length: Reels median views peak at 45 to 60 s (10,374) across 9.77M brand Reels, Jan to Jun 2026, pooled and not controlled for account size (https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/); TikTok median views peak at 2 to 3 min (https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos/); Shorts average about 16 s viewed and 2 to 4 a week works best per Short (https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/). MVP pin moved to 45 to 60 s.
- Search: Shorts are 5.7% of YouTube citations in AI answers (https://otterly.ai/blog/the-youtube-citation-study-2026/); in Google AI Overviews 17.5%, and about half link to a timestamp (https://help.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/google-ai-overviews-chatgpt-youtube-citations). Google indexes video only on a watch page (https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9495631?hl=en). YouTube becomes the search home from Phase 1 (Short plus a 60 to 120 s explainer with chapters); watch pages for top findings in Phase 2.
- Platform changes: Instagram originality judged over a rolling 30 days (https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram); YouTube warned on re-uploads and minor edits Oct 1 (press only, no effective date); Meta One puts Instagram links on a paid tier, Sep 15 (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/). Trial Reels via API confirmed (trial_params MANUAL or SS_PERFORMANCE); the 200-follower bar is third-party only, to check in the app.
- Native AI tools: none can be driven by code; Edits gives a small, temporary reach bump (https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-encourages-use-edits-app-short-term-boost-9647455). Paid AI scene clips stay behind a watch-time test.
- Voice: ElevenLabs now lists v3 as its previous generation (https://elevenlabs.io/docs/de/overview/models); pin Eleven v4.
