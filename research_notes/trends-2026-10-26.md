# Google Trends: free tools and pages for the trades (Oct 26, 2026)

Live chat, Todd's ask: brainstorm free tools and content from what is trending on Google Trends to pull traffic from the trades. Research only; nothing built or published. Write-up for Todd: Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/7gKKssDBPgHzGjDfx3GZyV

## Method
- Google Trends Explore, United States, past 5 years (262 weekly points to the week of Oct 25, 2026), read as page text in the built-in browser. 12 comparisons of 5 terms; "cleaning business" is the reference term in most of them. WebFetch is refused on /trends/explore (robots); no scripted Google search or autocomplete (coo/PIVOT.md).
- Columns below: last-52-week average, prior-52-week average, change, peak month (average of each calendar month over 5 years). Values are Google's 0 to 100 index within each comparison, not search counts, so compare within a row, not across rows.
- Caveat: since early 2025 most "<trade> business" terms rose 70% to 110% together and fell back after July 2026. The rising related queries for "cleaning business" (past 12 months) were travel queries (Paris attractions, Tokyo flights, Maldives), a sign of automated or AI-agent searches. Trust the seasonal shape and moves against the reference term, not raw growth.
- Trending Now RSS (US, Oct 25): no trade terms.
