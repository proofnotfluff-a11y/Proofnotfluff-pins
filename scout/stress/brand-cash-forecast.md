# Stress test verdict: done-for-you cash forecast for small online brands (Oct 29, 2026)

Brief: scout/stress/brand-cash-forecast/brief.md. Memos: offers.md, monopoly.md, product.md. Numbers: cfo.md (from numbers.json). No buyer panel ran. Nothing built, priced, published or sent.

## Vote
- Offers lens 6, FUND IF. Monopoly lens 5, FUND IF. Product lens 6, FUND IF. Average 5.7. No lens voted PASS.

## Risks more than one lens raised
1. Belief: no client result and no sample, so a buyer cannot see what they get before paying (offers, product).
2. Reach: the eligible count among the 15 is unknown once supplement and wellness brands, anyone tied to the former employer and brands below the band are left out (offers, monopoly).
3. Buyer effort: pulling exports from several platforms is the step most likely to stall the start and the monthly plan (offers, product).
4. Whose name: trust comes from the owner's own record, but PNF is set up as faceless; a split between note, invoice and file weakens it (all three).

## Merged conditions
- [ ] Count eligible contacts before any note goes out.
- [ ] One offer in one sentence: the 13-week forecast and what it flags. The channel P&L waits for month two.
- [ ] Position on cash stuck in staggered platform payouts (Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop), never as a cheaper CFO.
- [ ] One sample forecast on made-up data, shown at the yes step or in the note.
- [ ] A one-page export checklist per platform, tried on a real export; on the monthly plan the owner chases late data. Agents never log in to a client account.
- [ ] Name and billing route fixed before the first note and the same on note, invoice and file.
- [ ] Only a cheap guarantee, such as on time or the fee back (terms to check).
- [ ] Turn each job into a reusable payout timing playbook, with no client data in the repo (monopoly).
- [ ] The decisions are flags for the founder and their accountant, not advice.
- [ ] Price set only by the owner in a live chat (CONSTITUTION 1).

## Numbers (cfo.md)
- At the proposed $1,250 a month, one client-month leaves $1,124.50 after Gumroad's 10% plus $0.50, and one client covers the $1,000 month.
- At a 20% lower price a client-month leaves $899.50 and it takes 2 clients to reach the $1,000 month. The ramp in numbers.json is a test target, not data; the result that matters is whether 1 of about 15 notes turns into a paid invoice.

## Changes, ranked (each a yes or no for the owner)
1. Sell under the owner's own name with ProofNotFluff LLC as the billing entity. Expected effect: belief, the weakest value score (3 of 10), rises. Cost: $0; the faceless line in MAP.md changes for this offer only.
2. Build one sample forecast on made-up data before any note. Expected effect: the buyer sees the result before paying. Cost: owner or agent hours (to check).
3. Guarantee the first forecast: on time or the fee back (terms to check). Expected effect: lowers the buyer's money risk. Cost: the fee refunded if late, plus the owner's 6 to 10 hours on that forecast (estimate).
