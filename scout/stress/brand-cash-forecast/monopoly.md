# Monopoly lens: done-for-you cash forecast (Oct 30, 2026)

**Score: 5 of 10. Vote: FUND IF**

## Assumptions
1. Small brands have cash pain a tool alone does not fix.
2. A person accountable each month is worth paying for, despite cheap templates.
3. The warm list holds enough eligible founders in the revenue band.
4. Multichannel payout timing is a gap generalists handle badly.
5. Delivery fits owner hours.

## What is strong
- A small market to own first: multichannel brands with no finance lead and cash tied up in staggered platform payouts.
- The right team for that niche: an operator who built this forecast inside a multichannel brand.
- A possible secret: the cash gap sits in payout timing across channels, not the P&L. The free calculators already encode it.

## Top 3 killers
1. **No lasting edge.** A small step, not a breakthrough. AI templates make the model cheap; any fractional CFO or bookkeeper can copy it by month six. Undercutting those firms on price is cheapness, and the margin gets eaten.
2. **The first 10 cannot be reached.** Fifteen notes is the whole funnel. After excluding supplement and wellness brands and anyone tied to the former employer, the eligible count is unknown. PNF has 0 payers so far.
3. **It never grows past a job.** A cap near 5 clients means no scale and no network effect. Brand is the only edge left, and it clashes with PNF being faceless.
