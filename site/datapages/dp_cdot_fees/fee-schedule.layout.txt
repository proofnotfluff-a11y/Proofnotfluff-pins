  I.    Introduction
            Background

Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT), Division of Infrastructure Management (DIM) charges
permit fees for several different types of permits. The following document describes those permit fees.
Permit fees cover the cost of processing the permit, including support and maintenance for the permitting
database. Please be advised that permit fees may change year-to-year. It is incumbent upon permittees
to review the Permit Fee Schedule prior to applying for a permit. By applying for a permit, permittees
acknowledge and accept the fees posted in the Permit Fee Schedule. Furthermore, permittees must
comply with all applicable rules and regulations related to public right of way activity including the
Municipal Code of Chicago (MCC) and Rules and Regulations for Construction in the Public Way. Please
visit the CDOT Permit Portal for additional information.

             3. Monthly Usage Fees (Type I, II, and III)


                                               NOTES
       i.     Activity Types (see MCC 10-28-281.3)
                  a. Type I activity “shall mean work on a building which involves alteration,
                      repair of exterior facade, work conducted pursuant to the critical
                      examination program, or demolition. This includes, but is not limited to,
                      tuckpointing.”
                  b. Type II activity “shall mean work which involves the construction of a new
                      building.”
                  c. Type III activity “shall mean any routine maintenance work on a building
                      within the Central Business District, as defined in Section 9-4-010, which
                      does not require the removal of any physical structures. This includes, but
                      is not limited to, painting, cleaning and window washing.”

       ii.    The fees for obstruction are based on the length of the obstruction and the number
              of days the obstruction occupies the public right of way. Obstruction permits have
              a minimum monthly fee.

      iii.    All Occupy permits are issued for 30-day increments.

      iv.     Length is calculated using each foot of frontage.

       v.     Permittees will be charged for any metered spaces rendered unusable due to their
              occupation. See MCC § 9-68-080 for more information.

      vi.     The fees in the table below shall be subject to an annual CPI increase of up to 2.5%,
              rounded up to the nearest whole dollar.

III.    Additional Fees
            Degradation Fees

CDOT assess a degradation fee for any project involving cuts or trenches on a “Moratorium Street”-streets
that require additional protection, including newly paved streets. CDOT approves permits on moratorium
streets on a case-by-case basis upon request.
