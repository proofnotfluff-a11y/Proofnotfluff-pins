# Shorts tool

`python3 tools/make_slides.py spec.json out.mp4` builds a 1080x1920 YouTube Short in the slideshow style: five full-screen slides of text, each on screen for whole bars of the music (about 5.3 seconds), no voiceover. The music is a track from the library (tools/music, see Music below): the spec may name `"music"` or `"mood"`, the lift lands on the stat slide and the ending plus the sonic logo on the call to action. Every frame is rendered with Playwright at 30 fps, so motion is smooth. Needs numpy, scipy, ffmpeg, the pre-installed Playwright Chromium, and the Poppins Bold and Carlito fonts. About 90 seconds per video.

Spec: `theme` ("ink" or "paper"), `footer`, and `slides`, a list of five in this order:

1. `hook`: `kicker`, `title_html` (one `<span>` around the accent number)
2. `compare`: `kicker`, `left` and `right` as `{label, value, kind}`, `note`
3. `stat`: `kicker`, `label`, `value`, `kind` ("" or "bad" or "good"), `sub`
4. `fix`: `kicker`, `title`, `formula`, `sub`
5. `cta`: `title`, `button` (the product name), `sub` ("On Etsy: etsy.com/shop/ProofNotFluff", typed out because searching the shop name on Etsy only shows a small "did you mean the shop" link. Never point to the description: YouTube made Shorts description links unclickable in 2023. The tool rewrites any CTA that does.)

See example-slides-spec.json. Each slide teaches one number from the day's first pin; the product is named once, on the last slide. No em dashes anywhere.

Upload: commit the mp4 as shorts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.mp4, confirm with curl that the raw URL returns video/mp4, then run the Zapier YouTube "Upload Video" action with the raw URL as the video, privacy public, category Education, tags from the pin, and description = the pin description, the listing link on its own line, then "#Shorts".

## Etsy listing videos: make_listing_video.py

`python3 make_listing_video.py <short-spec>.json out.mp4 [--slides 0,2,4]`

Turns a Short's spec into an Etsy listing video: the hook, the first stat or compare slide (else the fix), and the CTA, 4.6 s each (13.8 s), 1080 x 2160 (1:2), no audio track. The CTA line becomes "Instant digital download" (set `listing_cta_sub` in the spec to change it), since the viewer is already on Etsy. Content sits in the middle third, so it still reads when Etsy crops the gallery frame.

Etsy's rules, checked Oct 4, 2026 (help.etsy.com, "How to Add Listing Videos"): 3 to 15 seconds, sound removed, 100 MB max, 1080 px recommended, 2:1 or 1:2, up to 2 videos per listing. The script refuses output that breaks the length or size limit.

## Product demo videos: make_demo.py (the main promo format from Oct 4, 2026; rebuilt Oct 13)

`python3 make_demo.py spec.json out.mp4 --format short|walkthrough|listing`

Shows the product's calculator on screen from frame one, types the inputs in, reveals the result cell (count-up), then an optional "fix" result and an Etsy end card. Captions are burned in. Every frame is rendered from a timeline, so motion is exact.

- `short`: 1080x1920. YouTube Short, Instagram Reel, TikTok, Pinterest video pin. The voice says the hook over frame one (`hook_say_short`, else the headline, else its first sentence when the whole one runs past 4 s). Layout keeps everything the viewer needs between y=270 and y=1250, the band no app covers (Reels covers about the top 14% and bottom 35%; TikTok the top 10% and bottom 20%), and out of the right 150 px: the kicker at y=274, the headline at 310 to 466, the sheet in a 576 px window (482 to 1058), captions at 1066 to 1218 and the note at 1226. The end card's text box stops at x=900 before its 3% zoom (x=924 after), and the render stops with an error if any end-card text reaches the right 150 px (Oct 13: the #20 PM Short was blocked at review for cta.what at x=951). A camera frames the whole sheet during the hook, follows the row being typed (the window always holds whole rows, never a row cut through its text; other rows dim to 42%), lands on the key result, then pulls back for the fix so the problem and the fix share one frame. The cursor fades once a value is typed so it never sits on the number. Narration runs at speed 1.12.
- `walkthrough`: 1920x1080, paced to reading speed (about 15 characters a second), uses each row's `why` caption and speaks `hook_say`. Upload as a regular YouTube video; every Short's related-video link points to it, and line one of its description is the listing URL.
- `listing`: 1080x2160, no audio, 15 s max, end card says "Instant digital download". Etsy listing video.

Spec fields are documented at the top of make_demo.py; an example is promo/specs/demo-13-hourly-rate.json. New optional fields (Oct 15): a row's `ask` (a question spoken and captioned after it) and `pause` (seconds held after it, default 1.2) for the Spot the Mistake and Myth vs Math formulas in cmo/CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md; a close spoken as "... dot com" shows as ".com" on the end card. Earlier optional fields: `mood` (calm, upbeat, playful, driving, tension: picks the music family), `music` (one track id), `palette` (ink, night, plum: background and sheet bar; red and green keep their meaning), `hook_say_short`, `voice_speed`. The end card is opaque and shows cta.say as text, the product name and cta.what (required when the close says "workbook"; e.g. "The full workbook for Excel and Google Sheets"). The tool refuses specs with em dashes, banned words, no result row, more than 7 rows, or a CTA that points to an unclickable link. Every number must come from the product's own files and the CMO review checks this before anything posts.

The key reveal is the result row that shows the headline's `<b>` number (else the first "bad" result, else the last result). Each reveal (every result, the fix, the end card) is moved onto the music's beat, never into the line before it; the music's lift lands on the key reveal, with 0.6 s before the voice so it is heard; the music keeps going under the closing line and its ending chord and the sonic logo land on the first beat after the last word, then ring 1.6 s.

make_demo.py prints JSON with `music`, `palette`, `moments` (hook length, key reveal, fix, end card, ending) and `sound`: `lufs` (target -14), `true_peak_dbtp` (-2 before AAC, under -1 after), `music_under_voice_lu` (about 14). `spoken` lists every narrated line, the hook first; `captions` the on-screen ones.

## Music and sound: tools/music and soundtrack.py (Oct 13)

Every video used to carry the same generated loop (one seed, one chord sequence), mixed at -24 LUFS with a 1.5 s silent start. Now:
- `tools/music/tracks/`: 18 original tracks composed in code by `tools/music/compose.py` and rendered with the FluidR3 GM soundfont (MIT licence): no third-party recordings, samples or melodies, so no rights holder can claim them. Each is intro (2 bars), A (8 bars, a drum fill on its last bar), B (8 bars, the lift, 2 dB up) and a 2-bar ending, at -16 LUFS, 80 kbps Opus. `tools/music/library.json` holds tempo, key, moods, bar length and sections. Moods: calm 5, tension 5, upbeat 5, driving 6, playful 3. `python3 tools/soundtrack.py --list` prints them. Re-rendering the library needs fluidsynth and the soundfont (compose.py's docstring); the video runs only read the files.
- `tools/soundtrack.py` picks the track (the spec's `music`, else a spec rendered before keeps its track, else the mood's track used longest ago) and logs it as `tools/music/used/<spec>.json` (one small file per spec, so two runs never edit the same file; the newest 60 count). Commit the folder with the run's other changes so the next video rotates from it. It cuts the track to the picture (A loops under the setup, B lands on the key reveal, the ending right after the spoken close, 30 ms crossfades), adds light keystroke ticks, a pop on each reveal, a chime in the track's key on the fix and a whoosh into the end card, and plays the sonic logo (tonic, fifth, octave on vibraphone and celesta, in the track's key) with the ending chord. That logo is the brand's constant sound; the beds vary.
- Narration: each line trimmed to 30 ms of silence, high-passed, a little less boom and a little more presence for phone speakers, a 3:1 voice compressor, then set to -15 LUFS. Music sits about 14 LU under the voice while it speaks and comes up in the longer pauses, around the key reveal and at the ending (Torcoli et al., AES 2019: at least 10 LU, general listeners prefer about 4 more). The master lands on -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube's playback reference; YouTube never turns quiet videos up) with a 4x oversampled true-peak limiter at -2 dBTP (AAC adds up to about 1 dB), and the video carries 48 kHz stereo AAC at 192 kbps.
- Research and sources: research_notes/Short video audio and edit best practice/ (audio_music.md, edit_and_format.md), Oct 13, 2026.

## legal_scan.py (Legal desk first pass)

```
python3 tools/legal_scan.py <product.zip | folder> [--listing listing.md] [--tier high|medium|low]
```
Unpacks nested zips and checks every file for: Todd's personal details in text or metadata, promise wording ("guarantee", "pass any ATS", "IRS-approved"), words to read in context ("certified", "official"), em dashes and banned words, review comments and tracked changes, hidden sheets, cached error cells, a missing short notice or Terms of Use, no as-of date, and (with --listing) the required listing lines. Exit code 1 means a high finding. It never clears a product by itself: the reviewer still reads every page against legal/README.md.

## etsy_oauth.py (Etsy API connection; cloud session on the Default environment only)

`link` prints the approval URL; Todd approves; `exchange CODE STATE` stores the refresh token in the private vault repo (`/home/claude/proofnotfluff-vault/etsy.json`); `me` is the read-only test and records user_id and shop_id; `refresh` rotates the token (commit and push the vault after every refresh). The keystring comes from the ETSY_KEYSTRING environment variable and the x-api-key header is added by the environment's API credential, so neither appears in the repo or the output. If openapi.etsy.com is taken off that credential (it appeared to drop the Bearer token, Oct 5, 2026), set ETSY_SHARED_SECRET in the environment and the script sends `x-api-key: keystring:secret` itself.

## etsy_list.py (list a packed product through the Etsy API; Default environment only)

`inspect` and `taxonomy` and `show` are read-only. `check listing.md` validates the builder's listing.md (title length, 13 tags of 20 characters, price ladder, AI disclosure and digital-download lines, no em dashes, category in the ledger rule) without calling Etsy. `create listing.md product.zip img1..img5 [--video mp4] [--publish]` makes a draft, uploads images, the digital file and the optional video, and publishes only with `--publish`. who_made, when_made and the other required fields are copied from the newest live listing so new listings match what Etsy already accepted. The scheduled API lister (agents/api-lister.md) runs it after each Legal desk run. Categories: when the ledger category name matches several Etsy nodes ("Templates" matches 3), the node the like-listing uses wins; a trade edition's listing.md carries a `Like: <parent listing id>` line under `## Category` so it lands in its parent's exact category (pressure washing uses #13, 4587962930, node 1874). Bundles (Todd, Oct 6): N products x $2.99 less 20%, rounded down (`bundle-price 3` gives 7.17); listing.md says `Bundle: 3 products (#5, #6, #7)` under ## Price and check enforces the exact price. `strip-line LISTING_ID "phrase" [--dry-run]` is the correction edit for retired description lines (meta/lister.descriptionFixes).

## etsy_page1_check.js (BUYER EVIDENCE GATE, Oct 7)
Paste into the browser's javascript tool on a signed-out `https://www.etsy.com/search?q=<phrase>` page (one page load inside the run's Etsy cap; it clicks nothing). It returns page-1 organic and ad counts, how many organic listings are under 60 and under 180 days old (from listing ids), how many carry Bestseller or Popular now badges, and PASS or FAIL with the reason. PASS needs buyer proof (3+ badged or 5+ older than 180 days), no flood (under 40% under 60 days old) and room to enter (2+ under 180 days). Record the result on the idea as buyerProof; cloud builders build only PASS ideas checked in the last 14 days. Set `window.PNF_OURS` to our listing ids first to see where ours rank.

## spec_lint.py (Oct 15, 2026)

`python3 tools/spec_lint.py spec.json [...]`: the content playbook's hard rules for a Proof Check short (cmo/CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md sections 2 to 4), checked in code before the cold viewer sees the video: formula and series, the kicker, hook_say_short at most 8 words, 1 or 2 numbers in the headline, 4 to 6 rows, one ask beat at 1.2 to 1.5 s (F2: "Spot the mistake?" then "The mistake..."; F3: a question), F4's "free calculator", the "<verb> free at ProofNotFluff dot com." close spoken once, the end card wording, no em dash, no invented people, the Reel and TikTok caption lines. From Oct 16 every dated short spec needs a formula, and (the pivot, coo/PIVOT.md) a kicker naming an e-commerce audience, the end card "Free online. No sign-up." or a premium line at $29 to $199, and captions that point at proofnotfluff.com with no Etsy link. Every spec is also checked for hype and fake urgency ("secret", "stolen", "last chance", "only 3 left"; playbook rule 11). make_demo.py runs it on every `--format short` render and refuses a spec that fails; the code gate runs it on every committed spec as the video-specs check.
