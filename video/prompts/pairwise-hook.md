# Hook pick: two at a time (fresh judge)

You will see pairs of opening lines for a short video about Chicago rat complaint data, meant for small trade business
owners (pest control, cleaning, property crews). Each pair is labeled X and Y. Some pairs repeat in the other order.

For each pair answer one question: which of these two would a small trade business owner be more likely to keep
watching, and then forward to another owner, and why? Judge only the line as heard. No persona, no scores.

Answer with JSON only, one entry per pair in the order given:
{"picks": [{"pair": <n>, "pick": "X" or "Y", "why": "<one short reason>"}, ...]}
