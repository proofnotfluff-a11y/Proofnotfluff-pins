## What they mean in practice (until the rebuild replaces the lower rules)
- Listen first: every story starts from what owners are saying now (forums, Reddit, Facebook groups, YouTube comments,
  trade press) or from what the AI-for-trades voices teach, distilled for an owner. Cite where it came from.
- One message a stranger can repeat in one sentence, said plainly early. Setup before payoff: who, the problem, then the turn.
- Coherence beats compression: word caps and beat timings are guides, never reasons to squeeze a sentence.
- Human voice: one owner talking to another, "you" and "I", a side taken. Disclaimers live in one caption line, not the voice.
- A video that a fresh listener cannot retell in one sentence, or that sounds choppy read aloud, does not pass, whatever the
  other gates say.
