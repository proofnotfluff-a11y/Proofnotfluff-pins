# Video rebuild plan (Nov 2, 2026; from PRINCIPLES.md)

Diagnosis: the scripts were built to pass rules (word caps, a belief flipped by 5 s, result-first openings, a format's beats,
a claims table), so sentences got squeezed and the story fell apart. Seven AI gates graded rule-following, not whether a
person understands or cares: the buyer gate said stop, send and save "yes" on a video real viewers left after 8 to 9 s
(research/PERFORMANCE.md). Topics were generic AI tips with nothing only we know.

## Quality, rebuilt (the gates that matter)
1. Facts (kept): every claim true and sourced.
2. Retell (new): a fresh agent hears only the transcript, never the brief, and must say the message in one sentence; it has
   to match the story's message line, or the video goes back.
3. Read-aloud (new): every sentence follows from the one before; no line needs something the viewer was never told; it
   sounds like one person talking. Choppy is a fail.
4. Compare (replaces buyer and golden): judged side by side against transcripts of 3 strong videos from the gurus file, not
   in a vacuum: "would I follow this account after this one?"
5. Real numbers decide: skip rate, average watch, shares and saves per video (research/PERFORMANCE.md) keep or kill a rule.
Relaxed: format word caps and beat timings become guides; the 5-second turn and result-first opening become options;
variety checks stay only where they stop repeats.

## Order of work
1. Build run (one session): `trade-pulse` skill and its first pulse, `gurus.md` first pass, `_shared/storycraft.md`, the
   story-desk upgrade, the retell and read-aloud gates, the relaxed rules, and one video end to end from a real pulse story,
   sent to Todd as a preview.
2. Second run: three more videos on that line; Todd watches them.
3. Then back to 2 a day on the new line; the evening read compares each new video with the old ones.
Open for Todd: whether queued videos made under the old gates still post, and when the build run happens.
