# Captions, keywords and tags (per platform)

front of the right audience." Evidence and sources: research/runs/2026-10-28-captions.md. Every lesson writes

## What decides reach
Viewers do (watch time, sends, likes, finishing). Text tells each platform what the video is about and which searches it
matches. So: the keyword in the first line, said out loud in the first seconds, and shown on screen.

## Keywords (buyers, not homeowners or marketers)
- Always "business" or "owner" with a trade: "[trade] business owner", "[trade] business tips", "how to get more leads for
  my [trade] business", "how to price a [trade] job", "home service business", "contractor business tips", "how to get
  more jobs as a contractor", "how to respond to leads" (with a trade word).
- Never as the main keyword: "plumbing tips", "HVAC tips" (DIY), "speed to lead" (marketers), "small business tips",
  "contractor marketing" alone (agencies).
- Each caption: one trade phrase (rotate trades through the week, lead with the lesson's example trade) plus one umbrella
  phrase (home service business or contractor business).

## Shape (all four)
1. Line 1, under 100 characters: the keyword phrase as a plain sentence that names the trades.
2. Proof line: the number, said exactly as the source supports it, with the source.
3. The takeaway or steps (the copyable part).
4. One send or save line ("Send this to whoever answers your phone.").
5. "Narrated by an AI version of my own voice."
6. Hashtags last.

## Per platform
- Instagram: 3 to 5 hashtags (cap 5): 1 umbrella (#homeservicebusiness or #contractorbusiness), 1 to 2 trade business
  (#plumbingbusiness, #hvacbusiness, #roofingbusiness), 1 topic. No link; "link in bio" is fine. AI label on. Share to
  Facebook off (Facebook gets its own post).
- TikTok: keyword in the first 70 characters, a question for owners, 3 to 5 hashtags, no #fyp/#foryou/#viral.
  AI-generated content on; commercial disclosure only when the video sells something. After posting, suggest the primary
  keyword and block off-target ones if the keyword panel shows.
- Facebook Page: 2 to 3 hashtags, no link, no capitals shouting. AI label on. Public.
- YouTube Shorts: title under 100 characters, keyword in the first 40 to 50, no hashtags in the title. Description: keyword
  line, proof with the full source, numbered steps, a next-lesson line, the AI voice line, 2 to 3 hashtags. Tags field 5
  to 10 (primary, secondary, trade variants, one common misspelling). Made for kids: No. AI use: Yes (YouTube exempts
  cloning one's own voice, but we disclose on every platform the same way; YouTube says it does not limit reach).
  Category Education. Language English.
- Never: generic or homeowner tags, more than 5 hashtags, a number not in facts.json, em or en dashes.
