Todd, live chat, Nov 1, 2026, 12:09 pm: "Let's go down to 2 videos a day until we get the formula and structure correct
to go viral. use the spare capacity to focus on developing quality videos".

Where we start (`anim.py lanes`, Oct 28 to Nov 1): 8 lessons, 2,662 views, 0 shares, 0 follows, 0 comments, 3 saves.
Facebook keeps 12 of 100 viewers past 3 s; TikTok average watch 8.9 s. Our best: lsa-missed-calls on Instagram, 29% skip

## One change a lesson
- brief.md names the one thing this lesson tries that the last ones did not: `- **Test:** <what> (<why>)`. One only, so
  the result can be read: the first frame, the hook line's pattern, the length, the save card, a share line, the lane.
- research/FORMULA.md gets a row per posted lesson at 24 h and 72 h: lane, test, 3 s hold (Facebook 3 s views per view),
  TikTok average watch and watched-full %, Instagram skip rate when shown, shares + saves + follows per 1,000 views.
- A test that beats the median of the 6 lessons before it on both hold and shares + saves + follows goes into
  `_shared/standard.md` as a line with its numbers; one that loses is written down and dropped. Two wins in a row on the
  same change make it the default.

## Reading small numbers (Nov 1, 2026 research)
At a few hundred views a lesson, 0 shares is what chance predicts (shares run a few tenths of a percent of views;
research/viral-science-2026-11-01.md), so one lesson's 0 says little. Read the stay rate first: in 5,400 Shorts,
viewed-vs-swiped under 60% rarely did well and 70 to 90% did best (Paddy Galloway, Apr 2023). Read shares + saves +
follows per 1,000 pooled over the week, not per lesson.

## Tests queued (Nov 1, 2026 research; take the first one not yet run, one a lesson)
1. **Skit cold open** (core lane): the host acts out one owner moment for 10 to 15 s (a lowball bid, a no-show, "just
   give me a ballpark") before the fix. Why: trade skit and personality accounts run 10 to 100x the size of
   business-advice accounts (ServiceTitan lists, 2025 to 2026), and humor goes with more likes (9,654 brand TikToks,
   arXiv Jun 2026). Our words and cast; no real customer.
2. **A line worth sending:** the last line names a pain owners share in words an owner would forward to a partner or a
   buddy. Why: sends count most for people who don't follow us (Mosseri, Jan 2025); a niche frustration put into words
   drives shares (Socialinsider, May 2026).
3. **Context, lean, turn by 5 s:** the topic is clear in the first line, a stake pulls the viewer in, and the wrong first
   guess is said and flipped by second 5 (Kallaway, Dec 2024; no data behind it beyond his own views).
4. **Proof from our own data** (after the AI lane test ends Nov 8): a lesson built on what only we hold (Chicago permits,
   inspections, 311): "we read every X; here's the number nobody tells you" (IDEAS.md row 22 is one).
