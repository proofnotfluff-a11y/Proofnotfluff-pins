# Finding the formula (from Nov 1, 2026)

Todd, live chat, Nov 1, 2026, 12:09 pm: "Let's go down to 2 videos a day until we get the formula and structure correct
to go viral. use the spare capacity to focus on developing quality videos".

Where we start (`anim.py lanes`, Oct 28 to Nov 1): 8 lessons, 2,662 views, 0 shares, 0 follows, 0 comments, 3 saves.
Facebook keeps 12 of 100 viewers past 3 s; TikTok average watch 8.9 s. Our best: lsa-missed-calls on Instagram, 29% skip
and 18 s average watch. Viral means shared: the number that matters is shares + saves + follows per 1,000 views, and
before it, how many stay past 3 s.

## One change a lesson
- brief.md names the one thing this lesson tries that the last ones did not: `- **Test:** <what> (<why>)`. One only, so
  the result can be read: the first frame, the hook line's pattern, the length, the save card, a share line, the lane.
- research/FORMULA.md gets a row per posted lesson at 24 h and 72 h: lane, test, 3 s hold (Facebook 3 s views per view),
  TikTok average watch and watched-full %, Instagram skip rate when shown, shares + saves + follows per 1,000 views.
- A test that beats the median of the 6 lessons before it on both hold and shares + saves + follows goes into
  `_shared/standard.md` as a line with its numbers; one that loses is written down and dropped. Two wins in a row on the
  same change make it the default.

## A run with 2 already waiting (no new lesson)
In this order, one run's worth, each step logged:
1. **Study.** Find 2 public Shorts or Reels for trade owners or AI for small business that drew far more shares or saves
   than their account's usual (the counts on the page). Ask the watch-video skill what the first 3 s show and say, the
   beats, the save moment, the length. Write the pattern in our words in research/FORMULA.md "References". Never their
   scenes, lines or look.
2. **Sharpen the next lesson in line.** Fresh eyes on its first frame, hook, C3 save card and end card against this
   file and the references. Change it only if the change is clearly better; re-run the gates it touches. New voice only
   when the script gate finds a real problem (credits).
3. **Improve the kit.** IMPROVE.md, "The bar first" items before anything else, proven on stills.

## Weekly (Monday research step)

once a lesson in each of two weeks beats the start numbers above by 3x on shares + saves + follows per 1,000 views.
