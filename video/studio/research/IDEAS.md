## Ranked ideas (run 2026-10-28-formats)

Ranking logic: (a) a real, recurring pain from section 3; (b) a copyable artifact we can show on screen (text, email, quote line), which drives saves; (c) an obvious person to send it to (partner, spouse, office manager, crew lead), which drives sends; (d) fits every trade, so one video serves the whole audience.

Scripts in every video must be our own wording. Stats used on screen must carry the source in the caption.
