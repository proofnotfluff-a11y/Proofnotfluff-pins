## Email outreach and deliverability (run 2026-10-28-email)
- Reply-rate averages are vendor numbers and disagree (0.74% Smartlead, 3.43% Instantly, 4.5% Hunter): never quote one as "the industry average".
- First emails of about 50 to 100 words are safe; two personal details beat none (Hunter 2026, vendor).
- Cold email: ask about interest, not a set meeting; on a live lead, offer a specific time (Gong, 304,174 emails, vendor).
- Follow-ups: each adds fewer replies; three messages total did better than one, more than three did worse (Hunter, vendor).
- Google and Yahoo bulk senders (about 5,000 a day to personal Gmail): SPF, DKIM and DMARC aligned, one-click unsubscribe, spam rate under 0.3% (Google advises under 0.1%); since November 2025 failing mail can be rejected (official pages, HIGH).
- Microsoft: 5,000+ senders need SPF, DKIM, DMARC; failing mail rejected with 550 5.7.515. Say "since May 2025"; the exact date is unverified.
- CAN-SPAM covers business email too: postal address, a working opt-out honored within 10 business days, honest subject lines (FTC, HIGH). Whether it covers a follow-up on a requested quote is a legal call: any video on it says it is not legal advice.
- GC prequalification asks for licensing, insurance and bonding, safety record, experience and references, key people and financials (ConsensusDocs 721); property managers want to be named additional insured, not just a certificate (National Apartment Association).
- No data supports "breakup emails work best": don't say it.
