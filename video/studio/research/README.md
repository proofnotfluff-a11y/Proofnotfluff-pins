## Rules
- Primary sources first: the original study, the platform's own page, the regulator. A famous number that can't be traced
  to its original is marked low confidence and is never said as fact.
- Our own words. No quote over 15 words, one per source. No private person's name or words.
- A claim older than 12 months, or about a rule or a platform, is re-checked before it goes in a new script.
- Never gimmicky: a lesson states what to do, why, and how sure we are. When the evidence is thin, the script says "in our experience" only if it is ours, and otherwise says less.
