## Assumptions in our method, checked
| # | Assumption | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | More design rounds make a lesson travel further | r = 0.10 between design score and views; all passed the bar | Not supported. The gates stop embarrassing posts; they do not create reach. Rounds past the bar are cost, not upside. |
| 2 | The topic is chosen well when a source proves it | Our top 4 are one topic cluster (phone); the bottom are sales technique. The 100K rows cluster on money between two people | Supported in part: topic is the strongest signal we have. The proven lane already picks topics; it does not yet weigh our own winners. |
| 3 | The hook is the first spoken sentence | Sources state the conflict in the first frame's text before a word; ours finish the spoken hook at 6.4 s (pay-before-you-leave, a NOTE since Nov 5) and most viewers leave by 3 to 8 s on TikTok | Weak where it counts. The cover and the first second carry the hook; the voice confirms it. |
| 4 | A different hook label means a different opening | 4 of the 7 openings Nov 6 to 8 were the same clock moment under four labels | False. Fixed today in code (hook moves). |
| 5 | Our narrator host carries the lesson | Sources show a conflict between two people, or a person talking straight to camera; we show Snow explaining | Untested. We have never made a two-character conflict lesson. |
| 6 | 50 to 65 s is the right length | Sources run 20 to 111 s; our length has no link to views (r = -0.04) | Length is not the lever; retention in the first seconds is. The 70 s cap stays (Todd's rule). |
| 7 | A forwardable card makes people send | Shares are 0 to 1 everywhere, including the lessons with the send card (from Nov 7) | Too early to say: the send rule is one day old. |
| 8 | Quiz questions hook owners | Our 4 question openers ("Do you know your close rate?") are all bottom half | Not supported for quiz questions. A judge-this question about a scene is untested. |
| 9 | Varied worlds avoid mass-produced flags | No flag seen; no test | Unknown; the variety rules stay. |

## Rebuilt from what holds
1. Pick the topic for pull first: money between two people, or the phone cluster that already works for us.
2. Put the conflict on frame 0 in 5 to 8 words, and land the stake in the first 3 s.
3. Rotate the opening move in code, not by label.
4. Spend fewer rounds getting to the bar: layout faults caught in code before render, kit pieces that cannot fail.
5. Test the biggest untested gap (a two-person conflict in our animation) once, fairly, instead of polishing more.
