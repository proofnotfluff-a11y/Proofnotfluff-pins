## Baseline (19 old-format lessons, research/metrics, Nov 9)
- Best single-platform views: 1,299. Five posts between 990 and 1,299; every other post under 450 on every platform.
- Medians: Instagram 122, TikTok 180, YouTube 33, Facebook 19.
- TikTok average watch 21 to 26% of runtime on the newest lessons, watched in full 0 to 6%. Best Instagram: skip 26.0%, 1 send per 1,122 reached. 1 share and 0 follows across 80 posts.

## Levels (per post, the best single platform)
| Level | Bar |
|---|---|
| Viral | 100,000 views on one platform within 7 days of posting |
| Breakout | 10,000 views on one platform within 72 hours |
| Lead | over 1,300 views on one platform within 72 hours (beats every post so far) |
| Quality, at 24 hours, only where the platform shows 200 views or more | TikTok: average watch 50% of runtime or more and watched in full 20% or more. Instagram: skip rate 30% or lower and sends per reach 0.5% or more. YouTube: viewed vs swiped away 70% or more |

The 0.5% send bar is an assumption (no platform publishes one); it is about 5 times our best.

## READ 1, each format
- PASS: 2 of its posts reach Lead or better, or its median TikTok average watch is 50% or more (at least 3 posts read).
- KILL: at least 4 posts read, none above 500 views on any platform, and median TikTok average watch under 35%.
- HOLD: anything else; one a day in week 2.
- No format passes: week 2 runs the best format by median TikTok average watch, and READ 2 decides.

## Week 2, each variable
A difference counts only if every post in one arm beats every post in the other on the same platform, on views or on a quality bar. Anything less is "no difference shown".

## Control
The old-format lessons posted in days 1 to 5 set the baseline for skip, watch and sends on the same days. A v4 format whose median TikTok average watch does not beat theirs has not fixed the middle.

## Scale and stop
- Breakout: 3 more in that format on new stories over the next 3 days, nothing else changed. 2 of 3 pass 5,000: that format takes 2 of the 3 daily slots.
- Viral: tell Todd first; the link line goes in the pinned comment; the next 5 in that format keep everything else the same.
- Nothing reaches Lead by day 14: stop daily posting, keep 3 posts a week of the best format, and say so plainly to Todd with the numbers.

## Money
One tagged link per platform in the bio and the pinned comment, to the missed call cost calculator. Read on day 14: views, profile visits, link taps, calculator completions. Assumed bars, no baseline exists: 50 tagged visits and 5 completions. Under 10 visits: the views are not turning into owners, whatever the count.

## What this cannot show
With 2 to 5 posts an arm, only large differences. Which part of v4 moved a number (the story, the length, the frame, the captions): it is one bundle against the old format.
