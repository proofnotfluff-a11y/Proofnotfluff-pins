## Decision 1: build the send into the story
- **In plain words:** every lesson says, out loud in its last two seconds, who to send it to, in the story's own words ("Send this to the one who always gives the discount"). The story is picked with that person in mind, and the last frame shows the two sides as two people the viewer knows.
- **Why:** Instagram's head says sends per reach matter most for reaching people who do not follow you. All our viewers are that kind. Today the send line is only in the caption.
- **What changes after Nov 23:** a `Send:` line in the v4 brief template; the v4 format's last line and last frame; the viewer gate asks the stranger who they would send it to.
- **Cost:** no build, no spend. Two seconds of voice.
- **The bar, set now:** over the next 5 lessons, 6 sends or more per 3,000 people reached on Instagram is a pass. 1 or fewer is a kill.
- **Yes or no?**
