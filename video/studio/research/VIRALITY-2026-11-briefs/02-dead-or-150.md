# Brief: Dead, or $150? (v4 call)

Sample brief from research/VIRALITY-2026-11.md, written Nov 11, 2026. Not planned with Todd yet and not in the queue. Rules: `_shared/formats.md` "v4"; coo/constitution/video.md. It lives in research/ so no run picks it up by accident; the make run copies it to videos/<slug>/01_voice/brief.md once Todd says go, and reads the source again that run.

- **Format:** call
- **Where:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, then TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts.
- **Audience:** anyone who has been handed a big repair quote, and every tech who has had to decide between repair and replace.
- **The one idea:** One plumber looked at a water heater for two minutes and quoted $3,100 to replace it, a second fixed it for $150: show the customer what you tested before you say replace, or the second opinion says it for you.
- **Pull:** conflict yes (the homeowner and the first plumber, with the second plumber as the turn); dollars yes ($3,100 against $150); send yes (anyone waiting on a quote this week; inside a shop, the tech who quotes a new unit on every call). Breadth: a homeowner stops because it is their fear; a tech stops to argue that a 10-year-old tank is due anyway.
- **Shape:** the two prices on frame 0, the first visit, the second call acted out, the reason it is not simple, the question that splits the room.
- **Music:** night (the make run checks `anim.py tunes`).
- **Voice:** Todd's clone for the narrator; the call is two speakers shown as captions (`_shared/formats.md` "v4" call).
- **Source:** The Cool Down, "Plumber declared water heater 'dead' after 2 minutes; a second opinion led to a $150 fix", Nov 1, 2026. It relays the homeowner's own post in a homeowners forum. Nothing in it was checked by the site: not the diagnoses, not the quotes, not the first plumber's side. The voice says "the homeowner says". No person or business is named.
- **Acted scene:** the phone call to the second plumber is a reenactment in our own words. The post gives the facts, not the words. Say so once: the line "Reenactment" on the call's first frame, or in the voice.

## Beats (a guide for the script, not the script)
1. Frame 0, settled: "$3,100" against "$150", one water heater between them. Spoken hook, 7 words: "Same water heater. Two plumbers. Two prices."
2. By 3 s: the first plumber looks for about two minutes, calls it dead, quotes $3,100 for a new one.
3. The call (reenactment, said once): the homeowner rings a second plumber. Two speakers, short lines. He comes about two hours later.
4. `[payoff]` by 40%: he finds a small leak at the top, swaps a pipe in under an hour, $150. Hot water is back.
5. Held for 70% to 85% (level-up, move 3): the tank is 10 years old. Some say a tank that age is near the end of its life. So was the first quote a scam, or an honest call made too fast?
6. Close: "Upsell, or fair warning? You decide." Last frame: both sides, each with its case ("2 MINUTES, $3,100" and "10 YEARS OLD").

## Staging notes (creative direction for cues and scene; no rule changed; from the read of the first v4 lesson's sheet, Nov 11)
- Show the fight happening, not labels over two people standing still: the two face each other; something changes hands or is pushed back (the invoice, the estimate, the phone); each reacts on the beat (arms crossed, a step back, turning to leave).
- One close shot on a face at the turn, and a second place or angle before the end. Faces must read at phone size: the refusal and the relief should not look the same.
- A plate carries the number or the two or three words the picture needs. It never repeats the caption under it, and the caption does not cover a face.
- Numbers move: the price is struck and the new one lands as it is said.
- The last frame shows the two people, one a side. Never the same person twice.
- One short line may be acted for each person, in our own words, with "Reenactment" on its first frame (formats.md "v4": an acted scene says so once). Say whose account it is once in the voice near the top and once in the caption, not on every sentence, as far as the facts gate allows.
