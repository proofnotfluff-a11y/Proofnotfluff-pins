## What the excerpts say (to confirm on the real pages or Todd's own screen)
- Businesses in ChatGPT opened in beta on Nov 10, 2026. Business Hub is where a business claims its profile, keeps its details current and sees how people engage with it.
- Claim: sign in to ChatGPT, open Business Hub from the sidebar, search the business by name, pick the listing.
- Verify the claim by one of: the business phone, a business email, the website domain, or Shopify. Then an identity check with Persona before any edit goes live; a blue check shows once that is approved.
- After approval: edit the business details, see Insights.
- The catches: it is a beta; a local store or restaurant can only claim a listing that already exists, United States only; claiming is not said to move a business up in ChatGPT's answers.
- Not for us: Business Agents (US online stores and online services only).
