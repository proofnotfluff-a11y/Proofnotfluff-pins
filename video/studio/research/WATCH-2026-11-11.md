## Two ways they are made (neither is ours)
- **AI-made lifelike video with acted people (4 of 8).** Both sides get spoken lines, lip-synced onto generated characters; the picture cuts between the two people, then papers, surveys, inspectors, a judge.
- **One narrator over unrelated "satisfying" clips (4 of 8).** A first-person post read aloud while cleaning, cooking and factory clips play. The two sides are never shown.
- None is drawn or 2D animation. No winner looks like our pixel world.

## What we cannot take, and why
- **Unsourced stories.** No winner names a source, and the same story appears on two channels. Our honesty rule needs a source read that run. We tell real, sourced disputes or none.
- **Lifelike AI people.** A realistic person who does not exist, speaking, is the kind of thing our "nothing deceptive" line and the platforms' AI labels are about. Our cast is plainly drawn, which keeps us clear of it.
- **Other people's clips as the picture.** The platforms now cut reach for re-used footage (research/VIRALITY-2026-11.md, Drag). Our pictures stay our own.
