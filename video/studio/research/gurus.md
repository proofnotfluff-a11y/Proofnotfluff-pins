# Gurus: who teaches trade owners on video, and which of their videos beat their own normal (first pass, Nov 5, 2026)

Desk 1 (.claude/skills/trade-pulse/SKILL.md) keeps this current. Method (adapted from Volume's library, Research Wiki,
Nov 5): judge a video by its **outlier ratio** = its views divided by that channel's typical views (median of its 48
newest uploads), never raw views; a pattern is **proven** only when 3 or more creators win with it. Views are lifetime
totals read Nov 5, 2026 from vidIQ's channel pages (vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@handle/videos/), which list the 48
newest uploads with views and length; YouTube, TikTok and Instagram pages show no counts to our reader. Older uploads have
had longer to collect views, and most channels mix Shorts and long form in one median: ratios are a guide, not a law.
The openings below come from titles, descriptions and chapters unless a watch note (video/watch/answers/) says otherwise.

## The honest read (what this first pass says about our plan)
1. **AI as the topic does not beat a channel's normal.** Tommy Mello's AI Short 0.4x, Housecall Pro's AI Shorts 0.4 to
   1.8x, TheNewFlatRate's AI long form 0.6x. The best AI video found is a screen demo that opens on the finished result
   (Housecall Pro with Claude, 3.4x). The small "AI for contractors" agency channels are too small to measure.
2. **The only proven breakout format is the relatable trade-life skit or meme with a "what would you do" or "tag
   someone" prompt** (3 creators: 11.8x to 95x). They teach nothing; they make an owner feel seen.
3. **Steady beats, not breakouts: a dollar figure or money myth in the title** (Contractor Fight, Million Dollar
   Plumber: 2.5x to 6.7x).
4. What it means for us: our story has to carry the feeling of the skit (an owner sees his own day) and the usefulness
   of the demo (a result he can copy). An AI topic alone won't pull; the owner's problem and the moment he recognizes do.

## Patterns
- **Proven (3+ creators):** the relatable trade-life skit or meme Short with a question or tag prompt (Roofing Insights,
  Roofing SEO Webmasters, TheNewFlatRate). Advice Shorts and podcast clips are the baseline, not the winners.
- **Near-proven (2 clear, 1 weak):** a dollar figure or money myth in the title (Contractor Fight, Million Dollar Plumber;
  Tommy Mello weak).
- **Unproven (1 creator):** the warm job story with a rule broken (Keith), the result-first screen demo (Housecall Pro),
  the test-the-viewer question (Roofing Insights), the contrarian AI take (Sweaty Startup, 2.7x).
