## Verdict
Adapted. Their pipeline is the one we already run (deterministic render(t), whisper check, ducking, -14 LUFS). The
value is craft: their pixel RPG style guide and director rules are a finished version of the look we are building.
Not installed: the plugin downloads several GB (sample packs, models) into the home folder, edge-tts sends text to
Microsoft, and our engine, voice (Todd's clone) and gates already exist. We keep one style, ours; the reel's own advice
says the same ("Pick one. Commit.").

## Craft we take (each now a line in _shared/standard.md or IMPROVE.md "The bar first")
- Pixel motion: sprites step at 8 to 12 fps, camera and parallax move every frame in whole pixels; UI windows slide in
  over 0.3 s with ease-out; a hand cursor bobs 1 px at 4 fps and stops when the host hesitates (IMPROVE 00a-2).
- Pixel transitions: mosaic cross, palette step fade in 4 levels, never an alpha fade; screen shake 3, 2, 1 px over 8
  frames on a hit (00a-4).
- Faces act in portraits: a 32x32 portrait with expression overlays in the dialog box; the sprite's face stays 1 pixel
  (00a-6).
- Native moves: a game moment only the medium can do, placed at the turn (ITEM GET, LEVEL UP, a stat screen, a save
  file); standard.md M7. ITEM GET is our save card (C3).
- Read time: text holds letters / 15 + 1.5 s after it lands; a subtitle at least 1.8 s and the line + 0.6 s (00a-5, M6).
- Rhythm: vary the pace, one acceleration and one held breath; one action, one sound, but not a cut on every beat.
- Sound: every visible action has a sound; two near-silences before the turn, the first sound after one of the biggest;
  sound as a transition at least once (J-cut or L-cut); text blips per speaker (00a-7).
- Camera: at least four different moves, one signature shot; at key moments the subject fills a third of the height.
- Review: frame strips at 0.2 s for every key action, besides the contact sheet.
- Failures they saw most, now design-gate checks for us: subject too small or at the edge, colour on the same colour,
  captions over the subject, a gag too fast to read, blank frames in transitions.
