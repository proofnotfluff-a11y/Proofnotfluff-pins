# Three a day: when to post, and what the platforms punish

Asked by Todd, live chat, Oct 30, 2026: "I want to switch to 3 posts per day on each platform ... Schedule the 3 video
releases each day based on when it will give us the best performance." Read Oct 30, 2026 (America/Chicago).

## Assumptions, checked
| Assumption | What is true | Source |
|---|---|---|
| More posts means more reach | Volume multiplies what a post does. Ours: 8 to 9 s average watch, 59.6% to 67.3% skip, 0 shares (Oct 29 insights). Three a day triples the tests we learn from; it does not fix the opening. | research/PERFORMANCE.md |
| Platforms punish volume | No page read says so. SocialPilot (4.4M posts, 45,700 accounts, updated Jun 1, 2026) found "no magical number" and recommends 7+ reels a week. | socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram |
| Platforms punish sameness | YouTube's inauthentic content policy (renamed Jul 15, 2025) names content that "looks like it's made with a template" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates"; the page frames it as monetization, not reach. A shared intro and outro is allowed when the body differs. | support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 |
| Timing matters a lot | Modestly, and not on TikTok. PostFast (61,008 posts scored against each account's own normal, Jun 6 to Sep 4, 2026): Instagram 3 PM local 1.20x; Facebook 3 PM 1.18x and 5 PM 1.17x; YouTube (mostly Shorts) 1 PM 1.25x and 9 PM 1.19x; TikTok no hour above 1.05x. Buffer (about 52M posts, Mar 25, 2026, local time): Instagram 9 AM and 6 PM weekdays; Facebook 8 AM to noon weekdays; TikTok 6 to 11 PM; YouTube Shorts 4 to 7 PM Fri and Sat. Both are vendor data from their own customers. | postfa.st/blog/best-time-to-post-social-media-data-report ; buffer.com/best-time-to-post |
| Our viewers keep office hours | Trade owners are on jobs mid-morning and mid-afternoon, check phones at lunch, at the end of the day and after dinner. Our audience spans US time zones; Central sits in the middle. | first principles, to be tested by PERFORMANCE.md |

## The three slots (Central; a run takes about 30 to 45 minutes to post all four)
| Slot | Starts | Posts land | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| lunch | 11:37 am | about 11:45 am to 12:20 pm (12:45 to 1:20 pm Eastern) | lunch break; YouTube's best hour (1 PM local) for the East and Central |
| end-of-day | 4:41 pm | about 4:45 to 5:20 pm (5:45 to 6:20 pm Eastern) | truck parked, invoices; Facebook 5 PM, YouTube Shorts 4 to 7 PM, Instagram 6 PM Eastern |
| evening | 8:13 pm | about 8:15 to 9:00 pm | couch time; TikTok 6 to 11 PM, YouTube 9 PM; 6:15 pm on the West Coast |

Minutes are off the hour on purpose (scheduled runs at :00 queue up). After two weeks the weekly rule in PERFORMANCE.md
ranks the slots on our own 72 h numbers and moves the weakest one.
