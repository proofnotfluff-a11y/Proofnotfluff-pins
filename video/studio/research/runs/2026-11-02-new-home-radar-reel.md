## What makes it work (for us to test, in our own scenes)
1. Zoom ladder: whole area, metro, one record, the thing the buyer gets, back to the whole area. The last frame matches the first.
2. One real record's life story is the spine (filed, inspected, finished). Proof without a testimonial.
3. Every noun spoken gets its own visual at that moment (a chip per trade). Our accents lint already enforces this.
4. The payoff object on screen: the exact letter or card the buyer would send, filled in for that one record.
5. A control moment: the viewer sees they could type their own rule.
6. "Same data, other trades" near the end widens who a viewer sends it to (our send gate).
7. Every big number rolls as a counter and carries its date window and source line.
8. The product's name shows as a small chip, never a logo card.

## What we never take

- Homeowner names on screen. Any record shown is a business record or is masked.
