# Homeowners would ask ChatGPT for a contractor almost as often as Yelp
- Score: new 2, consequential 1, surprising 2, documented 2, actionable 2 (total 9)
- Lede: Homeowners would ask ChatGPT to find a contractor almost as often as Yelp.
- Nut: if the AI gets your hours, area or services wrong, you lose the call and never hear about it; half of contractors in the same survey use no AI at all.

## Receipts
1. Survey by myCLEARopinion Insights Hub for BNP Media (ACHR News' parent): 782 homeowners and 205 contractors, fielded Apr 24 to May 19, 2026. Where homeowners would research contractors: Google Business 54%, contractor websites 45%, Yelp 38%, AI tools such as ChatGPT 37%, Facebook 35%. 50% of contractors do not use AI. 36% of homeowners name loss of human judgment as their top concern; 24% are comfortable with none of the listed AI uses. | https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166872-36-of-homeowners-draw-the-line-at-ai-replacing-hvac-expertise | ACHR News, Oct 20, 2026 | opened Nov 2, 2026

## The other side
- The question asks where homeowners would look, not where they did. A reputation-marketing firm's guest column in ACHR (Mar 18, 2026), citing SearchLight data, says ChatGPT referrals bring about 1% of revenue for most contractors against 67% from Google Business Profiles, but those ChatGPT leads average a $11,500 ticket against $3,441. One firm's data, method not published: said as theirs. | https://www.achrnews.com/articles/165972-before-you-chase-ai-leads-fix-your-google-business-profile | opened Nov 2, 2026

## What it means for a shop
Small share today, big tickets, and the AI answers from the same business data Google holds. The fix is the profile the owner already has.

## Do this week (the setup card)
1. Open a private window. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode: "best <your trade> in <your town>".
2. Write down: are you named, and what does it get wrong (hours, area, services)?
3. Fix those facts in your Google Business Profile and on your website's contact page.
4. Ask again in 2 weeks and compare.
