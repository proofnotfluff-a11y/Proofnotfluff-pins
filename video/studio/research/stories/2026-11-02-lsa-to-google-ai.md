# Google is moving Local Services Ads into its AI ad system, and your max bid goes away
- Score: new 2, consequential 2, surprising 1, documented 2, actionable 2 (total 9)
- Lede: Google is handing your Local Services Ads bidding to its AI, and the max cost per lead is gone.
- Nut: service-area shops (most trades) move in late 2026 on 14 days' notice, and their LSA performance reports do not come along.

## Receipts
1. LSA moves into Google Ads as a Performance Max campaign with pay-per-lead goals. Phases: August 2026 select US home and storefront service advertisers (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, pest control as examples); late 2026 service-area businesses without storefronts and accounts with custom bidding or booking; 2027 non-US and the rest. LSA performance reports (past impressions, clicks, weekly spend, ad-level reports) do not transfer; downloads come "soon" with no date; screenshots before migrating are the only option now. Manual bidding, including a max cost per lead, is no longer supported. Vertical-level Target CPA is replaced by one campaign-level Target CPA across verticals. Weekly budget / 7 = daily average; monthly spend capped at daily average x 30.4. BBB callouts no longer supported. Admin email 14 days before, a reminder 7 days later, one after. | https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17213585 | Google Ads Help, no date on page | opened Nov 2, 2026

## The other side
- Google says you still pay only for valid leads, ads show in the same Search and Maps positions, lead history (contact details, messages, older call recordings) moves to the Leads page, and a separate campaign per trade keeps separate rules. | receipt 1

## What it means for a shop
The owner stops setting the ceiling on what one lead costs; Google's AI bids toward a target instead. A shop running several trades in one campaign gets one target for all of them unless it splits campaigns. The history it would judge the new system against disappears unless saved first.

## Do this week (the setup card)
1. Screenshot 12 months of LSA reports (spend, leads, cost per lead) per trade.
2. Write down your max cost per lead and weekly budget for each trade.
3. Make sure the account's admin email is one you read (the 14-day notice goes there).
4. Different targets per trade? Plan one campaign per trade.
5. After the move: check your monthly cap equals weekly budget / 7 x 30.4.
