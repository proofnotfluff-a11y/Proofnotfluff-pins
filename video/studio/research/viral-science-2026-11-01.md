# Why our lessons don't spread, and what to change (Nov 1, 2026)

Asked by Todd, live chat, Nov 1, 2026: how to make the engine an expert at viral short-form, learn from current viral
videos, raise motion graphics, and find where PNF can get ahead. Research and a proposal only: nothing in `_shared/`,
the gates or the posting tasks changes until Todd picks moves. Builds on viral-formats-2026-10.md, _shared/standard.md
and _shared/formula.md; does not repeat them.

## Our numbers (from formula.md and PERFORMANCE.md, not re-measured)
- 8 lessons Oct 28 to Nov 1: 2,662 views, 0 shares, 0 follows, 0 comments, 3 saves.
- Holding attention already improved: lsa-missed-calls 29.1% IG skip, 18 s average watch; markup-margin 56% watched on
  TikTok. Lesson 5 was 59.6% skip.
- So the hook and the polish are no longer the main gap. Nobody sends a lesson to anyone.

## First principles
Assumptions we have been working under, and what holds:
1. "A better hook will make it go viral." Partly false now: skip fell from about 60% to 29 to 49% and shares stayed 0.
2. "More motion polish will make it go viral." False on our data: design gate 4.7 to 4.9 on every lesson, 0 shares.
3. "Posting more finds the winner." Each lesson gets only the small test audience (about 100 to 480 views) because
   it fails the share and follow signals that unlock the next audience. More of the same buys more test pools.
4. "Good advice is substance." Only if it is new to the viewer. Text missed calls back, reply fast, raise prices: every
   owner has heard it. Substance is information the viewer could not get elsewhere.
5. "A faceless brand can build trust." Only through proof. The brand name is the promise; most lessons carry no proof
   only we have.

Fundamental truths, each with its source:
- A send is a social act: the sender gains something (looks smart, helps a friend, gets a laugh, says "this is us").
  Sharing rises with practical value, surprise and high-arousal feelings (awe, anger, anxiety, amusement) and falls with
  low-arousal ones (Berger and Milkman, Journal of Marketing Research, 2012; 6,956 NYT articles, so text not video).
  Our lessons are calm and useful: practical value only, low arousal, no surprise.
- On Instagram the top three signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach, and sends weigh more for
  people who don't follow you (Mosseri, Jan 2025, via Social Media Today). With 4 followers, all our reach is people who
  don't follow us, so sends are the gate.
- Platforms now reward original work and cut reach for repeats: Instagram stops recommending accounts that repost
  unoriginal content (Apr 2024 for Reels, platform-wide Apr 2026); YouTube demonetizes "mass-produced and repetitious"
  content (Jul 15, 2025). Code-made lessons are original, but a template look posted twice a day reads as repetition.
  Our own data is original by construction.
- Retention benchmark: the median Short holds about 45% average view-through, the top 10% about 91% (quso.ai, 508K Shorts,
  vendor data, Jun 2026). Our best lesson holds 36 to 56%: middle of the pack, not the problem.

## Ranked moves
1. **Design for the send.** Every brief names who sends it, to whom, and why (helps a friend, looks smart, a laugh,
   "this is us"), and which feeling it carries (surprise, anger at a shared enemy, amusement, relief). A fresh reviewer
   plays an owner and must name the person they would send it to; no name, the lesson goes back. Fits PNF: costs no
   credits, one gate prompt. Test: the next 2 core lessons, read shares + saves + follows per 1,000 at 72 h.
2. **A proof lane from our own data.** Lessons built on what only we hold (Chicago permits, inspections, 311, the GC
   pipeline): "we read every X last month, here is the number nobody tells you." New information, surprise, local
   bragging rights, original by construction, and it points at products we sell. Idea bank already has some (IDEAS.md
   row 22). Test: one core lesson after the AI lane test ends Nov 8, so that test stays clean.
3. **Fill the reference library on purpose.** FORMULA.md "References" is still empty. Pick outliers only (views 10x or
   more of the channel's own median, in business, trades or animated explainers), then break each down with the
   watch-video skill (Gemini reads public YouTube links, about 1 frame a second plus audio, free preview, 8 h a day)
   on one fixed sheet: context by 1 s, the lean, the turn, picture changes per 3 s, when the payoff lands, the save
   moment, the send reason, the feeling. Our words only, no downloads (yt-dlp stays skipped). After 20, count which
   items show up in outliers and not in the channel's normal posts. Cost: spare runs only.
4. **Hook: context, lean, turn in the first 5 s.** Kallaway's method (context lean, scroll-stop interjection,
   contrarian snapback; video Dec 19, 2024). No data behind it beyond his own views; "scientifically impossible to
   skip" and "we have data" are not backed anywhere we could read. The structure matches our own result (money stake on
   the cover cut skip to 29%). The new part for us is moving the wrong-first-guess turn into seconds 3 to 5. One Test
   line, no credits.
5. **Motion: borrow, don't install.** Remotion's official agent skills (remotion-dev, 13 skills) checked: CAUTION.
   Our studio draws in its own Chromium runtime, not Remotion, so installing them adds nothing. Three craft ideas worth
   building in the kit through the lab: a hand-drawn circle or underline that draws onto the key number as it is said;
   zooms eased on a perceived-size curve so a punch-in doesn't feel slow at the end; stepped motion (every 2nd or 3rd
   frame) on the cast to fit the pixel look.
