# Brief: The first reply wins the job (if it sounds like you)

Planned in the golden render chat, Oct 28, 2026 (Todd, 12:05 pm and 12:10 pm: instructional, trusted, not gimmicky).

- **Where:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, 45 to 60 s.
- **Audience:** a trades or home-service owner (plumber, electrician, HVAC, cleaner) who gets quote requests by text, form or app and answers them when the day lets up.
- **The one idea:** answer a new lead fast, and make the answer sound like a person: their name, their question answered, one question back, a time.
- **Shape:** hook (a new lead lands, the clock starts) -> the study (an hour late roughly halves your odds) -> Thumbtack's own number -> the catch (fast but robotic loses) -> the four-part reply, shown on a phone -> the rule.
- **Metaphor:** none. The phone and the clock carry it.
- **Voice:** the library voice in `_shared/voice.md`.
- **Real screens:** a lead's text on a phone; a canned auto-reply; the four-part reply typed out; the study's name and journal on a source card.
- **Close:** "Fast. Human. One question. A time." Follow for the quote follow-up lesson.
- **Trust notes:** the study is Fiverr, not the trades, and the script says so. "About half" for 46%. No "21x" or "5 minute" claims (research do-not-use list).
