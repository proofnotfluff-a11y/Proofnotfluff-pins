# Script: The first reply wins the job

[matter-of-fact] A homeowner just asked you for a quote. You're on a job, so you figure you'll text them back tonight. That wait costs more than most pros think.

[leaning in] A study out this year looked at eleven point six million requests on Fiverr, the freelance site. Reply an hour late, and your odds of getting hired drop by about half. Wait a day, and they drop about ninety percent.

[matter-of-fact] And it's not just freelancers. Thumbtack says pros who answer inside thirty minutes are twenty-five percent more likely to get hired than pros who take three hours.

[short pause] But here's the catch. In that same study, speed stopped helping when the reply looked automated. Fast only works if it sounds like a person.

[warmly] So keep it short. Use their name. Answer what they asked. Ask one question back. Then give them a real time, like Thursday between two and four.

[confident] Fast, human, one question, a time. That's the reply that gets you in the door.
