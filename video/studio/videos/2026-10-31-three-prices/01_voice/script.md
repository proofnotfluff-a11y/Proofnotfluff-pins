# Script: Three prices on every quote (Lesson 4, Quote Clinic; trade evidence only, facts re-read Oct 28; Oct 29 hook and length rebuild: 9 word hook, 75 s cap)

[leaning in] You quote one price? Top shops usually quote three. Here's how to build yours.

[matter-of-fact] ServiceTitan pulled a year of home jobs. The top ten percent offered good, better, best on fifty-seven percent of them. Everyone else? Forty-one. That's a habit you can copy.

[curious] Why three? Housecall Pro surveyed over eleven hundred homeowners. Sixty-eight percent go get more quotes when a price is higher than they expected. With three, there's a cheaper option ALREADY on your quote.

[warmly] Build your three like this. Good is the job done right, nothing extra. Better is the one you'd pick for your own house. Best adds upgrades, like a longer warranty or a faster start date.

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. Your three is a floor. A heating and cooling trade group surveyed over a thousand contractors. Those offering four or more options closed fifty-two percent of the time. One to three? Forty-two.

[calm] That survey lumps one to three together, so it can't tell you three beats one. Start with three.

[confident] Send this to whoever writes your quotes. One price is a yes or a no. Three prices? That's a choice.
