# Cues: Three prices on every quote (Lesson 4, Quote Clinic, arcade look)

Worlds (brief.md): the storefront counter (hook, close), the arcade high-score table (stakes), a street of 100 houses
(why, our own piece), a quote clipboard on a truck tailgate (card, our own piece), the slot machine (survey and the turn).
None of lesson 9's versus, calendar, flaps or boss; no stock room. Steady camera: no stamps, no trauma; the motion lives
in the sets (tags flipping, reels spinning, houses lighting, rows landing). Each top-level cue names its world in a
"World:" line. Times come from the words (scene.js reads cue names and spoken words, never typed seconds).

music: rooms

## hook "One price on your quotes?" [sfx: pop]
World: the storefront counter. Frame 0: the cover, fully settled. Shop sign QUOTE CLINIC; a toolbox on the counter with one price tag ("$"); a cream card hung on the counter front: "Top shops quote" / "3 prices" (big) / "on 57% of home jobs." with the 3 and the 57% in red; source small: ServiceTitan data, 12 months to Jan 2026. Host and dialog hidden through the hook. After frame 0: sparks at 0.4 s and every 0.6 s, the card swings a pixel on its strings, shines cross the lines from 1 s, the OPEN sign blinks.
## hook.three "usually quote three."
On "three" the tag narrows to its edge, shows its barcode back and turns to "$ $$ $$$", sways to rest with sparks.
## hook.build "Here's how you build" [sfx: pop]
The chalk board on the wall writes "3 prices." / "1 quote." line by line; the card's 3 pulses gold once.

"12 months to Jan 2026" types under it.

## why "Why give them a choice?" [sfx: whoosh]
World: a street of 100 houses at dusk (10 by 10), windows dark, a quote envelope flies in across the rooftops and lands on the first house.
## why.hcp "Housecall Pro surveyed over" [sfx: tick]
The street sign plate: "Housecall Pro survey, 2026" and "1,100+ homeowners" counting up.
## why.68 "sixty-eight percent said" [sfx: pop]
68 of the 100 houses light up in a fast run, row by row; "68%" slams on the plate as it is said.
## why.quotes "go get more quotes" [sfx: tick]
Over each lit house a small envelope pops up and floats off to the right: "get more quotes when the price is higher than expected".
## why.surprise "One price that surprises" [sfx: pop]
The street dims; one big quote slip drops in: a single price "$$$$" with a red "?!" over it.
## why.shop "send them shopping."

## why.three "With three, the cheaper" [sfx: pop]
A three-price quote slips in: $ / $$ / $$$; on "cheaper" the $ row glows teal with a check: "The cheaper option is already on your quote."

## card.better "Better is the one" [sfx: tick]

## card.warranty "a longer warranty or"
