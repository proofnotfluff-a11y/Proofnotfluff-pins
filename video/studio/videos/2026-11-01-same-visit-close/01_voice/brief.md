# Brief: Close it before you leave the driveway

Lesson 5, segment Quote Clinic. Planned Oct 28, 2026 from the topic bank's T2 (research/runs/2026-10-28-topic-bank.md, "Close it before you leave the driveway"), the source page re-read the same day.

- **Where:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, about 75 s, then TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts.
- **Audience:** a trades or home-service owner (plumber, HVAC, electrician, roofer) who quotes on site, or whose techs do, and then waits for a call back.
- **The one idea:** ask for the decision before you leave the visit. (9 words)
- **Shape:** hook (quote still open when you leave? top shops close 77% of estimates on the same visit) -> stakes (ServiceTitan: top 10% by revenue 77%, everyone else 62%) -> why (the 15 point gap: momentum; the longer they wait, the more time to call a competitor or talk themselves out of it; fewer follow-ups) -> the artifact (the three-line ask, held big) -> the turn (the last question brings the worry out while you're there; same-visit closes show how a tech handles objections, frames financing, asks for the sale) -> the caveat (a pattern, not proof) -> close (ask for it in the driveway; send line).
- **Metaphor:** the driveway. The truck in the drive with the quote in hand; the clock running once it pulls away.
- **Voice:** Todd's clone in `_shared/voice.md`.
- **Real screens:** the ServiceTitan numbers as two bars with the window and source; a clock between quote and decision with the competitor's phone ringing; the three-line ask as a card (marked example); the caveat on screen while it is said.
- **Close:** "Send this to whoever goes out on your estimates." Brand plate, send line.
- **Trust notes:** ServiceTitan's top performers are the 90th revenue percentile in their trade and market segment (said "top ten percent by revenue", shown "Top 10% by revenue"). The page says "close 77% of their estimates during the same visit"; we say and show "estimates" and "same visit", never "quotes they win" or "home jobs" (the page does not say the same-visit rate is residential only). The comparison group is "the rest" / "everyone else" (both on the page). Data: participating ServiceTitan customers, trailing 12 months through January 2026, no sample size; HVAC, plumbing, electrical, garage door, water treatment and other specialty trades. Vendor data and correlational (the page says so): the script says "a pattern, not proof" and never "this made them top". The 15 point gap is the page's own figure. The three-line ask is our advice (topic bank takeaway), shown as "example"; Thursday is an example day.
