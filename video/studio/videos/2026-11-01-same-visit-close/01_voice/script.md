# Script: Close it before you leave the driveway (Lesson 5, Quote Clinic; topic bank T2, facts re-read Oct 28)

[leaning in] Still no yes when you leave the driveway? Top shops close seventy-seven percent of their estimates on the same visit. Here's how to ask.

[matter-of-fact] ServiceTitan looked at a year of data from its customers, up to this January. The top ten percent by revenue closed seventy-seven percent on the same visit. Everyone else? Sixty-two.

[thoughtful] Why a fifteen point gap? ServiceTitan puts it down to momentum. The longer they wait, the more time they have to call a competitor, or talk themselves out of it. Close on the visit and you get fewer follow-ups.

[warmly] So ask before you walk out. Say this. Here are your options. If you want to go ahead today, I can get you on the schedule for Thursday. What questions do you have before you pick one?

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. That last question brings the worry out while you're still there to answer it. ServiceTitan says same-visit closes show how well your tech handles objections, frames financing and asks for the sale.

[calm] One honest note. This is a pattern, not proof. Top shops might close more for other reasons. Asking still costs you one sentence.

[confident] Don't drive off and wait for a yes. Ask for it in the driveway. Send this to whoever goes out on your estimates.
