# Script: Reply to every review (Lesson 7, Reviews Clinic; topic bank T3, facts re-read Oct 28)

[leaning in] Trade owners: hotels that started replying got twelve percent more reviews. Here are two replies you'll want to copy.

[matter-of-fact] That's from a study in Marketing Science, on tens of thousands of TripAdvisor hotel reviews. The hotels' ratings went up too, point one two stars on average. If you think that's small, star ratings barely move.

[curious] Why would a reply help? It shows you care what customers think. And when owners answer, unhappy customers think twice before posting a complaint they can't back up.

[warmly] So here's the reply for a good review. "Thanks, first name. Glad the job went well. We're here when you need us." Then sign it with your name. That's it.

[calm] A bad review gets a different reply. "Sorry we fell short on the issue. I want to make it right. Call me directly at your number." Name the real problem. Don't argue in public.

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. Once hotels replied, bad reviews got rarer, but longer. That's more detail you can act on. And to be honest, this was hotels, not trades. Treat the numbers as a guide.

[confident] Good or bad, answer every review in your own words. Send this to whoever runs your Google profile, so your next review doesn't go unanswered.
