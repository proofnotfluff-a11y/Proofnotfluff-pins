# Script: Answer your reviews; many contractors don't (Lesson 7, Reviews Clinic; trades-first bank lesson 8, facts re-read Oct 28)

[leaning in] In one HVAC survey, forty-three percent of contractors don't answer reviews. Here are two replies to copy.

[matter-of-fact] That's The ACHR News, with Clear Seas Research. They asked four hundred homeowners, and ninety-one percent said reviews are important when they pick an HVAC contractor. Fifty-six percent look on Google.

[curious] And the contractors? Forty-five percent answer reviews on third-party sites. Forty-three percent don't. Thirteen percent don't know.

[warmly] Here's the five-star reply. "Thanks for trusting us with your furnace. Glad it's running right. We're here when you need us."

[calm] And the one-star reply. "Sorry we were late. That's on us. I want to make it right. Call me directly at the shop."

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. Answer the bad ones too. Keep it short and calm, and take the details offline. Never argue in public.

[matter-of-fact] One honest note. The survey shows reviews matter to homeowners. It doesn't prove a reply wins the job. So block ten minutes every Monday, and answer every new one.

[confident] Plenty of contractors leave reviews hanging. Don't be one. Send this to whoever runs your Google profile, and answer your reviews.
