# Script: Know your close rate (Lesson 8, Numbers Clinic; topic bank topic 4, facts re-read Oct 28)

[leaning in] Trade owners, median close rates run thirty-one to thirty-eight percent by region. That's ServiceTitan's data on its own customers, May through July. Do you know yours? I'll show you the math.

[matter-of-fact] Now take the top quarter of shops, ranked by close rate in each region. Their median runs seventy to seventy-three percent. [short pause] That's a big gap. And you can't close a gap you never measured.

[warmly] So here's your math. Screenshot this one. Close rate equals jobs sold, divided by estimates given. Count both over the same month.

[curious] Here's an example. Say you gave forty estimates last month and sold fourteen. Fourteen divided by forty is thirty-five percent. That's right inside the median range. Now plug in your own.

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. ServiceTitan doesn't say how it counts a close, and its customers aren't every shop out there. So the first number you need to beat is your OWN, from last month.

[matter-of-fact] Track it every Friday. Two numbers on the whiteboard: estimates given, jobs sold. Then run it in dollars too, sold over quoted. If the dollar rate's lower, your bigger quotes are the ones getting away.

[confident] Jobs sold, divided by estimates given. Send this to whoever writes your estimates. Then on Friday, you'll know your close rate.
