# Script: Know your close rate (Lesson 8, Numbers Clinic; topic bank topic 4, facts re-read Oct 28; hook and length rebuilt Oct 29)

[leaning in] Do you know your close rate? Here's the one-line math. Median close rates run thirty-one to thirty-eight percent by region. That's ServiceTitan's data on its own customers, May through July.

[matter-of-fact] Now take the top quarter of shops, ranked by close rate in each region. Their median runs seventy to seventy-three percent. [short pause] That's a big gap. And you can't close a gap you never measured.

[warmly] So here's how you measure it, and screenshot this. Close rate equals jobs sold, divided by estimates given, over the same month.

[curious] Here's an example where you gave forty estimates last month and sold fourteen. Fourteen over forty is thirty-five percent, right inside the median range, so plug in yours.

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. ServiceTitan doesn't say how it counts a close, and its customers aren't every shop out there. So the first number you need to beat is your OWN, from last month.

[matter-of-fact] Every Friday, put two numbers on the whiteboard, estimates given and jobs sold. That's all you need.

[confident] Jobs sold, divided by estimates given. Send this to whoever writes your estimates. Then on Friday, you'll know your close rate.
