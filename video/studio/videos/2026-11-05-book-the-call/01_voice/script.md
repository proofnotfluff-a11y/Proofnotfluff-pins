# Script: Book the call, don't just answer it (Lesson 9, Lead Clinic; trades-first bank lesson 1, facts re-read Oct 28)

[leaning in] Plumbers, top shops book sixty-two percent of lead calls. Everyone else? Thirty-nine. Here's the line that books yours.

[matter-of-fact] That's ServiceTitan's data on its customers, the year up to this January. Top shops are the top ten percent by revenue. If you're near thirty-nine, ServiceTitan says it's probably not your marketing. It's often the first minute after the phone rings.

[curious] What goes wrong in your first minute? Call tracker Invoca found fifty-five percent of home service businesses don't ask leads to buy or book the job.

[warmly] Here's what you say on every call. "Got it, water at the base of the heater. We can take care of that. I can get a tech there today at two, or tomorrow at nine. Which works better?"

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. Give two times, not "when works for you?" An open question is homework. Two times just need a pick. A ServiceTitan sample script does it too. Mornings or afternoons?

[calm] One honest note. That's ServiceTitan's customers, and a pattern, not proof. So track your own every Friday. Booked calls divided by lead calls.

[confident] Answering isn't booking. Send this to whoever answers your phone, and book the next call.
