# Cues: Book the call, don't just answer it (Lesson 9, Lead Clinic, arcade look)

Worlds (brief.md): versus screen (hook, close), switchboard (stakes, why), calendar lock (ask), boss fight (skip),
dispatch board (caveat). No stock room in any section; each top-level cue names its world in a "World:" line (not "Room:",
so the music stays on one room loop until the set-piece library gives each world its own bed).

music: rooms

## hook "Trade owners, top shops book" [sfx: pop]
World: the versus screen. Frame 0: the hook card, fully settled: "Top shops book" / "62%" / "of lead calls." The 62% in the accent. Source small: ServiceTitan customers, 12 months to Jan 2026. Behind it, the fighting-game select screen: TOP SHOPS on the left with a full score bar at 62, a big VS in the middle, the right slot dark. Host and dialog hidden. Sparks wait until 1 s.
## hook.top "percent of lead calls."
Sparks on the 62% and a ping on the TOP SHOPS score bar (the first motion after the settled cover).
## hook.vs "Everyone else? Thirty-nine." [sfx: tick]
The right slot lights: EVERYONE ELSE. Its score bar fills to 39 (count starts at 85% of 39), in grey; "39%" lands under it. The VS pulses once.
## hook.line "Here's how to ask" [sfx: pop]
Chip LEAD CLINIC / LESSON 9 drops in at the top. Under the VS: "The line that asks for the booking." Snow steps in small at the side and points at it.

## stakes "That's ServiceTitan's data on" [sfx: whoosh]
World: the switchboard. An old telephone switchboard slides up: rows of lamps, jacks and cords; a brass plate on top reads SERVICETITAN. Lamps start to light one by one, ringing.
## stakes.window "the year up to"
A brass strip under the plate: "Its customers, 12 months to Jan 2026".
## stakes.top "Top shops are the" [sfx: tick]
The left half of the board gets a tag "Top 10% by revenue"; an operator's hand plugs a cord into a jack marked BOOKED.
## stakes.near "If you're near thirty-nine," [sfx: blip]
A dial gauge on the board's side swings to 39, the 62 mark on its rim in the accent.
## stakes.marketing "probably not your marketing."
A card slides onto the board: "Near 39%? Probably not your marketing." Source line: ServiceTitan.
## stakes.minute "It's often the first" [sfx: tick]
A 60-second sweep clock pops above the ringing lamp and starts its sweep: "The first 60 seconds after the phone rings." (ServiceTitan's words, quoted).

## why "What goes wrong on" [sfx: whoosh]
World: the switchboard, close in. The operator answers a lamp; two speech bubbles trade questions and answers (no words, dots).
## why.invoca "Call tracker Invoca found" [sfx: tick]
The brass plate flips: INVOCA, Home Services Lead Conversion Benchmarks, Jul 2026.
## why.pct "fifty-five percent of home"
"55%" slams onto the plate in the accent (count starts at 85% of 55); "of home service businesses" under it.
## why.noask "don't ask leads to" [sfx: blip]
The line completes: "don't ask leads to buy or book the job." Snow points at it from the side, clear of the text.
## why.ends "or book the job." [sfx: pop]
On "job", the answered call's cord drops out of its jack with no booking; its lamp goes dark; a red tag swings on the empty jack: NO ASK. It holds to the end of the take.

Row 1 types: "Got it, water at the base of the heater."
## ask.l2 "We can take care" [sfx: tick]
Row 2 types: "We can take care of that."
## ask.l3 "I can get a" [sfx: tick]
Row 3 types: "I can get a tech there today at 2," and the TODAY 2 PM slot glows on the grid (tagged example).
## ask.tomorrow "or tomorrow at nine." [sfx: tick]
Row 3 goes on: "or tomorrow at 9." The TOMORROW 9 AM slot glows too (tagged example).
## ask.pick "Which works better?" [sfx: stamp]
Row 3 ends "Which works better?" One glowing slot clicks shut with a padlock and turns into a booked-job tile. The full card holds, unchanged, through the turn line below (3 s or more on screen complete).
## ask.skip "Here's the part people" [sfx: whoosh]
Only the header band changes: "The part people skip." The full card and the locked slot stay exactly where they are.

## ask.two "Give two times, not" [sfx: tick]
The header band flips again: "Two times. Not an open question." The two glowing slots pulse once; the card still holds.

## skip "when works for you" [sfx: whoosh]

## skip.pick "Two times just need" [sfx: tick]

## skip.st "A ServiceTitan sample script" [sfx: pop]

## skip.ko "Mornings or afternoons?" [sfx: stamp]
The buttons relabel MORNINGS / AFTERNOONS and fire; the boss's health bar drops to zero; KO flash: "BOOKED." Sparks.
