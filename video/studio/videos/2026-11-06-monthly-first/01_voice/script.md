# Script: Lead with the monthly payment (Lesson 10, Quote Clinic; trades-first bank lesson 2, facts re-read Oct 29)

[leaning in] HVAC owners, most of you say the big number first. Contractors who lead with the monthly payment get twice the share financed. Here's the line to use.

[matter-of-fact] That's from an ACCA survey of over a thousand HVAC contractors. Seventy-two percent lead with the overall price. The ones who lead with a monthly payment see forty-two percent of system sales financed. Lead with the total, and it's twenty-one.

[curious] And it's not only financing. The monthly shops sold fewer entry-level systems. Thirty-four percent of sales, instead of forty-two. Our take: your customer weighs a monthly number against bills they already pay.

[warmly] So put this on every replacement quote. "This system is X dollars a month, or Y paid in full. Which way works better for you?" Monthly line on top, the full price right under it.

[short pause] Here's the part people skip. Say it on every job, not just the big ones. In that survey, only thirty-seven percent offer it on every job. And don't ever hide the total.

[calm] One honest note. It's a survey, so it shows what those contractors saw. It doesn't prove the line did it. And only quote a payment plan you actually offer.

[confident] Monthly number first, the full price right under it. Send this to whoever writes your quotes. Don't lead with the big number.
