# Cues: Let me think about it (Lesson 5 in Lead Clinic v2, Quote Clinic, arcade)

music: rooms

## hook "Estimate's done." [sfx: pop]
Frame 0, the cover: the kitchen table from above, the estimate done on its clipboard (three options, the signature line empty), the coffee, the next-visit card blank. The title plate up top, settled: "The estimate's done." / "Let me think about it." No host, no dialog.
## hook.home "and they say," [sfx: whoosh]
Cut by 0.8 s: the street. The van parked in the driveway, the homeowner's door, a thinking bubble (dots) over it. The plate stays. Snow and the dialog box come in.
## hook.let "let me think" [sfx: blip]
Cut: close on the homeowner thinking it over. A big thought bubble whose dots step, an hourglass beside it that flips. The plate stays.

## play "You say, sure," [sfx: whoosh]
The street. Plate: "Sure, no rush." Snow waves.
## play.drive "and drive off."
The van rolls off the right edge; the bubble over the door turns to a red question mark. Plate: "And you drive off."

## wait "Then the waiting starts." [sfx: whoosh]
The dispatch board: the shop pin, the house pin, the route. Plate: "The waiting starts." An hourglass over the house flips once a second; follow-up trips pile on.
## wait.comp "They call a competitor," [sfx: tick]
A blue pin drops and a rival van routes to the same door. Strip: "Call a competitor."
## wait.talk "or talk themselves out" [sfx: blip]
The bubble over the house turns to a red X. Strip: "Or talk themselves out of it."

## fix "Try this before you" [sfx: whoosh]
The kitchen table again. The script card slides up: "TRY THIS BEFORE YOU LEAVE."
## fix.l1 "Here are your options." [sfx: tick]
Card head turns to "SAY THIS (EXAMPLE)"; row 1 types on as said; A, B, C land on the estimate.
## fix.l2 "If you want to" [sfx: tick]
Row 2 types on; the pen writes Thursday on the next-visit card, circles it, "example day".
## fix.l3 "What questions do you" [sfx: tick]
Row 3 types on; on "pick one" the pen ticks option B and signs. The card holds 3 s or more.

## proof.rest "Everyone else, sixty-two." [sfx: tick]
Bar 2 "Everyone else" fills to 62; the segments between blink as the gap.
## proof.why "It says the gap" [sfx: pop]
The gap row: "15 points" lands; on "momentum" the plate's second line turns to "The gap: momentum."

## button "That last question pulls" [sfx: whoosh]
The street again, the van stays parked. Board: "Ask before you leave."
## button.drive "while you're still in"
On "driveway" the bubble over the van turns to a green check, sparks.
## button.alone "So don't let them" [sfx: sting]
Board grows: "Don't let them think" / "about it alone." in the accent, an underline sweeps; Snow waves. Then the end card with the send line: "Send this to whoever runs your estimates."
