# Script: The after-hours quote request (AI lead lane, lesson 1; facts re-read Oct 29)

[curious] A move-out clean quote request came in at eleven last night. You see it at seven.

[matter-of-fact] So you start typing between jobs. Rates, the size, what's included. By lunch it's still a draft, and they've booked someone else.

[warmly] Instead, paste their message into an AI chat app with this. [short pause] Write a short, friendly reply to this cleaning quote request. Use my rates below. Ask one question if anything's missing. You read it, fix it, and send it yourself.

[matter-of-fact] In Jobber's survey, only twenty-six percent of cleaning businesses reply to a lead within an hour. And they're the fastest trade.

[confident] Nobody needs you up at eleven. [short pause] But the night quote should have your answer before your first job.
