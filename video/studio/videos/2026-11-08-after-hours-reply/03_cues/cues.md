# Cues: The after-hours quote request (AI lead lane, lesson 1, Lead Clinic, arcade)

music: rooms night

## hook "A move-out clean quote" [sfx: pop]
Frame 0, the cover: a dark bedroom at night, the moon in the window, the phone on the nightstand lit with a quote request. The title plate up top, settled: "Quote request, 11 pm." / "Seen at 7 am." Snow in his spot.
## hook.msg "request came in at" [sfx: whoosh]
Cut by 0.8 s: close on the phone at night, the customer's message (example) in a bubble, the screen glow. The plate stays.
## hook.seen "You see it at" [sfx: blip]
Cut: morning. The alarm clock rings at 7, the sun through the window, the phone face up. The plate stays.

## play "So you start typing" [sfx: whoosh]
The chat set: the customer's message at the top; the reply half typed in the box below, a cursor blinking. Plate: "Typing between jobs."
## play.rates "Rates, the size, what's" [sfx: tick]
Three sticky notes stack on the phone: RATES, SIZE, WHAT'S IN.
## play.lunch "By lunch it's still" [sfx: blip]
The clock in the phone's bar turns to LUNCH; the box still reads "Hi! So for a move-out we..."; "Still a draft." lands.
## play.booked "and they've booked someone" [sfx: tick]
A notification drops: "They booked someone else." Snow shrugs.

## fix "Instead, paste their message" [sfx: whoosh]
The AI chat app (an unbranded window, marked EXAMPLE). Their message pastes in as a grey block.
## fix.p1 "Write a short, friendly" [sfx: tick]
The prompt types on as said, line 1.
## fix.p2 "Use my rates below." [sfx: tick]
Line 2, with a [my rates] chip.
## fix.p3 "Ask one question if" [sfx: tick]
Line 3. The drafted reply slides in under it (example), with "[your rate]" in gold.
## fix.send "You read it, fix" [sfx: pop]
A pen ticks the draft; on "send it yourself" a SEND button presses, the reply goes. The prompt stays up 3 s or more.

## proof "In Jobber's survey, only" [sfx: whoosh]
The gauge. Plate: "Jobber" / "Cleaning businesses" / "1,050 owners, Dec 2025". The needle rises to 26 as it is said.
## proof.pct "twenty-six percent of cleaning" [sfx: tick]
The readout lands on 26%; label "Reply within an hour".
## proof.fast "And they're the fastest" [sfx: pop]
"Fastest trade." stamps in, gold.

## button "Nobody needs you up" [sfx: whoosh]
The departures board: TODAY. Row 1 flips to "11 PM ASLEEP".
## button.quote "But the night quote" [sfx: tick]
Row 2 flips to "QUOTE SENT" in gold. Board: "Answer the night quote".
## button.first "before your first job." [sfx: sting]
Row 3 flips to "FIRST JOB NEXT"; the board grows: "before your first job." An underline sweeps; Snow waves. Then the end card with the send line: "Send this to whoever answers your quote requests."
