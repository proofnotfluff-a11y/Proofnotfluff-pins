# Script: The AI receptionist myth (AI lead lane, lesson 2; facts re-read Oct 29)

[curious] The demo says an AI receptionist will answer every call for you.

[matter-of-fact] So you pay for the voice bot. The phone gets answered. But the quotes you already sent just sit there, and nobody follows up.

[leaning in] Fix follow-up first. Paste the quote into an AI chat app and ask. [short pause] Write a short check-in on this quote, in my voice. Ask what questions they have. Nothing goes out until you read it and hit send.

[matter-of-fact] ServiceTitan surveyed over a thousand contractors. Only eleven percent trust their AI tools highly. Fifty-eight percent, little or not at all.

[confident] So keep your hand on the send button. [short pause] Fix the follow-up before you buy an AI receptionist.
