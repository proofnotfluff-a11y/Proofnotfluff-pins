# Script: The missed-call text, part 1 of 3 (playbook; Depth bank #2; Twilio's page read Oct 31, 2026)

Your missed-call text gets a hundred sixty characters to book the job.

Part one of three, the words. Your gut says write a polite paragraph, like thanks for calling, we value your business. But there's no name and no question in it. Nobody answers that.

Three parts. Who you are. Sorry I missed your call. Then one question that books it, like what do you need fixed, and when works for you?

[matter-of-fact] Twilio's guide says one plain text holds a hundred sixty characters. Add one emoji and the limit drops to seventy. Curly quotes do it too. Go past that and Twilio splits it in two and bills both.

[confident] So count yours before part two turns it on. Keep it plain, and keep it under a hundred sixty.
