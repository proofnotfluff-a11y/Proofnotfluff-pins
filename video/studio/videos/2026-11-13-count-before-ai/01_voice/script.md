# Script: Do you need an AI receptionist? Part 1 of 2 (playbook; Depth bank #3; all numbers ours, EXAMPLE)

Fourteen missed calls. So do you need an AI receptionist?

Part one of two, the count. Your gut says the demo sounded great, so sign. But you don't know what those calls are worth yet.

For two weeks, mark every call you miss on a calendar. Then circle the ones that were real jobs. Say it's four of the fourteen.

[matter-of-fact] Four jobs times what you clear on an average job. Say three fifty. That's fourteen hundred bucks in two weeks. Call it twenty-eight hundred a month.

Now hold that next to the monthly price on the quote. Price is bigger? It can't pay for itself yet. Smaller? Part two tries three options side by side.

[confident] Count the missed calls before you buy what answers them.
