# Script: The price list prompt (AI lane, two-shops; Gemini run and plan page read Nov 1, 2026)

Gemini wouldn't price this stair railing. It's not on your list.

You'd think AI makes prices up. Not off your list. So who prices the railing?

Same walkthrough, two painters. Painter one types the quote at ten pm, from memory. The railing never makes it. He paints it for free.

Painter two keeps his price list in a phone note. In the driveway, he pastes the list, this prompt and his notes into Gemini.

[matter-of-fact] The prompt says use only his list. So anything new gets flagged. Four lines back. One flag.

Gemini's free. It won't price the railing. He does, and checks every line before it goes out.

[confident] Your list prices the job. You price the railing.
