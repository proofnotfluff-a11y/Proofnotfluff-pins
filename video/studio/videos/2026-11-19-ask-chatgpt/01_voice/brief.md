# Brief: Ask the robot who it would hire (story lesson, round 2)

Made Nov 6, 2026 in the 12:33 pm studio run from the Ready story "Homeowners would ask ChatGPT for a contractor almost as
often as Yelp" (research/stories/2026-11-02-chatgpt-finds-contractors.md), re-reported today (00_story/story.md).

- **Lane:** ai
- **Test:** open on two short scenes (a homeowner asking ChatGPT at 9 pm; an owner hearing "ChatGPT sent me" four times)
  before any claim, the Nov 6 round 2 opening (storycraft.md, Openings). Read against after-hours-robot (opened on a
  claim): 3 s hold and average watch.
- **Format:** story
- **Shape:** two-scenes
- **Story:** 00_story/story.md
- **Message:** Ask ChatGPT who it would hire in your town; if it isn't you, fix your Google profile first.
- **Where:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, then TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts.
- **Audience:** the owner of a small trade shop (HVAC, plumbing, roofing) whose phone got quieter and who has never asked
  an AI tool about their own business.
- **The one idea:** customers are starting to ask ChatGPT who to hire; it builds its answer from your Google profile,
  website and reviews, so check it and fix those.
- **Hook pattern:** stuck-moment
- **Treatment:** story
- **Example:** chatgpt-who-to-hire
- **Music:** sunny (free next to after-hours-robot, night; `anim.py tunes`)
- **Metaphor:** none
- **Voice:** Todd's own voice clone in `_shared/voice.md`.
- **Real screens:** the survey bars (AI tools 37% beside Yelp 38%) with the tag ACHR News, Oct 20, 2026, 782 homeowners;
  the forum line as a paraphrase card (Jobber Community, Nov 2026), no names; the column's line "interpret and
  reassemble structured business data" with its tag (ACHR News guest column, Mar 18, 2026, SearchLight data).
- **Do this week (on screen, held):** the check card: the exact question to type, what to write down, what to fix, ask
  again in two weeks. Held 4 s or more.
- **End card send line:** "Send this to whoever runs your Google profile."
- **Asked by owners:** Jobber Community thread 15769 (about Nov 3, 2026) and thread 10191 (Nov 6, 2026); pulse 2026-11-05
  problem 8, "the phone just got quieter".
- **Model videos:** the gurus.md compare set; Roofing SEO Webmasters (AI search) in gurus.md.
- **Diagnosis applied:** no read yet on after-hours-robot (posted 17:48 UTC today, under 2 h before this run); the round 2
  opening is applied from Todd's Nov 6 recommendation 1.

1. "Nine at night, her furnace quits. She doesn't call around. She asks ChatGPT." (moment, 13) PICKED: two scenes start
   here, the second is the owner; the claim waits until both are seen.
