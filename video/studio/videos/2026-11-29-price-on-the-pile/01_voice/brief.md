# Brief: Price on the pile (proven lesson, research/PROVEN.md row 11)

Made Nov 9, 2026 by the 9:33 am cloud studio run. Lane test says proven (`anim.py nextlane`, pick order rows 11, 14, 16;
row 11 first: money between two people). Our own version of research/PROVEN.md row 11, the customer who won't pay the
junk crew (Roofing Insights, 2,433,807 views against its channel's typical 8,356, 291x). Its opening move and structure
are not read (the Nov 9 study reads hit Gemini 503 and then the daily quota), so the pattern is taken from its row only:
a two-person money conflict, acted, after the work is done. Its lines, shots and numbers are not used. Different from
pay-before-you-leave (row 1, how to take payment when the job is done): this one is when the price gets said, before the
work starts. Today's pulse (research/pulse/2026-11-09.md, problem 1) has owners saying a price raised mid-job is heard as
padding.

- **Format:** proven
- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Rw_tRgRvZHM
- **Started:** 2026-11-09 11:05
- **Key ingredient:** an acted money fight between a crew and a homeowner after the work is done, which we draw with
  k.client arguing over a full trailer.
- **Viewer check:** a junk hauler watching ours sees the homeowner refuse four hundred once the trailer is full, then gets
  the one line to say at the pile before lifting anything, and leaves knowing to get a yes on the price first.
- **Test:** the pattern (a crew done, a homeowner who won't pay) pulled 2.4M views at 291x; ours acts the fight once
  (k.client, made-up, said once), hands over the line to say before the work (the send card, built line by line, held
  about 3 s, before half) and carries one checked number (The ACHR News with myCLEARopinion, Dec 23, 2024: 70% of
  homeowners surveyed more likely to call a contractor transparent on pricing). Aims at the low end of the band (about
  50 s) because the middle is the cause on every lesson with a watch read (research/PERFORMANCE.md, Nov 9 morning).
- **Lane:** core
- **Where:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, then TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts.
- **Audience:** the owner of a small junk removal or hauling crew who quotes at the door and loads the same visit.
- **The one idea:** say the price at the pile and get a yes before you lift anything, because a price after the work is a fight.
- **Shape:** pile: the moment (the trailer full, she says four hundred is too much and offers two, made-up), the
  dilemma said out loud (dump it back on her lawn?), the fix (the price said at the pile), the send card for the driver
  built line by line before half, why (a yes before is a deal, a price after is a fight), the receipt (70%), the
  last line asks: on the pile or on the trailer.
- **Hook pattern:** stuck-moment
- **Treatment:** before-after
- **Example:** achr-70-transparent-price-at-the-pile

  Online" (Dec 23, 2024; survey with myCLEARopinion), read Nov 9, 2026.
