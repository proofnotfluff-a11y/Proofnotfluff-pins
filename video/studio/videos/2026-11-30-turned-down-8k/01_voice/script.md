# Script: Turned down $8,000 (v4 verdict, r1, Nov 9; pre-voice facts r0: his own reason, attributed, and no run of the headline's words)

[conversational] A Florida roofer turned down an eight thousand dollar job. The client only wanted two hundred off.

He says the client was a flipper with two lower bids, who asked him to drop two hundred to stay competitive. He said no.

He says it wasn't the two hundred. The client had told him he only cares about price, not quality. To him, that meant a problem job. He says he felt relief.

Smart call, or pride that cost him the job? You decide.
