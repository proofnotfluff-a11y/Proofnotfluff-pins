# Cues: Where $100 of the bill goes (v4 invoice; one plumbing owner's own account and his own example budget, PHC Pros, Mar 1, 2022; Nov 11, 2026)

music: rooms bounce

## hook "She thought the plumber" [sfx: pop]
Frame 0, close and settled, a kitchen: the homeowner left, arms folded, facing the plumber right, who holds the bill on a
clipboard. Up top, big: $100 BILL in gold, under it in red: ALL HIS?. At 0.5 s she unfolds her arms and argues at him (the
first change). On "kept" a plain cut to her alone, close, still arguing, the same plate.

## hook.owner "One shop owner says" [sfx: blip]
A plain cut to the plumber, close, talking. The plate: HIS ACCOUNT:. On "held" a plain cut to the homeowner on the phone,
cross, and the plate lands again with a second row in red: JOB ON HOLD.

## pay "In his example," [payoff] [sfx: stamp]
A plain cut to the plumber, close, with the clipboard. The plate: HIS EXAMPLE: / 1-TRUCK SHOP. On "nine" it gives way to a
stamp: OF EVERY $100 / $9 LEFT, the last row big and gold.

## bill "Forty-one pays the tech" [sfx: blip]
The bill fills the frame over the dimmed kitchen: a cream invoice, one row landing as each is said: TECH $41, then on
"Sixteen" OFFICE $16, on "eight" TRUCK $8, on "Twenty" FEES $20, on "Six" INSURANCE $6.

## bill.home "And they work from home" [sfx: whoosh]
A plain cut to the plumber in the kitchen, close, talking. The plate: THEY WORK / FROM HOME.

## twist "Pay for a shop" [sfx: stamp]
A plain cut to the plumber, close, shrugging. The plate: $9 LEFT, struck through in red as he says "shop"; on "two" it gives
way to a stamp: WITH A SHOP: / UNDER $2, the last row big and red.

## ask "Fair, or still too much?" [sfx: blip]
The frame splits: the plumber on a slate left, the homeowner on wine right, arms folded. Left plate: PLUMBER: / FAIR, /
$2 LEFT. Right plate: CLIENT: then on "too" TOO / MUCH, / $100 BILL under it.

## ask.send "Send this to whoever" [sfx: blip]
The plumber points down at the viewer; the homeowner keeps her arms folded. Both plates hold. Ends 1 s after the last word.
