# Cues: Where $100 of the bill goes (v4 invoice; one plumbing owner's own account and his own example budget, PHC Pros, Mar 1, 2022; Nov 11, 2026; fix round 1 after viewer r0 and facts r0)

music: rooms bounce

## hook "She thought the plumber" [sfx: pop]
Frame 0, close and settled, a kitchen: the homeowner left, on the phone, cross, the plumber right with the clipboard (she
was at work and phoned). Up top, big: EVERY $100 in gold, under it in red: ALL HIS?. At 0.5 s a plain cut to her alone,
close, on the phone (the first change). On "kept" a plain cut to the plumber alone, close, shrugging, the same plate.

## hook.owner "One shop owner says" [sfx: blip]
A plain cut to the plumber, close, talking. The plate: HIS ACCOUNT. On "held" a plain cut to the homeowner on the phone,
cross, and a new plate lands: SHE PUT THE / JOB ON HOLD, the last row red.

## pay "In his example," [payoff] [sfx: stamp]
A plain cut to the plumber, close, with the clipboard. The plate: HIS EXAMPLE: / 1-TRUCK SHOP. On "nine" it gives way to a
stamp: OF EVERY $100 / $9 LEFT, the last row big and gold.

## bill "Forty-one pays the tech" [sfx: blip]
His example invoice on the left over the dimmed kitchen, the plumber close on the right holding the clipboard (she is not
in it): a cream card headed EXAMPLE $100 in red, one row landing under it as each is said: TECH $41, then on "Sixteen"
OFFICE PAY $16, on "eight" TRUCK $8, on "Twenty" OVERHEAD $20, on "Six" INS + HOME $6; then the plumber gestures at it and
a gold band steps down the five rows, one at a time, until the cut.

## bill.home "And that's with no" [sfx: whoosh]
A plain cut to the plumber in the kitchen, close, talking. The plate: NO RENT: / $9 LEFT, the last row big and gold.

## twist "Pay for a shop" [sfx: stamp]
A plain cut to the plumber, close. The plate: $9 LEFT, struck through in red as he says "shop"; on "two" he shrugs and it
gives way to a stamp: WITH A SHOP: / UNDER $2, the last row big and red, and the shot pushes in slowly on him to the cut.

## ask "Fair, or too much?" [sfx: blip]
The frame splits, one person a side: the plumber on a slate left, the homeowner on wine right, arms folded. Left plate:
PLUMBER, then FAIR? / UNDER $2 under it. Right plate: CLIENT, then on "too" TOO / MUCH? under it.

## ask.show "Owners, show this split" [sfx: stamp]
A plain cut back to the kitchen, both of them: the plumber left, one hand out to the customer right, who stands and
listens. The card lands: SHOW YOUR / NUMBERS / BEFORE THEY ASK, the middle row big and gold. Ends 1 s after
the last word.
