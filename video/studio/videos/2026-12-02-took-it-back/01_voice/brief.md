- **The one idea:** A contractor whose customer held back $4,225 smashed the shower he had just built and went to jail for it; small claims court would have heard the whole bill, so collect on paper, never with a hammer.

- **Shape:** the hammer on frame 0, the deal, the held payment, each side's line, the smash, what followed, the result, the legal route, the question.
- **Result (a case only):** he pleaded guilty to felony criminal mischief and was sentenced to 15 days in jail and 3 years of supervised probation (KRDO, Oct 13, 2022). Held to 60% or later.
