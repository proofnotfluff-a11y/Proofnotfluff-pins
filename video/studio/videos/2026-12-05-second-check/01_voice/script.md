# Script: Twenty minutes on a furnace, two checks, and a judge who said no (v4 told, Nov 18, 2026, fix round 2 on Todd's "One more round"; WRTV Investigates, three reports, Feb 2025 to Feb 2026)

[conversational] A couple says a contractor spent twenty minutes on their furnace and took almost eleven thousand dollars. Their system had started leaking, and a family friend recommended him.

They say he pulled the furnace apart, told them they needed a new system, and they wrote a check for seventy-two fifty. A day or so later, he said switching to an electric furnace would cost more, and he wouldn't leave until they wrote a second check, for thirty-six fifty.

The new system never came, and to me, that second check was the tell. They didn't know he had about six old convictions, and was out on bond in another county.

He got charged over their furnace, and took a deal: one year of probation. But the judge read his record and threw the deal out.

He pleaded guilty, and the judge gave him the most the law allows: two and a half years, and every dollar back.

But his sentence can't start until late twenty twenty-seven, when another one he's serving ends. He can do it at home on a monitor, paying them seven fifty a month, or face jail. Will they ever see the money? The wife says she'll pray about it.

As for him: was the max enough, or did he get off easy?
